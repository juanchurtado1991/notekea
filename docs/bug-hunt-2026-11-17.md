# Bug hunt, 2026-11-17

Asked by the developer after the loop's eighth run: are its failures the model's, or bugs in the code? Ten read-only
reviews, one per area, each finding with the input that triggers it. **Trace** means the finding was checked against a
failed task of the eighth run; the rest were read end to end and not run. Status: **fixed** (with a test), **open**.

Paths are under `app/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/notekea/`.

## Sync and the vault's view of the disk (`sync`, `core/notes/data`)
