Lisbon's yellow trams

By the Example team

Lisbon has run electric trams since 1901. The city's steep hills and narrow streets are the reason the small, old trams are still in use: newer and larger vehicles do not fit the curves of the old quarters.

The most famous line is number 28. It climbs from Martim Moniz through Graça and Alfama to Estrela, passing several viewpoints and the cathedral.

Most of the trams on line 28 are Remodelados, carriages from the 1930s rebuilt in the 1990s with new motors and brakes but with their wooden interiors kept.

Line 28 is crowded with visitors in summer. Riding early in the morning, or taking line 12, which makes a short loop around the castle hill, gives a quieter ride.
