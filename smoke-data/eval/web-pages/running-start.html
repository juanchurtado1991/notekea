Starting to run

By the Example team

Many new runners stop because they start too fast. The body adapts to running gradually: muscles in weeks, but tendons and bones in months.

A safe start is to alternate one minute of running with two minutes of walking for twenty minutes, three times a week, and to add a little more running each week.

Run at a pace where you can still speak in full sentences. Most of the training of experienced runners is at this easy pace too.

Increase the total time or distance by no more than about ten percent per week, and take a lighter week every three or four weeks. Pain that changes the way you run is a reason to stop and rest.
