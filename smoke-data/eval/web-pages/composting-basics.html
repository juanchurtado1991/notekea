Composting at home

By the Example team

Compost is made by microbes that break down kitchen and garden waste into a dark soil. They need four things: carbon, nitrogen, water and air.

Carbon comes from brown material such as dry leaves, cardboard and straw. Nitrogen comes from green material such as fruit and vegetable scraps, coffee grounds and grass clippings. A good mix is about three parts brown to one part green.

The pile should feel as damp as a wrung-out sponge. Turning it every week or two adds air and speeds things up; a well kept pile can be ready in two to three months.

Do not add meat, fish or dairy, which attract animals and smell. Citrus peels and onions are fine in small amounts.
