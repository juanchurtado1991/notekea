Cleaning and oiling a bicycle chain

By the Example team

A clean chain shifts better and lasts longer. Dirt mixed with oil works like sandpaper on the chain, the cassette and the chainrings.

Start by wiping the chain with a dry rag while turning the pedals backwards. For a dirty chain, use a degreaser and a brush, then rinse with water and let it dry completely.

Apply one drop of chain oil on each roller, on the inside of the chain, while turning the pedals slowly. Wait a few minutes, then wipe off the extra oil from the outside. Too much oil attracts more dirt.

Oil the chain every two or three hundred kilometres, or after every ride in the rain. A chain stretched by wear should be replaced before it damages the cassette; a chain checker tool measures it in seconds.
