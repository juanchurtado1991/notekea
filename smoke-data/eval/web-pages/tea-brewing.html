Brewing tea well

By the Example team

Different teas need different water temperatures. Green tea turns bitter with boiling water and is best at about seventy to eighty degrees Celsius. Black tea and herbal teas take boiling water.

Steeping time matters as much as temperature. Two to three minutes is enough for green tea, three to five minutes for black tea, and five to seven minutes for most herbal teas.

Use about two grams of tea, roughly a teaspoon, for each cup of two hundred and fifty millilitres. Loose leaves need room to unfold, so a large infuser works better than a small ball.

Good green and oolong teas can be steeped several times. Each new steeping brings out a slightly different taste.
