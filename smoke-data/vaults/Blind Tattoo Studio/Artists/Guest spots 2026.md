## General rules for guests
- Every guest signs the studio rules and gets a copy of the [[Consent form]] and the [[Aftercare log]] process.
- Guests use our deposit rules: deposits are taken by the studio and passed on at the end of the spot.
- Guests clean their station following the same daily checklist we use.
- Cash takings are counted every evening with me, and the split is settled on the last day.
- If a guest cancels a session, the client is offered a slot with me or Dario first.
