# Consent form

Studio consent and release form, version 4. Every client reads and signs this before the first session of a
piece. The signed paper copy goes into the client folder in the office; a scan goes into the booking software.

## 1. About you
Full name, date of birth, address, phone or another way to reach you, and an emergency contact. Please show an
ID card or passport so we can check your age. We tattoo adults only: you must be at least 18 years old, and we do
not make exceptions with a parent's permission.

## 2. Your health
Please tell us before we start if any of the following applies to you. Some of them mean we have to wait, some
mean we need a note from your doctor, and some only mean we work a little differently.

- You are pregnant or breastfeeding
- You have diabetes, epilepsy, a heart condition or a blood clotting disorder
- You take blood thinners, or took aspirin or ibuprofen in the last 24 hours
- You have a skin condition on or near the area (eczema, psoriasis, an open wound, sunburn)
- You have had a reaction to a tattoo, a pigment, latex, a plaster or a skin product before
- You have a condition that weakens your immune system
- You have drunk alcohol or taken drugs in the last 24 hours

We work with nitrile gloves only, no latex anywhere in the studio.

## 3. What happens during the session
The artist cleans and shaves the area, places a stencil and checks the placement with you in the mirror. You can
ask for changes until the first line is done. After that, changes to the design are only possible where the
artist thinks they can be done well. You can ask for a break at any time. Please eat before you come and bring
water; we have juice and snacks for emergencies.

Everything that touches your skin is single-use or sterilised in our autoclave. Needles are opened in front of
you and go into a sharps container right after the session. Ink is poured into single-use caps and the rest is
thrown away.

## 4. Risks
A tattoo is a small wound. Even with good aftercare, these things can happen: redness and swelling for a few days,
some weeping of plasma and ink, itching and flaking while it heals, and in rare cases an infection or an allergic
reaction to a pigment, sometimes months or years later. Colours can fade, lines can blur slightly over the years,
and some areas (hands, feet, fingers, lips) fade much faster. Laser removal later is possible but expensive,
painful and not always complete.

## 5. Aftercare
You get a printed aftercare sheet. Following it is your responsibility. We offer a free check-in a couple of weeks
after the session, by photo or in person. Touch-ups of our own work within six months are free if the aftercare
was followed.

## 6. Photos
We like to photograph finished work for our portfolio and Instagram. Tick the box below if you agree. You can
withdraw this consent at any time by writing to us, and we will take the photo down.

- [ ] I agree that the studio may post photos of my tattoo
- [ ] I agree only if my face and any name in the design are not shown

## 7. Your data
We keep this form and your contact details for ten years, as the health office asks us to. We never share them
with anyone except the health office during an inspection, and only if they ask to see the forms.

## 8. Prices and payment
The artist gives you a quote before the first session. For pieces charged by the hour, the quote is an estimate,
and you will be told during the session if it is going to take longer. We take cash, card and bank transfer.
Payment is due at the end of every session.

## 9. Deposits, cancellations and rescheduling
A deposit secures your booking and is taken when you book. It is deducted from the price of the last session of
the piece. The deposit is not refundable if you cancel. You may move your appointment free of charge if you tell
us at least 72 hours before the session starts; the deposit then moves to the new date. If you move it later than
that, or do not come, the deposit is kept and a new deposit is needed for a new date. You can move a booking this
way twice; after that we ask for a new deposit. If the studio has to cancel (illness of the artist, an emergency,
a problem with the room), you choose between a new date and a full refund of the deposit.

## 10. Declaration
I have read this form, I have had the chance to ask questions, and I agree to be tattooed. The information I gave
about my health is true and complete. I understand that a tattoo is permanent.
