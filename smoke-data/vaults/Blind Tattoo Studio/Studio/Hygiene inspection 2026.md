# Hygiene inspection 2026

Yearly visit from the district health office. This year's inspector was H. Albrecht, the same as last year. He
arrived at 09:40, unannounced as usual, and stayed for about two and a half hours.

## Before the visit
I keep a folder ready in the office with everything they usually ask for. This year it held:

- The autoclave logs for the last twelve months, with the monthly spore test results
- The cleaning plan for both rooms and the waiting area, signed off each week
- Proof of the sharps collection (the contract with the waste company and the last collection receipts)
- Training certificates for me and Dario (hygiene course, first aid)
- The product sheets for the surface disinfectant and the skin antiseptic
- A sample of signed consent forms

## What he looked at
He started in the waiting area and the toilet, then went through Room 1 while Dario was setting up, then Room 2,
which was empty that morning. He asked Dario to show how he sets up the station and how he takes it down, and
watched the whole sequence: gloves, barrier film on the lamp and the machine cord, ink caps, the order in which
things go into the bin and the sharps container.

In the back room he opened the autoclave, read the last cycle print-out and compared it with the log. He checked
the date on the last spore test and asked when the next one is due. He looked at how the sterile pouches are
stored (closed cupboard, off the floor, oldest at the front) and checked a few pouches for the date and the colour
change of the indicator.

He then went through the folder page by page. He spent a long time on the cleaning plan and asked who does the
cleaning on the days the service does not come. I said that I do it at closing with the daily checklist, and he
asked to see that too.

## What he liked
- Single-use ink caps and needles opened in front of the client
- The autoclave log is complete and the spore tests are all in order
- Sterile storage is tidy and every pouch was in date
- Nitrile gloves only, no latex in the studio
- Clear separation between clean and dirty areas in both rooms

## What he asked about but did not flag
- Whether guest artists get the same hygiene briefing — yes, they sign the studio rules
- How long we keep the consent forms — ten years
- Whether the floor in Room 2 is sealed — it was redone in August

## The finding
He flagged one thing, and only one: the sharps container in Room 2 was filled above the fill line. It was the
week after Rosa's last spot and nobody had swapped it. He wrote it down as a minor finding, not a fine, but it
needs to be fixed and shown at a follow-up visit.

What we changed straight away:

- New sharps container in Room 2 the same day
- "Check the sharps containers" added to the closing checklist
- Collection by the waste company moved from monthly to every two weeks

## Follow-up
The re-inspection is set for 2027-01-19, in the morning. He will only look at Room 2 and the sharps routine, and
wants to see the new collection receipts. Report letter arrived by post on 2026-10-20 and is filed in the folder.
