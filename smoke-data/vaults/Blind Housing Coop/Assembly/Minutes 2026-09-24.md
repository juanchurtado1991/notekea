# Minutes of the general assembly, 2026-09-24

Community room, 19:00 to 21:50. Chair: [[Petra Lindqvist]]. Minutes: Miriam Kessler.

## 1. Opening and quorum
At 19:00 only 8 of 19 members were present, so the assembly was not quorate. Following the [[Bylaws]],
the second assembly opened at 19:30 with the same agenda and was quorate regardless of attendance.
By 19:30, 16 members were present, and two more arrived during item 3 (18 in total).

## 2. Treasurer's report
[[Georg Wimmer]] presented the figures up to the end of August. Operating costs are within the budget
except heating gas, which was about 12 % over plan because of the cold spring. The [[Renovation fund]]
keeps growing as planned. Questions from Rudi Stastny about the bank fees; Georg will compare two
offers from other banks before the end of the year.

## 3. Facade renovation
Petra presented the three offers and the architect's recommendation (see [[Facade project]]). Long
discussion about noise, the closed courtyard and the bikes. Tomas Hauser asked that older residents
get a written timetable of the works well in advance; the board agreed. Several members asked whether the fund is
enough; the board answered that the gap will be covered by a bank loan or a subsidy, to be decided
in the next assembly.
Motion: approve the facade insulation in principle, with the budget in the Renovation folder, and
start the tender.
Vote: 15 in favour, 2 against, 1 abstention. Accepted.

## 4. Stairwell windows
Motion: replace the five stairwell windows together with the facade, budget 11200 EUR.
Vote: 14 in favour, 2 against, 2 abstentions. Accepted.

## 5. Elevator cables
Lift-Tec recommends replacing the cables in the next two years. No quote yet.
Motion to postpone the decision until there is a quote: accepted by show of hands, no counts taken.

## 6. Courtyard
The garden committee asks for 600 EUR for new benches and a raised bed. Accepted without objection.
Mara Okafor joins the garden committee.

## 7. Elections
The auditor Hannelore Pichl (former member, moved away) has stepped down. Two candidates: Rudi
Stastny and Ingrid Sorger. Ingrid withdrew in favour of Rudi.
Rudi Stastny was elected auditor for three years with 16 votes, 2 abstentions. He accepted.
The second auditor, Paul Sorger, stays in office until 2027.

## 8. Any other business
- Bike room: old bikes will be removed on 2026-10-17 if not labelled.
- Request to allow small dogs in the courtyard: the board will look at the [[House rules]].
- Next assembly planned for early December.

Closed at 21:50.
