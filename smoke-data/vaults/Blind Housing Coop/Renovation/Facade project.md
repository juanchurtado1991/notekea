# Facade project

The big one. The street and courtyard facades of Ahornweg 12 have never been insulated since the
house was built. Plaster is cracking on the north side, and the heating bills keep growing. The board
wants to insulate both facades in 2027, while also replacing the [[Stairwell windows]].

## Why now
- The heating gas line is the biggest item of the operating budget every year.
- An energy audit in spring 2026 rated the building class E. With 14 cm of insulation the auditor
  expects class C and roughly a third less gas.
- The [[Renovation fund]] has been saved up for this since 2015.
- Scaffolding is expensive, so the windows and the gutter work should happen in the same weeks.

## History
- 2025-11: first talks in the board, Petra takes the lead.
- 2026-03-19: assembly asks for offers and a window study (see [[Minutes 2026-03-19]]).

## Neighbours
The building next door (Ahornweg 10) belongs to a private owner. We need their written consent for the
scaffolding on the shared courtyard wall. Petra wrote to them in October; no answer yet.

## Noise and access
Work hours would be 7:00 to 17:00 on weekdays. The courtyard will be closed for about four weeks. The
bike room stays open but access goes through the cellar door.

## Colour
We asked all residents to vote on the new facade colour in October, with three samples on the wall of
the courtyard for two weeks. Result of the residents' poll: light ivory 7 votes, warm grey 2, pale
green 1. So the facade will be light ivory, with the window frames in white as before. The architect
will put the colour in the tender documents.
