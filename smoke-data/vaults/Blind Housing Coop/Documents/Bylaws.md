# Bylaws of the Wohnhof Ahornweg Housing Cooperative

Consolidated text kept by the secretary. The signed original is in the board cabinet.

## §1 Name and seat
The cooperative is called Wohnhof Ahornweg Housing Cooperative. Its seat is Ahornweg 12, 1160 Wien.

## §2 Purpose
The cooperative provides its members with affordable homes and runs the house at Ahornweg 12 on a
not-for-profit basis. Surpluses stay in the cooperative and are used for the upkeep of the house.

## §3 Membership
1. Any adult person can apply to become a member. The board decides on applications.
2. Members who do not live in the house are kept on a waiting list in the order of their application.
3. Each member holds at least one share of 1500 EUR. A member living in the house holds one extra
   share per started 20 m² of the flat.

## §4 Rights of members
1. Every member has one vote in the general assembly, whatever the number of shares.
2. Members may see the annual accounts and the minutes of the general assembly.
3. Members living in the house have the right to use their flat for as long as they are members.

## §5 Duties of members
1. Members pay the monthly fee set by the general assembly on time.
2. Members treat the house and the common areas with care and follow the [[House rules]].
3. Damage must be reported to the board without delay.

## §6 Monthly fee
1. The fee covers the operating costs of the house and a contribution to the renovation fund.
2. The general assembly sets the fee for the next year on a proposal of the board.
3. The board may raise the fee once a year by up to 3 % without a vote of the assembly, to follow
   rising operating costs. Any larger change needs the assembly.

## §7 Use of the flats
1. Flats are used as homes. Small home offices are allowed if they do not bring clients into the house.
2. Short-term rentals to tourists are not allowed.

## §8 Alterations
1. Changes inside a flat that touch walls, pipes or wiring need the consent of the board.
2. Changes visible from outside, such as balcony screens, awnings or satellite dishes, need the
   approval of the board and must fit the look of the facade.
3. The member pays for the change and, when moving out, restores the original state if the board
   asks for it.

## §9 Subletting
1. A member may sublet the whole flat only for good reasons, such as work or study abroad or care of a
   relative, and only with the written consent of the board.
2. Subletting is allowed for no more than 24 months in total within ten years.
3. The member stays responsible for the fee and for any damage caused by the subtenant.

## §10 Leaving the cooperative
1. A member may leave at the end of a calendar year by written notice to the board, given at least six
   months before.
2. Shares are paid back within one year after leaving, less any open debts to the cooperative.
3. A member can be excluded by the general assembly for serious breach of these bylaws, in particular
   for fee arrears of more than three months after two written reminders.

## §11 General assembly
1. The ordinary general assembly meets at least twice a year.
2. The board invites all members in writing at least two weeks before, with the agenda.
3. The assembly is quorate when at least half of the members are present. If it is not quorate, a
   second assembly with the same agenda opens thirty minutes later and is quorate whatever the number
   of members present.
4. Decisions are taken by simple majority of the votes cast. Changes to these bylaws need two thirds.
5. Minutes are kept by the secretary and signed by the chair.

## §12 Board
1. The board has three members: chair, treasurer and secretary, elected for three years.
2. The board runs the day-to-day business of the cooperative.
3. Payments and contracts need the signatures of two board members, one of them the treasurer or the
   chair.

## §13 Auditors
1. The general assembly elects two auditors for three years. They may not be board members.
2. The auditors check the accounts once a year and report to the general assembly.

## §14 Disputes
1. Disputes between members, or between a member and the board, about the use of the house or these
   bylaws are first brought to the arbitration committee.
2. The arbitration committee has three members: each side names one, and these two choose a chair.
3. Only after the arbitration committee has tried to settle the dispute may a party go to court.

## §15 Dissolution
1. The cooperative can be dissolved by a two-thirds majority of all members.
2. Any remaining assets go to a not-for-profit housing organisation chosen by the last assembly.
