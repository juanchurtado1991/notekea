# House rules

## Quiet hours
Between 10 pm and 6 am, and on Sundays and public holidays all day for drilling and hammering.

## Pets
Cats and small animals are allowed. Dogs are allowed in the flats but must be on a lead in the stairwell,
and are not allowed in the courtyard (under review, see [[Next assembly]]).

## Bike room and cellar
Label your bike with your unit number. Nothing may be stored in the corridors.

## Rubbish
Separate paper, plastic, glass and organic waste. Bulky waste goes to the city collection point, never
into the courtyard.

## Laundry
The drying room is shared; take your laundry down within two days.
