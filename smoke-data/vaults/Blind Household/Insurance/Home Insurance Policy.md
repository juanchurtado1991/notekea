# Home Insurance Policy

Our summary of the homeowner policy for 48 Larkspur Avenue. The full wording PDF is in the
filing cabinet, second drawer, and in the shared drive. Broker: [[Priya Shah]].

## At a glance
- Insurer: Maple Shield Mutual
- Policy number: HX-448120
- Policy period: 2026-11-01 to 2027-11-01
- Premium: 1,710.00 a year, paid monthly at 142.50 from joint chequing
- Named insureds: Nora Castellan and Theo Castellan

## Deductibles
- Standard deductible: 1,000 per claim.
- Sewer backup and overland water: separate deductible of 2,500.
- Earthquake (not purchased): would be 5% of the dwelling limit.

## Endorsements we added
- Sewer backup, limit 25,000. Added after the 2024 basement water (see [[Claims History]]).
- Overland water, limit 25,000.
- Guaranteed replacement cost on the dwelling (requires that the dwelling limit is kept at
  least at the insurer's estimate; we review it at every renewal).
- Identity theft expenses, limit 15,000.

## Discounts on the bill
- Claims-free for two years: no (the 2024 claim still counts until 2027).
- Monitored alarm: yes, 10%.
- Home and auto with the same broker: yes, 7%.
- Sump pump with backup battery: yes, 5%.

## Special limits on contents
Some kinds of property have lower limits unless they are listed (scheduled) separately with
a value and an appraisal:
- Jewellery, watches and furs: 6,000 for theft.
- Cash and gift cards: 500.
- Bicycles: 2,000 per bike.
- Collectibles and stamps: 2,500.
- Computers and equipment used for a home business: 3,000.
- Watercraft and their motors: 1,500.

Nora's engagement ring is scheduled separately at 7,800 with the 2023 appraisal; it has no
deductible.

## Conditions we have to meet
- Tell the insurer within 30 days about any change in the use of the home (for example, a
  rental unit or a business with customers coming in).
- Keep the home heated during the heating season.
- Take reasonable steps to protect the property after a loss (board up, call a plumber),
  and keep the receipts.
- Report theft and vandalism to the police.
- Give a proof of loss within 90 days when the insurer asks for it.

## How to claim
1. Make the place safe and stop further damage.
2. Call the 24-hour claims line: 1-888-555-0142, policy HX-448120.
3. Photograph everything before throwing anything away.
4. Tell our broker the same day.
5. Keep every receipt for emergency repairs and hotel nights.

## Renewal notes
- The renewal notice comes about 60 days before 2027-11-01.
- At renewal, ask about the claims-free discount and whether the alarm discount can go up
  with the new water sensors.

## Exclusions worth remembering
The policy does not cover:
- Wear and tear, rust, rot, mould, and gradual leaks we should have noticed.
- Damage by insects, rodents or other vermin.
- Water damage from a frozen pipe or a heating system, when the home has been unheated and
  unoccupied for more than 4 consecutive days, unless someone enters the home daily to make
  sure the heat is on, or the water is shut off and the pipes are drained. (Matters for
  Christmas at Theo's parents.)
- Settling, cracking or shrinking of foundations and walls.
- Losses from a home-based business beyond the 3,000 equipment limit, and business
  liability.
- Renovations: the insurer must be told in writing before any renovation lasting more
  than 30 days or costing more than 50,000. If not, damage in the area being renovated
  may not be covered while the work is going on.
- Vacancy: no coverage for vandalism or glass breakage after the home has been vacant for
  more than 30 consecutive days.
- Intentional acts, war, nuclear incidents, and government seizure.
