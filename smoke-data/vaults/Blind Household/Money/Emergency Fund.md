# Emergency Fund

Kept in a separate high-interest savings account we call "Rainy Day" at Cedarline Credit
Union. It earns 3.1% interest (rate checked 2026-10-15), paid monthly.

## Rules
- Only for job loss, medical costs, or urgent house and car repairs.
- Not for trips, gifts or the kitchen renovation.
- Anything we take out gets paid back within three months.
- Target: about six months of fixed costs, 30,000.
