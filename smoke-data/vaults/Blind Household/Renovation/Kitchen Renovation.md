# Kitchen Renovation

The kitchen is original to the 1990s flip: laminate counters lifting at the sink, a
cabinet door that falls off twice a month, one outlet on the whole counter, and a range
hood that only makes noise. We've talked about it since we moved in. This is the year.

## What we want
- Keep the same footprint (no moving the sink wall; the plumbing stack is there).
- Take out the half wall to the dining room. Daniel checked: not load-bearing.
- Full-height cabinets on the fridge wall, a pantry pull-out, deep drawers instead of lower
  doors.
- Quartz counters, simple and light.
- Induction range, proper ducted hood vented outside.
- Six counter outlets, under-cabinet lights, a dimmer for the pendants.
- Vinyl plank floor that matches the hallway as closely as possible.

## What we are NOT doing
- No island (the room is 3.4 m wide, it would block the dishwasher).
- No moving the window.
- No pot filler, no wine fridge, no smart fridge.

## Money
The numbers live in [[Renovation/Budget|the renovation budget]]. The cushion we are saving
on the side is in [[Savings Goals]]. We are not touching the [[Emergency Fund]] for this.

## Picking a contractor
We got three quotes, see [[Contractor Quotes]]. On 2026-10-12 we picked Okafor Build Co.
([[Daniel Okafor]]). Reasons: permits included, he looked at the wall properly, and the
timeline fits before Lisbon. Brennan was cheaper but permits extra and slower; Vega was the
most expensive.

## Living without a kitchen
- Temporary kitchen in the basement laundry room: old fridge, microwave, the induction hot
  plate, a folding table.
- Paper plates allowed. Dishes in the laundry sink.
- Biscuit's food and bowls move to the mudroom.
- Budget extra for takeout: one night a week, not more (comes out of Dining out).

## Design choices
- Cabinet colour: Sage Fog green, matte, with brushed brass pulls.
- Counter: white quartz with a faint grey vein, eased edge, 3 cm.
- Backsplash: plain white tile, 7.5 x 15 cm, stacked vertically, light grey grout.
- Floor: oak-look vinyl plank, "Harvest" tone.
- Walls: warm white, eggshell.
