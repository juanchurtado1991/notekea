# Care plan Ramón

Plan de cuidados de papá — the version I share with Lucía Benítez, my brother Javier (in Hamburg)
and anyone who covers. Mixed Spanish and English because Javier reads it too.

## 1. Quién es y cómo está

Ramón Alvarado, 84 años. Vive solo en su piso de El Palo (tercero sin ascensor — sí, ya lo sé).
Diagnósticos: hipertensión desde hace veinte años y Parkinson leve diagnosticado en marzo de 2025.
Cabeza perfecta, mal genio por las mañanas, encantador por las tardes. Camina con andador fuera de
casa; dentro se apoya en los muebles y no quiere oír hablar del andador.

**Alergias:** penicilina (le dio una reacción fuerte en 1998). Lo tiene apuntado en la tarjeta
sanitaria, pero hay que decirlo siempre en urgencias.

Weekends: I go on Saturday mornings; on Sundays he has lunch with his neighbour Paco or comes to me.

## 3. Alimentación

- Low salt (hypertension). No added salt at the table; Lucía cooks with herbs and lemon.
- Fibre and water: constipation is the Parkinson's friend nobody talks about. 1.5 litres a day.
- Protein in the evening rather than at midday, so it does not get in the way of the levodopa.
- He is allowed one small glass of wine on Sundays. He will try to negotiate two.

## 4. Movilidad y caídas

Riesgo de caída moderado. Alfombras retiradas del pasillo en septiembre; barra en la ducha instalada.
Lleva la pulsera de teleasistencia de la Junta (botón rojo) — check every morning that he is wearing it.

**If he falls:**
1. Do not lift him on your own. Ask him to stay still and check whether he can move arms and legs.
2. If he hit his head, is bleeding, or cannot get up with a little help, call 112 straight away.
3. If he is fine and can get up with a chair, help him up slowly, sit him down, and give him water.
4. In every case, write it in the log at the end of this note and tell me the same day.

## 5. Medicación

Lucía prepares nothing: I fill the pill organiser on Sundays. The details and the doses are in
[[Medication]]. If he refuses a dose, do not insist; tell me. Never give him ibuprofen.

## 7. Ayudas y papeles

- Grado I de dependencia reconocido en enero de 2026. Prestación económica para cuidados en el entorno
  familiar: 180 € al mes, ingresada a primeros de mes.
- La revisión del grado se puede pedir si empeora; la asistenta social del centro de salud es Carmen.
- Seguro de decesos con Santa Lucía (papeles en el cajón de la cómoda).

## 8. Señales de alarma

Call me straight away (and the GP the same day) if:
- he is more confused than usual or does not recognise someone he knows,
- the tremor or stiffness gets suddenly worse,
- he has a fever above 38 °C,
- he stops eating for a whole day,
- his blood pressure is above 170 on the morning reading two days in a row.

For chest pain, difficulty breathing or a face that droops on one side: 112, no thinking.

## 9. Lo que le hace feliz

Talking about ships. The Málaga CF on the radio. Crosswords from the Sur. Visits from the girls of
the team (they came in June and he still talks about Vera, "la portera"). Boquerones on Sundays.
Do not mention moving to a residencia unless you want an afternoon of silence.
