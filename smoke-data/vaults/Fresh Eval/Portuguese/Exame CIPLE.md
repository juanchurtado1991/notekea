# Exame CIPLE

CIPLE = A2, the first of the CAPLE exams of the University of Lisbon. Exam centre: Instituto Camões
in Seville (the closest one).

- **Exam date:** Friday 21 May 2027
- **Registration:** opens 2027-02-01, closes 2027-03-15
- **Fee:** 85 €
- Parts: reading and writing (75 min), listening (30 min), oral (about 15 min, in pairs)
