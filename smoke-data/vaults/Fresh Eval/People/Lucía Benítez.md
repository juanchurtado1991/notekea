# Lucía Benítez

Lucía looks after [[Ramón Alvarado|papá]] on weekday mornings. She has worked with older people for
fifteen years, mostly through the Ayuntamiento's home help programme before going private.

- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 9:00–13:00 (20 h a week)
- **Rate:** 10 € per hour, paid by bank transfer at the end of each month
- **Extra hours:** same rate, agreed by WhatsApp the day before
- Birthday: 14 May

## Notes
- She prefers to be told about appointments a week ahead so she can change her afternoon jobs.
- She does the shopping on Tuesdays and Fridays with papá's list.
- Holidays: two weeks in August; her cousin Rocío covered in 2026.

Hours are logged in [[Carer hours October]].
