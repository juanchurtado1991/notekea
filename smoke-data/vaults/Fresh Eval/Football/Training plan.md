# Training plan

Tuesdays and Thursdays, 18:00–19:30, Campo Municipal Los Pinares (half pitch).

## Structure of a session
1. Warm-up with ball, 15 min (Pedro)
2. Technique block, 20 min — passing in triangles, first touch
3. Small games 4v4, 25 min
4. Match situation, 20 min
5. Stretching and chat, 10 min

## Autumn focus
- Defending as a block, not chasing the ball all together
- Goalkeeper playing out with her feet (Vera is getting braver)
- Talking on the pitch: "¡tengo!", "¡sola!", "¡cambia!"
