# Club code of conduct

My English summary of the CD Pinares *Código de conducta y reglamento interno* (the Spanish PDF is on
the club website). I keep this here so I can check things quickly at the pitch.

## 1. Purpose

The club exists so that children and young people in the neighbourhood can play football in a safe,
respectful and fun environment. Winning matters less than learning, effort and team spirit. Every
member of the club — players, families, coaches, delegates and board — accepts this code when they
sign up or renew.

## 2. Players

1. Arrive at training at least ten minutes before the start, with the club kit and shin pads.
2. Tell the coach in advance if you cannot come; three unexplained absences in a month are reviewed
   with the family.
3. Respect teammates, opponents, referees and the facilities. Insults, including on WhatsApp and
   social media, are a serious offence.
4. Mobile phones stay in the bag during training and in the changing room.
5. Help to collect the material at the end of each session (balls, cones, bibs, goals).

## 3. Parents and guardians

1. Encourage, do not coach. Instructions from the touchline confuse the players; leave the tactics to
   the coaches.
2. Never address the referee or the opposing team's families during a match.
3. Bring and collect the children on time. Children under 12 are not left at the gate alone; a coach
   stays until the last child has been collected.
4. Keep the club informed of any medical condition, allergy or medication that may matter during
   activity.
5. Use the team's WhatsApp group only for organisation. Complaints go to the coach in person or to the
   coordinator, never to the group.

## 4. Coaches and delegates

1. Hold the federation licence that corresponds to the category, or be in the process of obtaining it
   within the season.
2. Give every player meaningful minutes over the season. In the alevín and younger categories every
   player called up for a match plays at least one full quarter of it.
3. Keep the team's attendance record and hand it to the coordinator at the end of each term.
4. Never be alone with a single child in a closed space; changing rooms are entered only when
   necessary and with another adult present.
5. Delegates handle the match sheet, the player cards and the first-aid kit on match days.

## 5. Match days

- Players meet at the venue 45 minutes before kick-off for home games and at the agreed point for
  away games.
- The match sheet is closed 20 minutes before kick-off; only players with their federation card and
  their medical form handed in can be on it.
- Families sit in the stand, not behind the benches.
- After the match both teams shake hands and say thank you to the referee, whatever the result.

## 6. Transport

- Families organise the cars for away matches, coordinated by the team delegate.
- Every child travels in a car seat or booster appropriate for their height, as the law requires.
- The club hires a minibus for tournaments outside the province; the cost is shared by the families
  of the players who travel.

## 7. Injuries and medical

1. Every team carries a first-aid kit, checked monthly by the delegate.
2. A player who receives a blow to the head leaves the pitch and does not return to play that day,
   even if they say they feel fine. The family is told immediately.
3. Any injury that needs a medical visit is reported to the club office within 48 hours, so the
   federation insurance can be used.
4. In case of a serious injury, call 112 first and the coordinator second.

## 8. Weather

- Training and matches are suspended if there is lightning within sight; play resumes 30 minutes
  after the last flash.
- With temperatures above 30 °C, coaches give a water break every 15 minutes, and sessions are moved
  to the last hour of the afternoon whenever possible.
- When the AEMET issues an orange warning for rain or wind, the coordinator decides by 15:00 whether
  that day's activity goes ahead.

## 9. Photography and social media

1. Photos and videos of players may only be published on the club's official accounts, and only with
   the signed image consent of both parents or legal guardians.
2. Coaches do not post photos of players on their personal accounts.
3. Families may take photos of their own children during matches; sharing pictures of other children
   requires their families' permission.
4. Players' surnames are never published next to their photos.

## 10. Disciplinary procedure

1. Minor misconduct (lateness, unkind remarks, ignoring instructions) is dealt with by the coach with
   a conversation and, if repeated, a written warning to the family.
2. Three written warnings in one season lead to a suspension of two matches.
3. Serious misconduct (violence, discriminatory insults, bullying) goes directly to the disciplinary
   committee, which can suspend a player or a family member from club activities for up to a season.
4. Any decision can be appealed to the board within ten days.

## 11. Fees

- The annual fee for the 2026-27 season is 240 €, payable in three instalments of 80 € (September,
  January and April).
- Siblings receive a 20 % discount on the second and further children's fees.
- Families in financial difficulty can ask the social committee for a reduced fee in confidence.
- The fee covers federation insurance, the training kit and the end-of-season party; the match kit is
  paid separately.

## 12. Lost property and material

- Lost property is kept in the material room for 30 days and then donated.
- Balls and material taken home for practice must be signed out with the coach.

## 13. Final provisions

This code is reviewed every two years by the board. Suggestions can be made to any board member or by
email to the club secretary. In everything not covered here, the regulations of the Real Federación
Andaluza de Fútbol apply.
