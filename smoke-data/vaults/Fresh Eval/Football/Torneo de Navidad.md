# Torneo de Navidad

Torneo de Navidad Costa del Sol, alevín femenino, at the Ciudad Deportiva de Carranque.

- **Date:** Saturday 19 December 2026, groups from 9:30, final at 13:30
- **Registration:** 15 € per player on the roster, paid by the team in one transfer
- **Deadline to register:** 30 November
- Eight teams, two groups of four, matches of 2 × 12 minutes
- Only players with the medical form handed in can be on the match sheet

Lucía Ortega will collect the money from the families.
