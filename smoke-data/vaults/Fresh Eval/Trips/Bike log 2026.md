# Bike log 2026

Every ride worth writing down this year, with the distance from the computer and a line or two.
Short commutes to the studio do not count.

## January

### 2026-01-11 — Málaga → Rincón de la Victoria and back

- Distance: 34 km
- Cold and windy on the paseo marítimo. Legs dead after Christmas.
- Moving time: 2 h 05
- Weather: 12 °C, poniente gusts
- Turned back at the Cueva del Tesoro instead of going on to Torre de Benagalbón.

### 2026-01-25 — Montes de Málaga loop (Fuente de la Reina)

- Distance: 48 km
- First climb of the year; walked the last bend. No shame.
- Moving time: 3 h 20
- Weather: 9 °C at the top, fog
- Fuente de la Reina café closed, of course. Ate the emergency bar.

## February

### 2026-02-08 — Málaga → Torremolinos and back

- Distance: 42 km
- Flat, sunny, a coffee in La Carihuela.
- Moving time: 2 h 15
- Weather: 19 °C, no wind
- Counted eleven other cyclists with panniers; touring season is starting.

### 2026-02-22 — Guadalhorce river path → Cártama

- Distance: 51 km
- Muddy after the rain; cleaned the bike for an hour.
- Moving time: 3 h 10
- Weather: 15 °C, grey
- The path is flooded under the motorway bridge; take the road from Campanillas next time.

## March

### 2026-03-14 — Málaga → Nerja

- Distance: 58 km
- Coast road, then bus back with the bike. Great views at Maro.
- Moving time: 3 h 40
- Weather: 20 °C, clear
- Bus Nerja–Málaga takes bikes in the hold if the driver is in a good mood.

## April

### 2026-04-04 — Antequera → El Torcal → Antequera

- Distance: 46 km
- Steep! Wind on top of the Torcal, saw ibex.
- Moving time: 3 h 30
- Weather: 14 °C, strong wind on top
- Rode the road up and the old track down; brakes squealing all the way.

### 2026-04-18 — Caminito del Rey area: Álora → El Chorro

- Distance: 39 km
- Train to Álora, rode up to the reservoir and back to the station.
- Moving time: 2 h 50
- Weather: 22 °C
- Too many tourists on the walkway itself; rode around the reservoirs instead.

## May

### 2026-05-09 — Málaga → Ronda

- Distance: 112 km
- With Marta. Seven and a half hours, the climb after El Burgo felt endless. Slept in Ronda and came back by train.
- Moving time: 7 h 30
- Weather: 26 °C, hot in the afternoon
- Ate at El Burgo, two bocadillos. Train back from Ronda had only two bike hooks.

### 2026-05-30 — Vía Verde de la Sierra (Olvera → Puerto Serrano)

- Distance: 72 km
- Tunnels and viaducts, vultures. Car to Olvera with Marta's van.
- Moving time: 4 h 30
- Weather: 24 °C, breezy
- Peñón de Zaframagón: dozens of griffon vultures. Bring binoculars next time.

## June

### 2026-06-14 — Málaga → Rincón and back (shop run)

- Distance: 30 km
- Took the bike to Ciclos Victoria: new chain.
- Moving time: 1 h 50
- Weather: 28 °C
- Waited an hour at the shop; read the paper with papá's glasses by mistake.

### 2026-06-28 — Montes de Málaga, early

- Distance: 44 km
- Left at 7:00 to beat the heat. Worked.
- Moving time: 2 h 55
- Weather: 25 °C at 7:00, 33 °C by 11:00
- Home before the worst of it. Learned the lesson.

## July

### 2026-07-19 — Vélez-Málaga → Torrox → Nerja

- Distance: 37 km
- Short and hot; swim at Burriana.
- Moving time: 2 h 20
- Weather: 34 °C
- Too hot to ride in July. Last ride until September.

## August

No rides: too hot, and the shop's summer sale took every free hour.

## September

### 2026-09-06 — Málaga → Fuengirola and back

- Distance: 62 km
- First long one after summer. Headwind on the way back.
- Moving time: 3 h 30
- Weather: 29 °C, levante then poniente
- Wind changed at noon and I got it in the face both ways. Typical.

### 2026-09-27 — Vía Verde del Aceite, Jaén (Martos → Alcaudete)

- Distance: 56 km
- Olive trees forever. Weekend with Marta's family.
- Moving time: 3 h 45
- Weather: 23 °C, sunny
- Marta's uncle lent us his old Peugeot tandem for a lap of the village. Never again.

## October

### 2026-10-11 — Guadalhorce loop

- Distance: 28 km
- After the match against Teatinos, to clear my head.
- Moving time: 1 h 40
- Weather: 21 °C
- Good for the mood. The girls played well even if we lost.

### 2026-10-31 — Málaga → Benalmádena Pueblo

- Distance: 41 km
- Climb to the pueblo, sunset on the way down.
- Moving time: 2 h 35
- Weather: 20 °C, golden light
- Halloween kids already out in costumes when I got back.

## Mechanical

- 2026-02-23 — Cleaned and re-greased the bottom bracket after the Cártama mud.
- 2026-04-20 — New rear tyre (Schwalbe Marathon 40 mm). Front still fine.
- 2026-06-14 — Chain replaced at Ciclos Victoria at 4820 km on the odometer; cassette still OK.
- 2026-09-07 — Brake pads front and back.
- 2026-11-02 — Rear wheel slightly out of true; leave it for the service before the Algarve.

## Kit that worked

- Merino base layer for winter mornings: never again without it.
- The handlebar bag from Decathlon holds phone, wallet and two bananas exactly.
- Gel saddle cover: useless, took it off in March.

## Lessons for the Algarve

- More than 90 km in one day is fine if I eat every hour. Ronda proved it, barely.
- Start early from May onwards.
- Carry two tubes: the second puncture always comes.
- A rest day after the longest stage, not before.
- Book the bus with the bike in advance; on the way back from Ronda the train only had two bike hooks.
