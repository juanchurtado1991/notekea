# Route plan

Sevilla → Lagos by bike, Easter 2027. Alone, or with Marta if she gets the days off.
Start: Saturday 3 April 2027, with the train Málaga–Sevilla the evening before.

| Stage | From → To | km | Sleep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sevilla → Huelva | 95 | Hostal in the centre |
| 2 | Huelva → Ayamonte | 55 | Camping Isla Canela |
| 3 | Ayamonte → Tavira (ferry to Vila Real) | 40 | Casa de hóspedes |
| 4 | Tavira → Faro | 35 | Hostel |
| 5 | Faro → Portimão | 70 | Guesthouse |
| 6 | Portimão → Lagos | 25 | Apartment, 2 nights |
| — | Rest day in Lagos | 0 | |

Then the bus back Lagos → Sevilla and the train home.

## Notes
- Ecovia do Litoral from Vila Real all the way, mostly flat; the bit after Faro airport is ugly.
- Stage 1 is long and has little shade: leave at 7:30.
