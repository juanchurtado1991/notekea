# Race Fueling

- Breakfast 3 hours before the start: bagel, honey, banana, coffee.
- Gels: one every 40 minutes from minute 40, 25 g carbs each.
- Caffeine gel at about two hours.
- Water at every aid station, electrolyte drink every other one.
- Lesson from the 2025 marathon: I cramped at mile 22 with only three gels.

Used in [[Mile High Half 2025]] and planned for [[Front Range Marathon 2027]].
