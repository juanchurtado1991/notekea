# Strength Routine

Twice a week (Monday and Thursday).

- Spanish squats with band — 4 x 45 s
- Single-leg press — 4 x 8 (current load 60 kg)
- Bulgarian split squat — 3 x 10 each leg
- Romanian deadlift — 3 x 8, 40 kg
- Calf raises, straight and bent knee — 3 x 15 each
- Side plank — 3 x 30 s each side

Progress one variable at a time. See [[Physio Sessions]] for what Sarah changed.
