# Puppy Contract

Sales agreement used for [[Litter Juniper 2026]]. Version 3. Changes from version 2: clearer
rehoming clause, added late pickup terms.

## 1. Parties
This agreement is between Dana Whitlock, doing business as Whitlock Canine, Eugene, Oregon
("Breeder"), and the person named on the signature page ("Buyer"). It covers one Border Collie puppy
identified by collar colour and, after 2026-11-20, by microchip number.

## 2. Price and deposit
The purchase price is listed on the signature page. A deposit holds a specific puppy once the Breeder
has matched Buyer and puppy. The deposit is non-refundable, except when the Breeder cannot provide a
suitable puppy, in which case it is refunded in full or moved to a future litter, at the Buyer's
choice. The balance is due at pickup, before the puppy leaves the premises. Breeder does not accept
payment plans for the balance.

## 3. What the Buyer receives
- The puppy, with age-appropriate vaccinations and deworming as recorded in the vet record.
- A vet health exam done within seven days of go-home day.
- Microchip, registered to the Buyer after the balance is paid.
- Registration paperwork (limited registration unless section 9 says otherwise).
- A puppy packet: feeding chart, a small bag of the food the puppy is eating, a blanket that smells of
  the litter, and a copy of this agreement.

## 4. Buyer's care obligations
The Buyer agrees to:
- keep the puppy as a companion or sport dog, indoors with the family, not kennelled outdoors full time;
- provide routine veterinary care, including the vaccinations listed in the puppy packet;
- feed a good quality diet and keep the dog at a healthy weight (lean, ribs easy to feel);
- give the dog daily exercise and mental work suited to a working breed;
- enroll in a positive-reinforcement puppy class before the puppy is 6 months old;
- never use shock, prong or choke collars in training;
- keep the dog on leash or in a securely fenced area except during supervised training or sport.

## 5. Vet check after pickup
The Buyer must have the puppy examined by a licensed veterinarian within 72 hours of pickup (weekends
and holidays excluded). If that vet finds a serious congenital problem, the Buyer may return the puppy
for a full refund of the purchase price, with written findings from the vet.

## 6. Spay and neuter
For pet and sport homes the Buyer agrees not to spay or neuter the dog before 18 months of age, to let
growth plates close, unless a veterinarian recommends it for a medical reason. Proof of spay or neuter
is not required for limited registration puppies.

## 7. Health guarantee
The Breeder guarantees the puppy against hereditary conditions that are life-threatening or that
seriously affect quality of life for **26 months** from the date of birth. Covered conditions include
hip and elbow dysplasia (as rated by OFA, or by PennHIP with a vet's opinion), Collie Eye Anomaly,
progressive retinal atrophy, and epilepsy with no other cause found. To claim, the Buyer sends the
Breeder copies of the diagnosis and x-rays or test results. The Breeder will then, at the Buyer's choice:
replace the puppy with one from a future litter, or refund half of the purchase price. The Buyer is not
required to return the dog. The guarantee does not cover injuries, infections, parasites, conditions
caused by environment or diet, or hips of a dog that was overweight or spayed/neutered before 18 months.

## 8. Rehoming and return
If at any point in the dog's life the Buyer can no longer keep it, the Buyer must first offer the dog
back to the Breeder. The Breeder will take the dog back at any age, for any reason. The dog may never
be surrendered to a shelter, rescue, or sold at auction. If the Breeder agrees, the Buyer may place the
dog directly with a new home approved by the Breeder. No refund is given for a dog returned after the
health check period in section 5, except as the Breeder decides case by case.

## 9. Registration and breeding
Puppies are sold on limited registration. Full registration may be granted later, in writing, if the
dog passes hips, elbows and eyes after age two and the Breeder agrees. The Buyer agrees not to breed
the dog without full registration and the Breeder's written consent.

## 10. Contact and updates
The Buyer agrees to send a photo and a short update at least once a year, and to tell the Breeder
about any change of address or any serious health issue. The Breeder is available for training and
care questions for the life of the dog, free of charge.

## 11. Pickup and late pickup
Go-home day is 2026-11-22. If the Buyer cannot collect the puppy that day, the Breeder will keep the
puppy at no charge until 2026-11-29. From 2026-11-30, a boarding fee of **$35 per day** applies, payable
at pickup. If the puppy is not collected by 2026-12-20 and the Buyer has not been in touch, the Breeder
may treat the sale as cancelled and keep the deposit.

## 12. Whole agreement
This is the whole agreement between Breeder and Buyer. Changes must be in writing and signed by both.
Oregon law applies. If one part is found invalid, the rest still applies.
