# Megan Pruitt

Dog: **Ranger**, German Shepherd, neutered male, 3 years. Rescue from a farm outside Cottage Grove.
Referred by [[Lucy Fernsby]]. Interested in the waitlist for a future litter, maybe 2028.

## Presenting problem
Ranger lunges and barks at bicycles and at unfamiliar dogs on leash. Fine with people, fine with the
cat at home, fine off-leash with two dogs he grew up with. Megan has stopped walking him in daylight
and walks at 5 am to avoid the bike path. She is exhausted and a bit embarrassed. Neighbours have
complained twice.

## Plan
1. Management first: change walk routes, use the side yard, cover the front window film.
2. Engage-disengage game with bikes at distance (use the park lot, Sunday mornings are quiet).
3. Pattern games ("1-2-3 walking") so Ranger has something predictable to do.
4. Mat settle at home, decompression sniffaris twice a week.
5. Later: parallel walks with a calm helper dog (Pepper is too old for this; ask Lucy about Pip?).

## Session log

### 2026-09-15 — intake (home visit)
Long history. Ranger reacted to a bike at roughly 40 m on the street outside. Muzzle trained already
(basket muzzle, fits well). Megan uses a prong collar — talked about switching to a harness with a
front clip. She agreed to try for two weeks. Homework: hand-feed breakfast during a "look at that" game.

### 2026-09-29 — park lot
Harness switch went fine. Worked at 35 m from the bike path. Ranger noticed, looked back at Megan
about half the time. Some barking when a group of three cyclists went by. Shortened the session.

### 2026-10-06 — park lot
Better. Megan's timing is improving; she clicks too late when she is nervous. Practised the
"U-turn, let's go" emergency exit five times without any trigger around, then twice for real.

### 2026-10-20 — neighbourhood walk
First session on her own street. One dog walker across the road, Ranger whined but did not lunge.
Paid on the spot (see [[Invoices]]). Megan cried a little, happy tears.

### 2026-11-03 — park lot + bike helper
Used my friend on a bike as a controlled trigger. Ranger stayed under threshold at about 20 m,
ate every treat, offered check-ins on his own. Great day.

## Medical
Ranger had a full vet workup in August at Coburg Road Vet (Dr. Okonkwo): thyroid normal, no
orthopedic pain found. He is very stressed at the clinic itself, so Dr. Okonkwo prescribed
**trazodone** to be given only before vet visits and grooming — not daily. Megan says it helps a lot.
Nothing else on board. No supplements.
