# Walt Hendricks

Dog: **Duke**, yellow Labrador, 2 years, intact male. Walt is 78 and had a fall when Duke pulled him
down the porch steps in September. His daughter set up the training. Goal: Duke walks politely, sits
for the door, does not jump on Walt.

## Board-and-train (2 weeks)
Duke stayed with me from 2026-10-10 to 2026-10-24. Invoiced at the end (see [[Invoices]]).

### Daily log
- **Day 1** — settling in, crate at night, a bit vocal. Ate fine. Jumped on me every time I came in.
- **Day 2** — started "four on the floor" with treats tossed low. Fewer jumps by evening.
- **Day 3** — door routine: sit, wait, release word **"okay"**. 10 reps, 3 times a day.
- **Day 4** — first loose-leash work in the yard. Lots of pulling to the fence line.
- **Day 5** — rain all day, indoor games only. Mat settle, 3 minutes.
- **Day 6** — street walk, short. Pulled at squirrels. Used the U-turn.
- **Day 7** — good day. 15-minute walk mostly on a loose lead. Waited at the door with it open.
- **Day 8** — helped at the Tuesday class as a demo dog for "leave it". Very proud of him.
- **Day 9** — stairs practice: Duke goes down only after the handler, on cue. This is the important
  one for Walt.
- **Day 10** — tried with an older friend of mine walking him; went well.
- **Day 11** — mat settle up to 10 minutes while I ate dinner.
- **Day 12** — proofing the door with visitors. Two small jumps, then sat.
- **Day 13** — transfer prep: wrote Walt a one-page cheat sheet.
- **Day 14** — went home. Transfer session with Walt and his daughter: door, stairs, short walk.

## After
- 2026-11-05 — follow-up private at Walt's house. Duke good at the door, forgot stairs once. Walt is
  using the waist leash we picked out. Invoiced separately.
- Daughter: Renee Hendricks, handles the payments. Reminder that both invoices are still open.

## Things to remember
- Walt can't bend down easily: teach a hand target instead of a treat-to-floor lure.
- Duke is food obsessed, use his kibble for most of the training; Walt can't afford fancy treats.
- Walt calls on the landline, not the cell.
