# The Calm Pager

On running on-call without burning people out.

- Pages should be actionable; everything else is a ticket.
- Secondary on-call should be paged automatically after 10 minutes.
- Track pages per week per person; above 5 means the rota is broken.

Ideas to try: automatic secondary paging (we got bitten by this in September).
