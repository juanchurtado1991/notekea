# Ledgers at Scale

The best book I have read on double-entry systems in software.

- Balances are a cache; entries are the truth.
- Snapshots make reads cheap but must be rebuildable.
- Every money-moving call needs an idempotency key that outlives the longest queue.

Recommended to [[Priya Castell]] for the [[Ledger Migration]].
