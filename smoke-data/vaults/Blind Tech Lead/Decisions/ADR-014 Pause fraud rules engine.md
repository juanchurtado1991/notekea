# ADR-014 Pause fraud rules engine

## Context
Risk team is evaluating Sentinax, an external fraud scoring vendor. If it is bought, most of our rules engine is not
needed.

## Decision
Pause work on the [[Fraud Rules Engine]] until the Sentinax evaluation is finished. Keep shadow mode running.
Revisit on 2027-01-15.

## Consequences
- Omar Haddad moves to Payouts v2 in the meantime.
