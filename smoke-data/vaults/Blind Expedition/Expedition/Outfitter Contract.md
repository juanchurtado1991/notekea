# Outfitter contract — Rhododendron Trail Expeditions

Signed 2026-09-30 by me for the whole team (each member pays their own share).

## What is included
- Airport pick-up and drop-off in Kathmandu.
- Lead guide and assistant guide for the whole trek, two porters for the team.
- All meals on the trek, tea-house lodging below Khare, tents at Mera High Camp.
- Group climbing gear: fixed ropes, tents for high camp, stove and fuel.
- Booking of the Lukla flights (paid by us separately, see [[Budget]]).

## What is not included
- International flights, the Nepal visa and the park and municipality fees (see [[Permits and Fees]]).
- Personal gear, travel insurance with helicopter rescue (mandatory, proof by 2027-03-01).
- Tips. They suggest a pooled tip at the end of the trek.
- Extra nights in Kathmandu after the planned dates.

## Payment
- Package price per person: 2950 USD.
- Deposit of 900 USD per person on signing — mine is paid.
- Balance of 2050 USD per person due 2027-02-15. Late payment means the Lukla seats are released.

## Cancellation
- More than 90 days before arrival: deposit kept, nothing else owed.
- 90 to 30 days before arrival: half of the package price.
- Less than 30 days: full package price.
- If the outfitter cancels for safety (strike, earthquake, closure of the park) we get a full refund or a new
  date in the same or the next season.

## Our duties
- Send every team member's medical form and a passport copy before 2027-01-15.
- Follow the guide's turnaround decision on summit day. The guide may turn any one of us back if they show
  signs of altitude illness; that is not a reason for a refund.
- Bring the gear on their required list: double boots, crampons, ice axe, harness, a sleeping bag rated to
  at least -20°C, and a down jacket.

## Notes from the call (2026-09-28)
- Pemba has summited Mera more than forty times.
- They prefer the Paiya approach for first-timers; the direct route over the Zatrwa La straight from Lukla is
  too fast for acclimatization going in.
- Group size max 6, so we have room for two more if anyone wants to join.
