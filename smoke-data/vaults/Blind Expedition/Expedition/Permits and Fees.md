# Permits and fees

My own permits (each team member pays their own).

| Item | Fee (USD) | Status | Paid on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mera Peak climbing permit (spring) | 250 | paid | 2026-10-02 |
| Makalu Barun National Park entry | 35 | unpaid | |
| Rural municipality fee | 20 | unpaid | |
| TIMS card | 15 | unpaid | |
| Nepal visa on arrival (30 days) | 50 | unpaid | |
| Garbage deposit (refundable) | 500 | paid | 2026-10-02 |

- The garbage deposit comes back after the trip if we bring our trash down. Not a real cost, so it is not in the
  expedition [[Budget]].
- Park entry, municipality fee and TIMS are paid in Kathmandu through the outfitter on Day 2.
- Visa: cash at the airport, bring one passport photo.
