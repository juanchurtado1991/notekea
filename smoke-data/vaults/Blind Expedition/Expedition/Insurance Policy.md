## 2. Who is covered
The insured person named on the certificate: Dana Whitfield, born 1988, resident in Colorado, USA. Cover
is for one person only. Travel companions need their own policy even if they travel on the same itinerary.

## 3. Period of cover
From 2027-04-01 00:00 to 2027-04-30 23:59 (local time where the insured person is). If the return is
delayed by something covered (illness, injury, a cancelled flight caused by weather), cover is extended
automatically by up to 10 days at no extra cost.

## 4. Where you are covered
Worldwide, excluding the countries on the insurer's sanctions list at the date of travel. Nepal is covered.

## 5. Emergency medical expenses
We pay reasonable and necessary medical costs following an injury or a sudden illness during the period of
cover, including hospital stays, doctors' fees, prescribed medicine and a hyperbaric chamber treatment.
Dental emergency treatment is limited to 500 USD and only for the relief of sudden pain.

## 6. Search, rescue and evacuation
We pay the cost of search and rescue by recognised rescue services, and of helicopter evacuation to the
nearest suitable hospital, when the insured person's life or health is at risk. Before any helicopter
flight that is not life-threatening, the assistance service must authorise it. If there is no way to call
first (no signal, unconscious), call as soon as possible afterwards.

## 7. Pre-existing medical conditions
Conditions you have been treated for in the last 12 months must be declared. Declared: none. Allergy to
penicillin noted on the application (not a condition, no extra premium).

## 8. Sports and activities
Covered without extra premium: trekking on marked or guided routes, glacier travel roped up with a guide,
use of crampons and ice axe, fixed-rope ascent on a guided trekking peak. Not covered: solo climbing on
glaciers, ski touring, paragliding, rock climbing graded above 5.9 without a guide.

## 9. Altitude
Activities are covered up to 6500 m above sea level. Above that height there is no cover for any section of
this policy, including rescue. The altitude limit applies to the highest point reached on the day of the
incident, not to where the incident happens.

## 10. General exclusions
- Being under the influence of alcohol or drugs not prescribed by a doctor.
- Ignoring the advice of a guide or a doctor, including continuing to ascend with signs of altitude illness.
- Travelling against the advice of the government of your country of residence.
- War, terrorism, nuclear risks.
- Any claim where the insured person cannot show a receipt or a medical report.

## 11. Making a claim
Send the claim form within 30 days of coming home, with original receipts, the medical report and, for a
rescue, the rescue company's invoice. Claims are paid in USD to a US bank account.
