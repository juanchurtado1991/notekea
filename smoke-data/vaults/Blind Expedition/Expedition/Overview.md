# Mera Peak 2027 — overview

**Goal:** summit Mera Peak (6476 m), a trekking peak in the Hinku valley, Nepal.

- Leave Denver: 2027-04-02 (see [[Flights]])
- Arrive Kathmandu: 2027-04-04
- Summit day (planned): 2027-04-16, with one reserve day after it
- Home again: 2027-04-24

The route goes in over the Zatrwa La the slow way (via Paiya and Panggom) so we gain height gently, then
up the Hinku to Khare and Mera High Camp. Full plan in [[Itinerary]].

Outfitter: Rhododendron Trail Expeditions — lead guide [[Pemba Dorje]]. Contract in [[Outfitter Contract]].

Team: see [[Team]]. Money: see the expedition [[Budget]].

## Why Mera
- Non-technical glacier climb, crampons + ice axe, short fixed-rope section at the top.
- Highest thing I have stood on so far is Mount Elbert (4401 m). This is a big jump; acclimatization is everything.
