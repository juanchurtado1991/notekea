# Prescriptions for the trip

- **Acetazolamide (Diamox)** 125 mg twice a day, from Day 7 of the [[Itinerary]] until we start going down.
  Tingling fingers are a normal side effect.
- **Dexamethasone** 4 mg — emergency only, for HACE on the way down, guide or Alex Kowalski decides.
- **Azithromycin** 500 mg once a day for 3 days — traveller's diarrhoea.
- Allergy: penicillin (rash). Tell the guide.

Trial run of Diamox at home: two days in December, to check side effects.
