# Nephrology consults (block 4)

Attending: [[Grace Kim]]. Fellow: rotating each week.

## What I learned
- Urine studies before fluids whenever possible (urine sodium, urine osm).
- Correct sodium slowly: no more than 8 mEq/L in 24 hours if chronic.
- Think about thiazides in every older patient with low sodium.
- Dialysis indications: AEIOU, but call early for refractory hyperkalemia.
