## Rounding expectations
- Present in the order: overnight events, exam, data, then assessment and plan.
- Every patient on pressors needs a stated MAP goal and a weaning plan.
- Lines and tubes: say the day count and whether we still need them.
- Daniel Kim wants one clear plan per problem, not a list of options.

## Protocols worth knowing
### STEMI pathway
- ECG within 10 minutes of arrival. Activation goes through the page operator.
- Aspirin load, heparin per protocol, ticagrelor only after the cath lab says so.
- Door-to-balloon target is 90 minutes. The cath lab team is on call from home after 17:00.

### Cardiogenic shock
- Early lactate, early echo, early call to the shock team.
- Norepinephrine first. Dobutamine if the cardiac output is low and the pressure allows it.
- Mechanical support decisions belong to the shock team, but the resident writes the note.

### Drips I titrate myself
- Heparin (nomogram on the unit intranet page)
- Amiodarone (load, then maintenance)
- Nitroglycerin for flash pulmonary edema
- Insulin drip only with the endocrine order set

## Pearls from the block
- Aortic stenosis plus syncope means admit and echo, always.
- New left bundle with symptoms: treat like a STEMI equivalent until proven otherwise.
- Check potassium and magnesium twice a day on every patient with arrhythmia.
- When the family meeting is hard, ask the nurse who knows the family best to come.
- Endocarditis patients: three sets of cultures before antibiotics if they are stable.
