# Abstract - Thiazide hyponatremia

**Title:** Timing of severe hyponatremia after starting a thiazide in older inpatients

**Authors:** Nora Castellano, [[Priya Raman]], [[Grace Kim]] (senior author and mentor)

**Format:** poster. Session and travel details are in [[Conference trip]].

## Abstract (as submitted)
**Background.** Thiazides are a common cause of low sodium in older adults, but the timing of the
drop after the first dose is not well described in hospitalized patients.

**Methods.** Single-center chart review of adults aged 65 and over admitted with a sodium below
125 while on a thiazide. Time from the first dose to admission was taken from pharmacy records.

**Results.** Most patients were admitted within the first month of starting the drug. Women and
patients with a low body weight were over-represented. Recurrence was common when the thiazide
was restarted.

**Conclusion.** The first weeks after a thiazide is started are the period of highest risk;
a sodium check at two weeks could catch most cases.
