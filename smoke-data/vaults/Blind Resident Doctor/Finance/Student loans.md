## The plan
- Federal loans stay federal. Refinancing them would kill PSLF, and Fenmore is a nonprofit
  employer, so every month of residency can count.
- The private loan is the only one I pay down on purpose. It is small and not eligible for any
  forgiveness program.
- The income-driven payment is recalculated every year from last year's tax return. With a
  resident salary it stays low: $287 a month right now, covering both federal loans together.
- Never pay extra on the federal loans while going for PSLF. Extra money goes to the emergency
  fund and the Roth IRA first.

## Servicer notes
- Federal servicer: online account, autopay on the 5th of the month.
- Private lender: autopay on the 15th, gives a small rate discount for autopay.
- Annual recertification of income: due every August. Calendar reminder set for 2027-07-15.
- Keep a PDF of every employment certification form in the loans folder of the drive.

## Things I keep forgetting
- Interest on the federal loans is not paid off by the income-driven payment, so the balance
  can grow. That is fine under PSLF as long as the payments qualify.
- A forbearance month does not count toward PSLF. Do not accept one, even if the servicer
  offers it during a processing delay.
- Consolidation would reset the count for some loans. Do not consolidate.
