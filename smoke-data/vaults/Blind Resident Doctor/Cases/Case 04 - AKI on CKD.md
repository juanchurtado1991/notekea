# Case 04 - AKI on CKD

Anonymised. Woman with CKD stage 3b, creatinine doubled after a course of NSAIDs for back pain
plus a new ACE inhibitor.

- Stopped NSAIDs and the ACE inhibitor, gentle fluids
- Creatinine back near baseline in five days
- Teaching point: the "triple whammy" (NSAID, ACE inhibitor, diuretic)

Presented at morning report: 2026-10-02.
