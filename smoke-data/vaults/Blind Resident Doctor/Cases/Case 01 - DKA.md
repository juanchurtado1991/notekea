# Case 01 - DKA

Anonymised. Young man, type 1 diabetes, missed insulin for three days after losing his job.

- Presentation: vomiting, Kussmaul breathing, glucose above 500, anion gap 28
- Management: fluids, insulin drip, potassium repletion before the drip ran fast
- Pitfall: closed the gap but bicarbonate still low, overlap with subcutaneous insulin too early
- Teaching point: start long-acting insulin two hours before stopping the drip

Presented at morning report: 2026-10-14.
