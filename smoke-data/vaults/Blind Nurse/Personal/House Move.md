# House move

Leaving the rented flat in Levenshulme for a two-bed terrace on Ashfield Road, Chorlton. First place
I've owned. Linked notes: [[Move Checklist]], [[Removal Quotes]], [[Annual Leave]].

## The house

- Two bedrooms, one bathroom upstairs, through lounge, kitchen at the back, small yard.
- Agreed price £238,000. Offer accepted on 2026-08-29 after one counter-offer.
- About twenty minutes to the hospital by car, and there's a bus that stops at the end of the road for
  the days the car park is full.
- Gas central heating, boiler about eight years old. Windows are fine at the front, the back bedroom
  ones will need doing in a year or two.

## Money

- Deposit from the house fund plus the help-to-save account.
- Mortgage: 25 years, fixed for five years. Arrangement fee added to the loan.
- Solicitor: Hartley & Moss, conveyancing fee £1,180 + VAT, searches extra.
- Stamp duty: none, first-time buyer under the threshold.
- Need to keep a buffer for curtains, a bed for the spare room and the first month of bills twice
  over (flat and house overlap).

## Survey

Level 2 survey done on 2026-10-03. Mostly fine: some damp at the bottom of the back bedroom wall
(probably the gutter), the loft insulation is thin, and the fence at the back leans. Nothing that
changed the price. The surveyor said to get the gutter fixed before winter.
