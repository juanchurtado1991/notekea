# Critical Care Module

Level 6 module "Nursing the Critically Ill Adult" at Pennine University, part-time, September 2026 to
January 2027. Fees are paid from the CPD fund (see [[Courses/Budget]]); travel and parking are on me.
Module lead: Dr H. Sandford. Clinical mentor on the unit: [[Priya Nair]].

## Why I'm doing it

I have been on ICU 2 for six years and most of what I know I learned at the bedside. The module is the
first step towards the senior staff nurse post Priya mentioned at my last appraisal, and it counts
towards my revalidation hours. It also gives me protected time to read properly about the things I do
every shift without thinking: sedation holds, fluid balance, weaning, and talking to families.

## Teaching days

Teaching is on Thursdays, 09:30–16:00, on the city campus, every other week. I have asked for the
Thursdays off on the roster where I can, and swapped the rest. The sessions so far:

- Week 1 — Introduction, the deteriorating patient and early warning scores.
- Week 3 — Respiratory failure, modes of ventilation and weaning. Chris Doyle lent me his notes from
  the ventilation teaching on the unit, which helped a lot.
- Week 5 — Shock states and vasoactive drugs. Lots of overlap with what we see in sepsis.
- Week 7 — Renal replacement therapy and fluid balance. Good to finally understand the filter
  pressures instead of just reacting to the alarms.
- Week 9 — Neurological assessment, sedation and delirium. Linked to the delirium study day.
- Week 11 — Ethics, end of life care and family communication.

## Reading

The reading list is long. I'm trying to read one article a week on nights when the unit is settled,
and I keep short notes for each one. So far the articles on delirium screening and early mobility have
been the most useful; the ones on nutrition I still need to finish. The library will post books to the
hospital if I ask, which saves a trip into town.

## Assessment

There are two parts:

1. Assignment 1 — a reflective account of an episode of care, 1500 words. Submitted on 2026-10-16,
   passed with 64.
2. Assignment 2 — a case study of a patient I have cared for, fully anonymised, linking the care to
   the evidence.

Feedback from assignment 1: good reflection, but I need to use more primary research rather than
guidelines, and to be more critical about the evidence instead of only describing it. My tutor
suggested building the case study around one clear problem rather than the whole admission.

## Ideas for the case study

- A patient on CRRT with sepsis — lots of physiology to discuss, and I've nursed several.
- Weaning a long-stay patient from the ventilator — good link to week 3.
- Delirium in an older patient after surgery — links to the study day and the reading.

I'm leaning towards delirium. Need to clear the choice with the module lead at the next tutorial and
make sure nothing in it could identify the patient or the unit.

## Practical bits

## Deadline

Assignment 2 (case study) is 3000 words, due on 2027-01-08 at 12:00 through the online submission
portal. Late submissions are capped at a bare pass, so book annual leave or swap shifts around the
first week of January to finish it.
