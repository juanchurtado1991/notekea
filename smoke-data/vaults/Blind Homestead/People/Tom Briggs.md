# Tom Briggs

Hay farmer on Briggs Hollow, about six miles out. Square bales of orchard grass and some clover mix.

- $8 a bale, stacked in the loft if Eli is here to help; $10 delivery on loads under 30 bales
- Prefers cash or a check, pays no attention to invoices
