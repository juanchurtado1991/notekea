# Plantings 2026

Everything that went in the ground this year, bed by bed. The garden is twelve raised beds (4 x 12 ft)
inside the deer fence plus the long row along the south fence. Beds are numbered from the gate.
Harvests are in [[Harvest Log 2026]].

## Spring

### Bed 1 - peas and then beans
- 2026-03-01: sugar snap peas, two rows on the cattle panel trellis. Soaked the seed overnight.
- 2026-03-22: second sowing of peas where the first row had gaps (mice, probably).
- 2026-06-08: pulled the peas, sowed bush beans 'Provider' in the same bed.
- Beans did fine until the Japanese beetles in July. Hand-picked into soapy water every morning.

### Bed 2 - lettuce and greens
- 2026-03-05: lettuce mix, spinach and arugula under row cover.
- 2026-03-28: transplanted 12 lettuce starts from the shelf in the mudroom.
- Bolted in early June with the heat. Chickens got the bolted lettuce.
- 2026-09-02: fall sowing of spinach, kale and lettuce mix.

### Bed 3 - onions
- 2026-03-10: 200 onion sets, yellow and red. Mulched with straw.
- Weeded twice in April, once in May.
- Pulled 2026-07-04, cured in the barn loft for three weeks.

### Bed 4 - potatoes
- 2026-03-17: seed potatoes, 'Kennebec' and a red variety from the feed store, 10 lb total.
- Hilled twice. Colorado potato beetles showed up in May; picked by hand.
- Dug 2026-07-20.

## Summer

### Bed 5 - tomatoes
- 2026-04-25: 14 tomato plants. Six slicers, four paste, four cherry.
- Florida weave between T-posts. Pruned suckers on the slicers only.
- Early blight on the lower leaves by July; stripped the bottom leaves and mulched.
- Last ones picked green before the frost and ripened on the porch.

### Bed 6 - peppers and okra
- 2026-05-03: 8 sweet peppers, 4 jalapenos, and a row of okra seed.
- The okra was the star of August. Picked every other day.

### Bed 7 - squash
- 2026-05-10: zucchini (2 hills), yellow squash (2 hills).
- Squash vine borers took the zucchini in July, as every year.
- 2026-07-18: replanted with a short row of cucumbers. Pickled six quarts.

### Bed 8 - herbs
- Perennial bed: thyme, oregano, sage, chives. Basil and dill sown 2026-05-01.
- Cut back the oregano twice, dried it on screens in the loft.

### South fence row - winter squash and pumpkins
- 2026-05-20: butternut, a few acorn squash, and six pumpkin hills for the October markets.
- Vines ran out into the pasture; goats ate a few leaves through the fence.
- Pumpkins picked 2026-09-28, cured in the barn.

### Bed 10 - sweet potatoes
- 2026-05-24: 30 slips, 'Covington', from the feed store.
- Black plastic mulch to warm the soil.
- Dug 2026-10-08 before the first frost; curing in the warm corner of the mudroom.

### Bed 11 - flowers
- Zinnias and sunflowers along the end for the bees and for market bouquets.
- Sunflower heads hung in the coop for the hens in September.

### Bed 12 - strawberries
- Second-year bed. Runners trimmed in August, new straw mulch in November.

## Fall

### Bed 3 again - fall brassicas
- 2026-08-15: cabbage and broccoli transplants into the old onion bed.
- Cabbage worms; Bt sprayed twice.

### Bed 7 again - garlic
- After the cucumbers came out, the bed got compost and the garlic. Details in [[Fall Garlic]].

### Bed 9 - the new bed by the well house
- Built in September from the leftover cedar boards. Filled with half topsoil, half compost from
  pile 1 (see [[Compost]]).
- The county extension soil test came back on 2026-10-21: pH 5.8, low in calcium, phosphorus fine,
  potassium medium. Too sour for the asparagus planned there.
- 2026-10-30: spread lime and dug it in. Retest in spring before planting the asparagus crowns.
- Cover crop of crimson clover sown 2026-11-01 to hold the soil over winter.

## Notes for next year
- Start tomatoes a week later; the April cold snap set them back.
- Try a borer-resistant squash (moschata types) instead of zucchini.
- More okra. Less yellow squash.
- Keep the goats farther from the south fence row.
