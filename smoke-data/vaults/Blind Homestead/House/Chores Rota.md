# Chores Rota

Who does what on the homestead. It's mostly me (Wren); [[Eli Pruitt]] helps on weekday afternoons
and [[June Okafor]] trades help for milk.

## Daily
| Time | Chore | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 6:00 | Morning milking, three does | Wren |
| 6:30 | Let hens out, fresh water, pellets | Wren |
| 7:00 | Goat hay and water, check the buck pen | Wren |
| 15:30 | Egg collection, top up water | Eli (weekdays), Wren (weekends) |
| 16:00 | Muck out the milking area | Eli (weekdays), Wren (weekends) |
| 18:00 | Evening milking | Wren |
| Dusk | Shut the coop, count hens | Wren |

## Weekly
| Day | Chore | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Scrub and refill all waterers | Eli |
| Tuesday | Clean the milk stand, wash the strainer and jars | Wren |
| Wednesday | Fresh bedding in the goat stalls | Eli |
| Thursday | June's herd-share pickup, check the fence line | Wren |
| Friday | Coop litter top-up, collect the compost bucket | Eli |
| Saturday | Market (every other week) or garden work | Wren |
| Sunday | Rest, as much as goats allow | everyone |

## Monthly
- First Saturday: deep clean the coop, old litter to compost pile 3
- Mid month: trim hooves on whoever needs it (Daisys, Clover, Rufus)
- Last week: inventory feed, write the order for Millbrook Feed & Seed
- Check the well house heat lamp from November to March

## Seasonal
- Spring: kidding watch, garden beds, fence repairs after the winter
- Summer: fly control, garden every morning before it gets hot, shade cloth on the goat run
- Fall: stack hay, clean the gutters on the barn, put the water heaters in, plant the garlic
- Winter: heat lamps and heated buckets, keep paths shoveled, seed orders

## Pay and trades
- Eli: $12 an hour, paid on the first of the month for the month before
- June: a gallon of milk a week as her herd share, plus she covers chores when I travel

## Rules
- Anyone who goes in with the goats latches both gates behind them.
- The buck pen is Wren only. Rufus is gentle but strong.
- Eggs go straight to the fridge in the mudroom, dirty ones in the bucket to wash.
- Write the egg count on the coop clipboard every time.
- If a goat isn't eating or is off by herself, call Wren right away.

## Changes for the holidays
- Thanksgiving week (Nov 26-29): Eli is away with his family. June Okafor covers his afternoon
  chores. Wren does the Monday and Wednesday chores herself.
- Christmas: Eli wants Dec 24-26 off; not decided who covers yet.
