# Flock

Coop built in spring 2024 from the old corn crib. Run is 20 x 30 ft with hardware cloth buried a foot
down after the raccoon year.

## Hens
| Breed | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Buff Orpington | 6 | broody in summer |
| Barred Rock | 4 | best layers in winter |
| Easter Egger | 3 | blue and green eggs |
| Australorp | 1 | named Ink |

That makes 14 hens, plus the rooster, Admiral.

## Routine
- Let out at first light, shut in at dusk
- Layer pellets free choice, oyster shell on the side, scratch only in the afternoon
- Water heater goes in when nights drop below freezing
- Eggs collected twice a day, logged in the egg log for the month

## Molt
Most of the Orpingtons started molting mid October. Laying always drops in November.
