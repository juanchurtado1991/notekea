# Client Contract

Peachlight Studio — Photography Services Agreement (template, version 2026.2). Every client signs this; the
client's name, event, date, package and fee go in the blanks of section 1.

## 1. Parties and event
This agreement is between Peachlight Studio, owned by Dana Whitfield ("Photographer"), and the person named on the
signature page ("Client"), for photography of the event, on the date and at the venue named there ("Event").

## 2. Services
Photographer will provide the coverage of the package chosen by Client, as described in the current rate sheet
(see [[Business/Rates|Rates]]). Coverage hours are continuous. Any break in coverage requested by Client counts as
coverage time.

## 3. Retainer and payment
A retainer of thirty percent (30%) of the total fee is due when this agreement is signed. The date is not held until
the retainer is received. The retainer is non-refundable and is applied to the total fee. The balance is due on the
Event date unless a different due date is written on the signature page. Payments later than seven (7) days after
their due date carry a late fee of $35.

## 4. Overtime
If Client asks Photographer to stay past the contracted hours, overtime is billed at $250 per hour or part of an hour,
and is invoiced after the Event. Overtime must be requested by Client or by the person Client names as day-of
contact, not by guests.

## 5. Second photographer and assistants
Packages that include a second photographer or assistant are staffed by Photographer's chosen team. Photographer may
replace a team member with a person of equal skill if needed.

## 6. Client responsibilities
Client will give Photographer a timeline and a list of required group photos at least fourteen (14) days before the
Event, and will name a day-of contact who can point out family members. Photographer is not responsible for missed
photos caused by the timeline running late, by venue restrictions, or by guests blocking the view.

## 7. Venue rules
Client will make sure the venue allows professional photography and will tell Photographer about any restrictions
(flash, ceremony positions, drones). Photographer will follow the venue's rules and the officiant's requests.

## 8. Meals and breaks
For coverage of six (6) hours or more, Client will provide a hot meal for Photographer and each team member, served
at the same time as guests, so the team is ready again for toasts and dances.

## 9. Travel
Travel inside I-285 is included. Travel outside I-285 is billed per mile as listed on the rate sheet. Events more
than 120 miles from Atlanta require lodging for the night before, paid by Client.

## 10. Postponement
If Client postpones the Event, the retainer moves to the new date once, provided Photographer is available and the
new date is within twelve (12) months of the original. A second postponement counts as a cancellation.

## 11. Cancellation
If Client cancels:
- more than 180 days before the Event, Photographer keeps the retainer only;
- between 180 and 61 days before the Event, fifty percent (50%) of the total fee is due;
- sixty (60) days or fewer before the Event, one hundred percent (100%) of the total fee is due.
If Photographer must cancel because of illness or emergency, Photographer will try to send a replacement of equal
skill; if none is available, all payments are refunded in full, and that is Photographer's whole liability.

## 12. Image delivery
Photographer will deliver an online gallery of edited images within forty-two (42) days after the Event. The number
of images varies with the Event; Photographer chooses and edits them in Photographer's own style. Unedited images
(raw files) are not delivered and are not for sale.

## 13. Copyright and use
Photographer keeps the copyright of all images. Client receives a personal-use license to print and share the
images. Commercial use needs a written license. Photographer may use images for the portfolio, website and social
media unless Client opts out in writing on the signature page.

## 14. Gallery archive
The online gallery stays available for 18 months after delivery. After that it is archived. Client can
reactivate an archived gallery for a fee of $75 for another six (6) months, while Photographer still keeps the files.
Photographer keeps backups of delivered images for at least three (3) years but does not guarantee it.

## 15. Limit of liability
If images are lost or damaged because of equipment failure or other cause beyond Photographer's control,
Photographer's liability is limited to a refund of the fees paid for the affected coverage.

## 16. Whole agreement
This is the whole agreement. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Georgia law applies.
