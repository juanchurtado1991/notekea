# Shoot Day Checklist

The night before:
- [ ] Charge every battery (bodies and speedlights)
- [ ] Format cards in camera, label them A1–A4, B1–B4
- [ ] Print the timeline and the shot list
- [ ] Check the forecast and the sunset time
- [ ] Text the second shooter the call time and parking

On the day:
- [ ] Arrive 30 minutes early
- [ ] Sync camera clocks with the second shooter
- [ ] Back up cards to the laptop before leaving the venue

See also [[Packing List]].
