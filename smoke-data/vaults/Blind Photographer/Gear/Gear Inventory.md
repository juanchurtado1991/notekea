## Bodies
Body A does almost everything now. Body B is the backup and the second body on wedding days, with the 70-200 on it
when the lens is home. Body C went to a student in June; it was my first paid-work camera and I kept it far too long.
The crop sensor was useful for reach at school events, but I do not do those anymore.

## Lenses
The 24-70 lives on Body A. The 35 is for getting-ready rooms and dance floors when it gets tight. The 70-200 is the
ceremony lens; while it is in the shop I rent one for weddings (rentals are covered by the equipment policy). The 50
was sold in September — the 35 and the 85 cover what it did.

The 85mm f/1.2 was the big buy of 2025: bought in June for portraits, $2,299 after a $200 instant rebate, from the
camera shop on Ponce. It is the lens clients notice in the gallery.

## Lighting
Two speedlights for on-camera bounce, the strobe kit for The Foundry Loft and anywhere else with a black ceiling.
Stands and modifiers are cheap but they add up; they live in the rolling bag.

## Computers
The editing laptop is the newest thing here. Note for myself, because I keep forgetting when I renew things: the
laptop is NOT on the equipment policy schedule — it is covered by my renter's policy at home, which has a $500
deductible. If it is stolen from a venue, the claim goes to the renter's policy, not to the equipment insurer.
