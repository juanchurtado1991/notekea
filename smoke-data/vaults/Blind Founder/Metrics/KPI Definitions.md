# KPI Definitions

- **MRR**: monthly recurring subscription revenue at month end; annual contracts divided by 12.
- **MRR churn**: MRR lost to cancellations and downgrades in the month, divided by MRR at the start
  of the month.
- **Logo churn**: customers lost in the month.
- **NRR**: MRR today from customers we had 12 months ago, divided by their MRR back then.
- **NPS**: survey to dock managers every quarter. First survey planned for January 2027.
- **Detention hours saved**: truck waiting hours before Haulwise minus after, per site per week.
