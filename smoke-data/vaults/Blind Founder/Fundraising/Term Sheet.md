# Term Sheet (Caliche Partners)

Summary of the non-binding term sheet from Caliche Partners for the Haulwise Series Seed Preferred.
Copied section by section from the PDF so I can comment inline. Counsel: see [[Legal]].

## Offering terms
- **Issuer:** Haulwise, Inc., a Delaware corporation.
- **Security:** Series Seed Preferred Stock.
- **Amount:** up to $3,000,000 in total, of which Caliche Partners invests $1,500,000. The rest
  comes from other investors approved by the company (see [[Fundraising/Pipeline]]).
- **Pre-money valuation:** $12,000,000, which includes the unallocated option pool described below
  and the conversion of all outstanding SAFEs.
- **Closing:** one or more closings; the first as soon as practicable after signing, the final
  closing no later than 90 days after the first.

> My note: 90 days gives us room for Mesquite if their diligence drags.

## Option pool
- An unallocated option pool equal to at least 10% of the post-money fully diluted capitalisation,
  created before the closing and counted in the pre-money.

> My note: the usual pool shuffle. It comes out of our side. Taken to the board.

## Liquidation preference
- 1x non-participating. On a sale, holders of Series Seed get the greater of their original
  purchase price plus declared but unpaid dividends, or what they would get as common.

## Dividends
- 6% non-cumulative, only when and if declared by the board. Nothing accrues.

## Conversion
- Convertible at any time into common at the holder's option, initially one to one. Automatic
  conversion on an IPO or with the consent of a majority of the Series Seed.

## Anti-dilution
- Broad-based weighted average. Usual carve-outs (options under the plan, conversions, equipment
  lenders).

> My note: fine. Full ratchet would have been a deal breaker.

## Voting and protective provisions
- Series Seed votes with common on an as-converted basis.
- Consent of a majority of the Series Seed is needed to: change the rights of the Series Seed,
  create a senior or pari passu security, grow the option pool beyond what is approved at closing,
  pay dividends, sell the company, or change the size of the board.

## Board of directors
- Three seats at closing: two elected by the common (Dana Whitlock and Marcus Lee) and one elected
  by the Series Seed (Owen Pratt).
- The board meets at least quarterly. Observers by mutual agreement only.

## Information rights
- Major investors (holding at least $250,000 of Series Seed) get annual unaudited financials within
  120 days of year end, quarterly financials within 60 days of quarter end, and the annual budget.

## Pro rata rights
- Major investors may buy their pro rata share of future equity rounds. The right ends at an IPO or
  a sale of the company.

## Founder matters
- Existing founder vesting stays: four years with a one-year cliff from 2025-02-01; double-trigger
  acceleration of 25% on a change of control plus termination.
- Founders and employees sign invention assignment agreements.

## Drag-along
- Founders and major investors agree to vote for a sale approved by the board, a majority of the
  Series Seed and a majority of the common.

## Expenses
- The company pays Caliche's reasonable legal fees at closing, capped at $25,000.

> My note: ask to bring the cap down to $15,000. Our own legal spend is already high
> (see [[Burn and Runway]]).

## Conditions to closing
- Satisfactory legal and financial due diligence.
- Option pool created as above.
- Customary closing documents: stock purchase agreement, investor rights agreement, voting
  agreement, right of first refusal and co-sale agreement.

## Confidentiality
- Terms are confidential except to the company's officers, directors, counsel, and current and
  prospective investors in this round.

## No-shop
- For 45 days from signing, the company will not solicit, encourage or accept any offer for the
  sale of its equity from anyone other than Caliche Partners and the investors in this round, nor
  any offer to buy the company.

> My note: we can still close the other names in the pipeline. They count as investors in this
> round. Confirmed with counsel.

## Expiration
- This term sheet expires if not signed by both parties by 2026-11-20.

## Governing law
- Delaware. Non-binding except for Confidentiality, No-shop, Expenses and Governing law.
