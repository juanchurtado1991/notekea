# Hiring Plan

Plan for the 12 months after the seed. Approved in principle at the [[Board Meeting 2026-08-20]];
the option pool for it was approved at the [[Board Meeting 2026-10-15]].

## Principles
- Hire for the next 12 months of work, not the next three years.
- Every role has a written scorecard before the first interview.
- Two steps: one paid work sample, then a half-day onsite with Dana and Marcus.
- Offers include equity from the expanded pool; four-year vesting with a one-year cliff.
- No hires ahead of the closing except the first Account Executive.

## Order of hiring
1. Account Executive #1: now (offer accepted, [[Sofia Reyes]])
2. Senior Backend Engineer: right after closing
3. Account Executive #2: Q1 2027
4. Customer Success Manager: target start 2027-02-01
5. Data engineer: contractor for now, convert in H2 2027

## Account Executive (two roles)
- Owns mid-size distributor deals from first call to signature.
- Background: sold software to logistics or warehousing buyers; knows what detention costs.
- Compensation: $80,000 base / $160,000 OTE, uncapped commission above quota.
- Quota: $360,000 new ARR per year once ramped (four-month ramp).
- Scorecard: pipeline built in the first 90 days, win rate, forecast accuracy.

## Customer Success Manager
- Onboards new sites (target: live in 14 days) and runs QBRs.
- Owns renewals together with the AE; flags churn risk every month.
- Compensation: $85,000 to $95,000 base plus a renewal bonus.
- Process: phone screen, work sample (an onboarding plan for a made-up distributor), onsite.

## Data engineer (contractor)
- Builds the yard analytics and the monthly customer reports.
- 20 hours a week on contract today; it is in the contractors line of [[Burn and Runway]].

## Interview loop (all roles)
| Step | Who | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Phone screen | Dana | 30 min |
| Work sample | Hiring manager | 3 hours, paid |
| Onsite | Dana and Marcus | half day |
| References | Dana | 2 calls |

## Where we find people
- Austin logistics meetup, the founders dinner, referrals from Priya.
- For the backend role: people who built carrier integrations at brokers or 3PLs.
- No agencies before the round closes.

## Senior Backend Engineer
- Owns the EDI layer and the WMS connectors, starting from Marcus's spec.
- Must have: production experience with EDI (204, 214, 990), Kotlin or Go, Postgres.
- Nice to have: built carrier or WMS integrations before.
- Compensation: $165,000 to $185,000 base, plus equity from the expanded pool.
- Start: as soon as the round closes. Candidates in [[Candidates]].

## Budget
