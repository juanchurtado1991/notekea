# Mile High Throwdown 2026

Our third in-house competition, held at the gym on Saturday 2026-12-05. Open to members and athletes from other
Denver boxes. Money side: [[Events/Budget|event budget]] and [[Sponsors]]. Day timing: [[Events/Schedule|event day
schedule]]. People: [[Volunteers]].

## Why we run it
The Throwdown is the one day a year when the whole community is in the building at once. Last year it brought in
nine new members in January and it is the best marketing we do. It should feel like a party with a scoreboard, not
like a qualifier. Keep it friendly, keep it on time, and make sure the Scaled athletes get as much cheering as the
Rx heats.

## Format
- Teams of one (individual), three divisions: Rx, Scaled and Masters 40+.
- Room for 20 athletes per division, 60 in total. The floor cannot take more than five lanes per heat.
- Entry fee $65, which includes a T-shirt and lunch.
- Three workouts across the day plus a final for the top five in each division.

## Workouts (draft)
1. **Event 1 — "Larimer Ladder"**: ascending ladder of thrusters and bar-facing burpees, 10-minute cap.
   Rx 95/65 lb, Scaled 65/45 lb, Masters 75/55 lb.
2. **Event 2 — "Thin Air"**: 2,000 m row for time, then max-effort clean & jerk in 6 minutes.
3. **Event 3 — "Rig Run"**: three rounds of 400 m run, 15 toes-to-bar, 10 wall balls. Scaled does hanging knee
   raises.
4. **Final**: chipper announced at lunch, roughly 8 minutes.

Workout standards get posted on the gym's channel two weeks out. [[Ryan Delgado]] writes the movement standards
video; [[Jess Amari]] checks that every judge has watched it.

## Scoring
- Points per placing (1st = 100, 2nd = 95, then minus 5 per place).
- Ties broken by the tiebreak time written on the scorecard.
- Scores go on the rented scoreboard and into the spreadsheet; the spreadsheet wins if they disagree.
- Score disputes go to the head judge within 15 minutes of the heat ending. After that the score stands.

## Floor and safety
- Five lanes in the main room, warm-up area in the back by the rowers.
- The pull-up rig has to be inspected before the event (see [[Repairs]]).
- EMT on site from 08:00 to the end of the awards. First-aid kit at the judges' table.
- Every athlete who is not a member signs the [[Liability Waiver]] online before the day. No waiver, no heat.
- Chalk bins away from the back exit (fire inspector).

## Spectators
- Free entry for spectators. Space for about 80 people standing on the mezzanine and by the garage door.
- Kids are welcome but stay off the floor.
- Coffee cart from Rocky Ridge Coffee in the parking lot, if they confirm.

## Parking
Parking lot is reserved for athletes and judges. Spectators use the street or the lot behind the bakery on 29th,
which the owner has said is fine on Saturdays after 08:00.

## Prizes
- Cash for the top three in Rx (prize money line in the event budget).
- Gear prizes from Peak Gear Outlet for Scaled and Masters.
- Medals for the top three in every division.

## Athlete day-of logistics
Athletes park in the gym lot and come in through the garage door, not the front office. Athlete check-in opens at
7:15 at the front desk table, where they pick up their wristband, T-shirt and heat sheet. Anyone not checked in 30
minutes before their first heat loses the spot to the waitlist. Lunch is served from 12:00 in the back room.
