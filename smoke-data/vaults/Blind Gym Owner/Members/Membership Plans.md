# Membership Plans

Prices as of September 2026. Billed on the 1st of each month through the gym software.

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Unlimited | $185 / month | Every class, open gym |
| Three per week | $155 / month | Up to 12 classes a month |
| Student | $125 / month | Every class, valid student ID |
| Foundations | $199 one-time | 6 intro sessions over 3 weeks |
| Drop-in | $25 per class | Visitors with experience |
| 10-class punch card | $220 | Expires 6 months after purchase |

## Rules
- Freezes: up to 3 months per year, $15 freeze fee.
- Cancellation needs 30 days notice (see [[Cancellations]]).
- Everyone signs the [[Liability Waiver]] before the first class.
