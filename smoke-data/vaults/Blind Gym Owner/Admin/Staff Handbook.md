# Staff Handbook

For every coach and front-desk helper at Forge & Barbell CrossFit. Read it once a year.

## Our standards
- Every member gets greeted by name. New faces get introduced to two other members before the warm-up.
- Classes start and end on time. The whiteboard is written before the first athlete walks in.
- Coaches coach. Phones stay in the office during class unless you are using the timer app.
- We scale everyone to the intent of the workout, not to their ego.

## Opening and closing
- The first coach of the day opens: alarm off, lights, music, fans, check the bathrooms and the first-aid kit.
- The last coach of the day closes: bars racked, plates stacked by weight, chalk swept, garage door locked, alarm on.
- The door code changes on the first Monday of each quarter; Dana texts the new code.

## Class flow (60 minutes)
1. Welcome, intros, explain the workout — 5 minutes
2. General warm-up — 10 minutes
3. Skill or strength — 15 to 20 minutes
4. Workout — 15 to 20 minutes
5. Cool-down, scores on the board — 5 minutes

## Personal training
- PT sessions are booked in the gym software, never by text only.
- Packages are paid before the first session. If a member has not paid, the session does not happen; tell Dana.
- Coaches get their normal hourly rate for PT hours.

## Pay and hours
- Hours are logged in the software by the end of each week.
- Payroll runs on the 5th of the following month.
- Raises are reviewed every June.

## Safety
- Every coach keeps a current CPR/AED card (see [[Certifications]]). An expired card means you cannot coach
  alone until it is renewed.
- Know where the first-aid kit, the AED and the fire extinguishers are.
- Any injury goes in the incident log the same day, and the member is reminded of the waiver's reporting rule.
- Kids class: two adults in the room at all times.

## Time off and cover
- Ask for time off at least two weeks ahead.
- You find your own cover first, then tell Dana who it is.
- Sick on the day: text Dana and the coach of the next class before 05:00 for morning classes.

## Front desk
- New visitors fill in the waiver on the tablet before they touch a bar.
- Drop-ins show proof of experience at another box or do Foundations first.
- Apparel sales go through the software, never cash in a drawer.
- Lost and found is cleared on the last day of each month and donated.

## Being on time
Coaches must be in the gym 20 minutes before their first class of the day, warmed up and with the whiteboard done.
Being late twice in a month means a talk with Dana.

## Social media
- Ask members before posting a photo of them.
- No posting about injuries or members' bodies, ever.
