# Liability Waiver and Release (version 3)

Reviewed by our attorney in February 2026. Members sign it in the gym software; drop-ins and Throwdown athletes sign
the online copy. Keep the signed copies for seven years.

## 1. Who this covers
This agreement is between Forge & Barbell CrossFit ("the Gym"), its owner, coaches, staff and volunteers, and the
person signing below ("the Participant"). It covers every class, open gym session, personal training session,
seminar, competition and social event run by the Gym, on or off the premises, including runs on public streets
and sidewalks near the building.

## 2. Nature of the activity
The Participant understands that high-intensity functional training includes weightlifting, gymnastics,
running, rowing, jumping, carrying odd objects and other strenuous movements, often performed for time and in a
group. These activities carry real risk. Risks include, but are not limited to: muscle strains and tears, sprains,
broken bones, joint injuries, back injuries, heat illness, dizziness, fainting, rhabdomyolysis, heart attack,
stroke and, in rare cases, death. Risks also come from equipment that is dropped, fails or is used by other people
nearby, and from slipping on chalk, sweat or water.

## 3. Health and fitness
The Participant confirms that they are in good enough health to take part, and that they have talked with a
doctor if they have any heart condition, high blood pressure, recent surgery, pregnancy or other condition that
could make exercise unsafe. The Participant agrees to tell the coach before class about any injury, illness,
medication or limitation that could affect their training. The Gym does not give medical advice. Coaches may
offer scaling options, but the Participant decides how hard to work and may stop at any time.

## 4. Assumption of risk
Knowing the risks above, the Participant freely chooses to take part and accepts all risk of injury, illness,
damage or loss that may happen while training, whether the risk is known now or not, except where the law does not
allow such risk to be waived.

## 5. Release
To the fullest extent the law of Colorado allows, the Participant releases the Gym and everyone named in section 1
from any claims, demands or lawsuits arising from the Participant's training, including claims based on the
ordinary negligence of the Gym. This release does not cover gross negligence or willful and wanton conduct.

## 6. Equipment and facilities
The Participant agrees to use equipment only as instructed, to put weights and bars away after use, to use collars
on loaded barbells, and to report any damaged equipment to a coach right away. The Participant will not use the
pull-up rig, rings or climbing ropes without a coach in the room. Personal belongings are left at the
Participant's own risk; the Gym is not responsible for lost or stolen items.

## 7. Minors
Participants under 18 must have a parent or legal guardian sign this agreement on their behalf. Children aged 8 to
12 may only attend the Kids class. Teens aged 13 to 17 may attend regular classes with a coach present but may not
use open gym alone. The parent or guardian agrees to the release in section 5 on behalf of the minor to the extent
Colorado law allows.

## 8. Photos and video
The Gym may take photos and video during classes and events and use them on its website and social media. A
Participant who does not want to appear may tell the front desk at any time, and the Gym will not post new images
of them.

## 9. Emergency care
If the Participant is injured or becomes ill, the Gym may call emergency services and arrange first aid. The
Participant is responsible for the cost of any medical care or transport.

## 10. Reporting an injury
Any injury that happens at the Gym or at a Gym event must be reported to a coach or the owner, in writing or in the
gym software, within 48 hours of it happening, even if it seems minor at first. The Gym keeps an incident log and
may share it with its insurer.

## 11. Membership terms
Billing, freezes and cancellations follow the [[Membership Plans]] page in force at the time. Signing this waiver
does not by itself start a membership.

## 12. Term
This agreement stays in effect for as long as the Participant trains at the Gym, and for any later return, unless
the Gym asks the Participant to sign a newer version.

## 13. Disputes
Any dispute will first go to mediation in Denver. If mediation fails, it will be heard in the courts of the City
and County of Denver, under Colorado law.

## 14. Severability
If any part of this agreement is found unenforceable, the rest stays in force.

## 15. Signature
By signing, the Participant confirms they have read this agreement, understood it, and sign it freely.
