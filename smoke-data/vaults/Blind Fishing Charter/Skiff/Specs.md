# Little Ledger specs

- 18 ft flats skiff, hull built 2021.
- 150 hp outboard, gasoline. Trolling motor with two batteries.
- Poling platform and a 20 ft push pole.
- Kept on the lift at the house, trailered to the ramp for Florida Bay trips.
- Max 2 anglers plus me.
