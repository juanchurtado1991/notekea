# Deposit policy

What I tell every client when they book:

1. A deposit holds the date. Amounts are listed on each month's booking note.
2. If **I** cancel for weather (my call, made the evening before or at the dock), the client gets the full deposit back or rebooks with it, their choice.
3. If the **client** cancels 7 or more days before the trip, the deposit is refunded minus a $25 processing fee.
4. If the client cancels less than 7 days before, the deposit is kept. I may let them use it on a new date within 60 days, my call.
5. The balance is paid at the dock on the day, cash or card.
6. Corporate groups can be invoiced instead, net 15 from the trip date.
