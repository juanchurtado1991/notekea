# Warm-up library

Exercises I've collected from workshops, to pick from.

- Humming on "mm" to a fifth and back
- "Many mighty men making much money" on a rising scale
- Vowel ladder: ee – eh – ah – oh – oo on one note
- Octave slides on "wee"
- Chord building by part: basses, then tenors, altos, sopranos
- Rhythm echo: clap four bars, choir claps back

From the Cardiff choral day, 2025-06-14.
