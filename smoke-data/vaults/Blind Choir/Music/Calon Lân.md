# Calon Lân

Opening piece of the [[Winter Concert 2026]]. Traditional Welsh hymn, new SATB arrangement
(order SM-0431, see [[Sheet music orders]]).

- Tenors flat in bar 24 — work on it at the tenor sectional.
- Sing the last verse unaccompanied, Owain gives the note.
- Duration about 4 minutes.
