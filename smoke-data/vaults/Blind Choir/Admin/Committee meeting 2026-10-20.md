# Committee meeting 2026-10-20

Minutes by Gareth Lewis (secretary). Held at Bethan's house, 19:30–21:15.

Present: Bethan Davies (chair), Helen Griffiths (treasurer), Gareth Lewis (secretary), Ann Kendrick,
Ieuan Phillips, Carys Pryce (Musical Director, no vote).
Apologies: Lowri Parry.

## 1. Minutes of the last meeting
Agreed as a true record. Matters arising: the new folders have been ordered.

## 2. Treasurer's report
Helen reported a healthy balance going into the winter. Subscriptions are mostly in, a handful
still owed; Helen will send a friendly reminder. The 2026–27 budget is still a draft and will come
back to the next meeting. Alun Pugh has left the choir and his autumn subs were refunded.
The insurance renewal is due in February.

## 3. Musical Director's report
Carys reported good attendance this term, usually around twenty. The winter programme is set
apart from the order of the second half. Tenors need extra work, hence the sectionals in November.
Rhian Hughes is on leave after her operation; the committee sent a card.

- Helen to check the church will take card payments on the door.

## 5. Spring Concert 2027
Penylan Arts Centre provisionally booked for 2027-03-20. The deposit is not yet paid. Theme:
songs of the sea. The committee asked Carys to look for a guest choir.

## 6. Grants
Gareth reported that the Cardiff Arts Fund application went in on 2026-10-01; a decision is
expected in December. If successful it would pay for a workshop day with a visiting conductor.

## 7. Safeguarding
The annual review of the policy was done; no changes. Megan Lloyd is now 18, so no consent form is
needed for her this year.

## 8. Choir van
Dafydd Price reported the MOT passed. Ieuan raised the cost of fuel for trips outside Cardiff;
agreed that drivers can claim mileage.

## 9. Annual General Meeting
The committee agreed to hold the AGM on Wednesday 2027-01-27 at 19:00 in Canton Community Hall,
before a short rehearsal. Gareth will send the notice and agenda by email and pin a copy on the
hall board. Two committee places will be open: Ann Kendrick is standing down after three terms and
Ieuan Phillips's term ends.
