# Constitution of Taff Harmony Community Choir

Adopted at the founding meeting on 2014-05-21 and last amended at the AGM on 2022-01-26.
The choir is a registered charity in England and Wales.

## 1. Name
The organisation shall be called Taff Harmony Community Choir ("the Choir").

## 2. Objects
The objects of the Choir are to advance the education of the public in the art of choral singing,
in particular the music and language of Wales, by holding rehearsals, workshops and public
performances, and to give people of all backgrounds and abilities in and around Cardiff the
chance to sing together.

## 3. Powers
In furtherance of its objects the Choir may:
- raise funds and receive grants, donations and legacies;
- hire rehearsal and performance spaces;
- buy, hire and keep music, equipment and costumes;
- employ or engage a Musical Director, accompanists and other professionals;
- work with other charities and community groups with similar objects;
- do anything else lawful that helps it to meet its objects.

## 4. Membership
4.1 Membership is open to anyone aged 16 or over who supports the objects of the Choir. No audition
is required, although the Musical Director may place a new member in a voice part after a short
voice check.
4.2 A member who is under 18 must have the written consent of a parent or guardian.
4.3 The committee may refuse or end membership only if it believes this is in the best interests of
the Choir, and the person concerned shall have the right to be heard by the committee first.
4.4 A member may resign at any time by telling the secretary.

## 5. Subscriptions
5.1 Members pay a subscription each term at a rate set by the committee and reported to the AGM.
5.2 A reduced rate is available for students, people who are unwaged and people over 75.
5.3 The committee may waive the subscription of any member in hardship or on leave.
5.4 A member whose subscription remains unpaid six weeks after the start of term may not vote at a
general meeting until it is paid.

## 6. The committee
6.1 The Choir is run by a committee of between five and nine members, elected at the AGM.
6.2 The officers are the chair, the secretary and the treasurer. The Musical Director attends
committee meetings but has no vote.
6.3 Committee members serve for two years and may stand again, but no person may serve more than
three consecutive terms.
6.4 The committee may co-opt up to two further members until the next AGM.
6.5 Committee meetings are quorate when four voting members are present.

## 7. General meetings
7.1 An Annual General Meeting shall be held every year, no more than fifteen months after the last.
7.2 The secretary shall give every member at least 21 clear days' notice of the AGM, with the
agenda and any proposed amendments to this constitution.
7.3 The business of the AGM shall include: the chair's report, the treasurer's report and accounts,
the Musical Director's report, the election of the committee and any other business notified in
advance.
7.4 A Special General Meeting may be called by the committee, or by the secretary on the written
request of at least ten members, with 14 clear days' notice.
7.5 The quorum of a general meeting is one third of the members or twelve members, whichever is
fewer.
7.6 Each member present has one vote. Decisions are by simple majority, except where this
constitution says otherwise. The chair has a casting vote.

## 8. Finance
8.1 All money raised by or on behalf of the Choir shall be used only for its objects.
8.2 The treasurer shall keep proper accounts and present them, independently examined, to the AGM.
8.3 The Choir shall keep a bank account in its own name. There shall be at least three signatories,
none of whom may be related to another.
8.4 Any payment over £250 must be authorised by two signatories. Smaller payments may be made by the
treasurer alone and reported to the next committee meeting.
8.5 No committee member may receive payment for their work as a committee member, but reasonable
expenses may be repaid.
8.6 The Musical Director and accompanists may be paid for their professional services under a
written agreement approved by the committee.

## 9. Property
Music, folders, the choir van and other equipment belong to the Choir and are held by the committee.
An inventory shall be kept by the secretary and checked once a year.

## 10. Safeguarding and conduct
The Choir has a safeguarding policy for members under 18 and adults at risk, reviewed every year
by the committee. Members are expected to treat one another with respect at rehearsals and events.

## 11. Amendments
This constitution may be changed by a two-thirds majority of the members present and voting at a
general meeting, provided notice of the amendment was given with the notice of the meeting. No
amendment may be made that would stop the Choir being a charity.

## 12. Dissolution
The Choir may be dissolved by a two-thirds majority at a general meeting called for that purpose.
Any assets remaining after debts are paid shall be given to another charity with similar objects
in Wales, chosen at that meeting, and shall not be shared among the members.

Signed on behalf of the founding meeting: Bethan Davies (chair), Ann Kendrick (secretary).
