# Warm-ups (rehearsal rotation)

15 minutes at the start of each rehearsal. Rotate so nobody gets bored.

## Week A
- Shoulder rolls and stretches
- Sirens on "ng"
- 5-note scale on "mah", up by semitones

## Week B
- Breathing: hiss for 8, 12, 16 counts
- Arpeggios on "noo"
- Round: "Ding Dong" opening phrase

## Week C
- Tongue twisters (Welsh and English)
- Staccato "ha ha ha"
- Tuning chord, hold and listen
