# Listener survey (autumn 2026)

Ran from 2026-09-14 to 2026-10-05, linked in the show notes and read out at the end of four
episodes. 412 people answered. Results below are my summary; the raw export is in the shared
drive.

## Who listens
- Toronto: 64%
- Elsewhere in Ontario: 14%
- Montreal: 6%
- Other Canada: 9%
- Outside Canada: 7%

Most people listen on a commute (walking or transit) or while cooking. A surprising number
listen at work with one earbud in. Almost nobody listens in the car, which makes sense for a
city where so many people take transit or walk.

## How long they have listened
- Less than six months: 22%
- Six months to two years: 41%
- More than two years: 37%

## What they like
The thing people mention most is that the guests are ordinary people with unusual jobs rather
than famous names. Several people said they now notice the people in the episodes when they
walk past them in real life: the ferry crew, the market vendors, the people who fix the
streetlights. Others like that episodes stay around forty-five minutes and do not run long.

Quotes I want to keep:
- "It makes the city feel smaller in a good way."
- "I learned my neighbour was on the show. We talk now."
- "Please never add a laugh track."

## What they would change
- More episodes about neighbourhoods outside downtown (a lot of Scarborough and Etobicoke
  listeners said this).
- Better sound on remote interviews. Fair.
- Fewer ads. Also fair, but the ads pay for the editing.
- A transcript for every episode. People who are hard of hearing asked for this.

## Ads
Most listeners say the ads do not bother them because they are read by me and are for local
businesses. Northfield Coffee was the sponsor people remembered most, then Harbourline Bikes.
A few people said they had used the Harbourline code.

## Format questions
- Keep the weekly schedule: 81%
- Would like a short mid-week bonus episode: 33%
- Would pay for an ad-free feed: 19%

## Topics they asked for
The open question was "What would you like an episode about?" I grouped the answers into
topics. The long tail is fun (someone wants an episode about the raccoons, several people want
the person who runs the streetcar announcements). The top of the list:

- Transit workers of every kind: 18%
- Neighbourhoods outside downtown: 15%
- Musicians and venues: 12%
- Small shop owners: 11%

And the clear winner, which I did not expect: Toronto food history, asked for by 31% of the
people who answered the question. People want the stories of the old bakeries, the first
restaurants of each neighbourhood, the market stalls that have been in one family for
generations. That should be a whole mini-series in January.
