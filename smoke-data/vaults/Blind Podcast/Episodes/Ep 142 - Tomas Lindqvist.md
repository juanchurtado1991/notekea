# Ep 142 — Ferry captains of the harbour

Guest: [[Tomas Lindqvist]]

## Prep

Tomas has skippered the island ferries for most of his working life, first as a deckhand in the
summers while he was at college, then as a mate, then as captain of the smaller boat that does the
early run. He is shy on the phone but opened up completely once we were talking about weather. The
plan was to keep the conversation moving between three things: the daily routine of a crossing,
the strange things people leave behind on the boats, and what the harbour looks like in the
middle of winter when hardly anyone rides.

Things to ask about:
- The first crossing of the day and who is always on it
- The worst fog he has sailed through
- Lost and found: the wedding cake, the cello, the tortoise
- What changed when the new terminal opened
- Whether he ever wanted to work on the big lake boats instead

Things to avoid:
- Union negotiations (he is not allowed to talk about them)
- Anything about the accident inquiry from years ago; he will say no comment

## The recording

We recorded on 2026-10-22 at Studio B. He brought a printed map of the harbour and kept pointing at
it, so a few passages need a little context added in the intro because listeners cannot see the
map. Good energy throughout. The tortoise story runs long but it is the best part of the episode;
keep it whole.

Rough timeline of the raw file:
- 00:00 small talk, cut
- 02:40 the first crossing of the day
- 09:15 fog stories
- 17:50 lost and found, including the tortoise
- 26:30 the new terminal
- 31:05 winter on the harbour
- 36:20 the lake boats question
- 44:10 wrap-up and thanks

## Edit notes for Sam

The mix is mostly fine. Bring his voice up slightly in the fog section where he leaned back from
the mic. The map rustling around 12 minutes is distracting; reduce what you can. The laugh at the
end of the tortoise story can stay. The ad break goes between the lost and found section and the
terminal section.

## After the recording

Tomas called the day after. He was worried about one thing he said while answering the lake boats
question: he named the shipping company he left years ago and said something unkind about a
former manager. He asked very politely that we take it out. The passage starts at 38:12 in the raw
file and runs about forty seconds, ending where he says he has no regrets. I agreed straight away
and passed it to Sam. Sam cut it in the final mix, and the published episode does not include it.
