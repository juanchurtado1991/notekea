# Northfield Sponsorship Agreement

My plain-language copy of the agreement with [[Northfield Coffee]]. The signed PDF is in the
shared drive; this note is what I actually look things up in.

## 1. Parties
The agreement is between me, as producer and host of The Long Table, and Northfield Coffee, a
roaster based in Toronto, represented by Elena Marsh. Either side may name a new contact person
by email without changing the agreement.

## 2. Term
The agreement runs from 2026-03-01 to 2027-02-28. It covers every regular weekly episode
published in that period. Bonus episodes, live shows and trailers are not covered unless both
sides agree in writing.

## 3. What Northfield gets
- One host-read pre-roll on every regular episode, 30 to 45 seconds long.
- A mention of Northfield in the show notes with a link to their website.
- The read stays in the episode for as long as the episode is online, including after the term
  ends, unless Northfield asks for it to be removed.
- One social post per month that mentions the sponsorship.

## 4. Fee
Northfield pays $425 per pre-roll. I invoice after each episode goes out. Payment is due thirty
days after the invoice date. Invoices go to the accounts address Elena gave me, with a copy to
her.

## 5. Approval of reads
I send the script for each read at least three days before the episode is published. Northfield
may ask for changes once per read. If they do not reply within two working days, the script is
approved. I write the read in my own voice; Northfield does not supply a script word for word.

## 6. Content and independence
Northfield has no say over guests, topics or anything said in an episode outside the read.
If an episode covers something Northfield finds uncomfortable, they may ask for their read to
be left out of that one episode. In that case no fee is owed for that episode and neither side
owes the other anything else.

## 7. Exclusivity
During the term I will not take another coffee sponsor. Tea, cafés that do not roast and
kitchen equipment are fine. This is why the fourth-sponsor search has to stay away from coffee.

## 8. Pulled or broken reads
If a read is missing, wrong or cut short because of my mistake, I either re-record it in the
next episode at no charge or refund the fee for that episode, whichever Northfield prefers.

## 9. Late payment
Payment is due thirty days after the invoice date, as in section 4. If a payment is late, a fee
of 1.5% per month is added to the unpaid amount, counted from the due date. I have never
actually charged it, but it is in there, and I can point to it when an invoice is slow.

## 10. Live read at events
Northfield may ask for one live read per quarter at an event in one of their cafés. I am not
paid extra for this, but they cover travel within the city and provide coffee for the audience.

## 11. Data
Every three months I send Northfield the downloads for the episodes that carried their read,
taken from the hosting dashboard. I do not share listener personal data. Survey results are
shared only as totals.

## 12. Download bonus
Northfield pays a one-time bonus of $1,250 the first time an episode carrying their pre-roll
passes 7,000 downloads in its first 30 days. The bonus is invoiced separately once the 30-day
figure is in, and it is paid under the same terms as any other invoice. It can only be earned
once during the term.

## 13. Ending early
Either side can end the agreement early with 60 days' notice in writing. Reads for episodes
already published are paid in full. If Northfield ends it early, they still pay for any
episode whose read was already recorded.

## 14. Renewal
The agreement does not renew by itself. If either side wants to continue, we talk in January.
If I want to raise the fee for a new term, I should tell Elena at least 45 days before the end
of the term.

## 15. Disputes
We try to sort out any disagreement by talking first. If that fails, we use a mediator in
Toronto and split the cost.

## 16. Small print
- Changes to the agreement have to be in writing; email counts.
- Neither side may use the other's logo except as described in section 3.
- The agreement follows the law of Ontario.
