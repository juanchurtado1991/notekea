# Service agreement

Signed copy, pasted from the PDF the cooperative sent on 4 December 2026.

## 1. Parties

This Service Agreement is made between Tinamu Labs S.A. (the Provider), with its office in Heredia, and Cafetal Norte Cooperative (the Client), with its office in San Carlos. Each is a Party and together they are the Parties.

## 2. Purpose

The Provider will run, watch and maintain the Client's harvest tracking system, which records every delivery of coffee cherries from the Client's 412 member farms, and will keep it available to the Client's staff during the harvest and outside it.

## 3. Term

This Agreement starts on 1 January 2027 and lasts 24 months. It ends on 31 December 2028 unless it is renewed in writing by both Parties at least 90 days before that date.

## 4. Services

The Provider will host the system, apply security updates within 10 working days of their release, keep a daily backup for 35 days, and answer the Client's requests through the support desk described in clause 9.

## 5. Monthly fee

The Client will pay the Provider a monthly fee of 4,250 USD for the Services. The fee covers up to 60 named users. Each named user above 60 costs 18 USD a month.

## 6. Invoices

The Provider will send an invoice on the first working day of each month. The Client will pay each invoice within 30 days of receiving it, by bank transfer to the account named on the invoice.

## 7. Late payment

An invoice not paid within 30 days bears interest of 1.5 % a month on the unpaid amount, counted from the day after it was due until the day it is paid in full.

## 8. Fee review

The monthly fee stays the same for the first 12 months. From month 13 the Provider may raise it once, by no more than 4 %, with 60 days of written notice.

## 9. Support desk

The support desk answers from Monday to Saturday, 6:00 to 18:00, Costa Rica time. During the harvest, from 1 November to 28 February, it also answers on Saturdays until 20:00. A request marked urgent gets a first reply within 2 hours.

## 10. Availability

The Provider commits to the system being available 99.5 % of each calendar month, measured by the Provider's monitoring and not counting planned maintenance announced 5 days ahead.

## 11. Service credits

For each full 0.5 percentage points below 99.5 % in a month, the Client gets a credit of 5 % of that month's fee, up to 25 % of it. Credits are taken off the next invoice and are the Client's only remedy for low availability.

## 12. Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance happens on Sundays between 1:00 and 5:00, never during the harvest weeks of December, and lasts no more than 4 hours a month.

## 13. Data ownership

All delivery records, farm records and member records in the system belong to the Client. The Provider gets no right over them beyond what it needs to perform the Services.

## 14. Data return

When this Agreement ends, the Provider will hand the Client a full export of its data in CSV and SQL formats within 15 days, and will delete its own copies within 45 days, confirming it in writing.

## 15. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep the other's confidential information secret for 5 years after this Agreement ends, and will share it only with staff and advisers who need it and are bound to the same secrecy.

## 16. Personal data

The Provider will process the members' personal data only on the Client's written instructions, keep it in data centres in Costa Rica or the United States, and tell the Client of any breach within 48 hours of learning of it.

## 17. Subcontractors

The Provider may use subcontractors for hosting and for the support desk. It stays responsible for their work and will name them to the Client on request.

## 18. Changes to the Services

Either Party may ask for a change. The Provider will answer within 10 working days with what the change costs and how long it takes. No change binds the Parties until both have signed it.

## 19. Training

The Provider will give two training sessions of 3 hours each in the first 60 days, at the Client's office, at no extra cost. Further sessions are charged at the rates of Annex B.

## 20. Client duties

The Client will name one contact person, keep its users' accounts up to date, and give the Provider the access to its scales and printers that the Services need.

## 21. Intellectual property

The software stays the Provider's. The Client gets a licence to use it for its own cooperative business during the Term, which it may not pass on or sell.

## 22. Warranties

The Provider warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care, and that the system will work as its user guide describes in every material respect.

## 23. Liability

Neither Party is liable for loss of profit or indirect loss. Each Party's total liability under this Agreement in any 12 months is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. Nothing limits liability for fraud or for a breach of clause 15.

## 24. Insurance

The Provider will hold professional liability insurance of at least 500,000 USD during the Term and show proof of it on request.

## 25. Force majeure

A Party is not in breach while an event beyond its reasonable control, such as an earthquake, a flood or a national power failure, stops it from performing, provided it tells the other Party within 3 days.

## 26. Termination for convenience

Either Party may end this Agreement without giving a reason with 60 days of written notice. If the Client does so in the first 12 months it will pay an early termination charge equal to two monthly fees.

## 27. Termination for breach

A Party may end this Agreement at once by written notice if the other Party breaches it materially and does not put the breach right within 30 days of being asked to, or becomes insolvent.

## 28. After termination

Clauses 13, 14, 15, 23 and 30 go on after this Agreement ends. Fees due before it ends stay due.

## 29. Notices

Notices are given in writing, by email to the addresses in Annex A with a confirmation of receipt, and take effect on the next working day.

## 30. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Costa Rica. The Parties will first try to settle a dispute between their managers for 30 days, and then by arbitration in San José under the rules of the Centro de Conciliación y Arbitraje, in Spanish, before one arbitrator.

## 31. Entire agreement

This Agreement and its Annexes are the whole agreement between the Parties about the Services and replace every earlier proposal, including the Provider's offer of 14 September 2026.
