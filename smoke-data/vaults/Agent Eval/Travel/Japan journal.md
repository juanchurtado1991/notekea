# Japan journal

Fourteen days in Japan with Tomás, 3 to 16 April 2026: Tokyo, Kyoto, Nara, Lake Kawaguchi and Tokyo again. Written every night, tidied a little afterwards. "Spent" is what I spent that day, in yen, not counting the ryokan.

## Day 1. Tokyo, Friday 3 April

Landed at Haneda at 6:40 after the long flight through Dallas. Train and metro to the Hotel Kaede in Shinjuku; the room was ready early, which saved us. Walked around Shinjuku in a daze until the evening. Dinner at Menya Hikari, a tiny ramen counter near the station: you buy a ticket from a machine and hand it over.

Weather: cloudy and mild. Walked 7 km.
Spent: 6,400 yen.

## Day 2. Tokyo, Saturday 4 April

Meiji shrine in the morning, very quiet under the trees; then Harajuku, which was the opposite. Lunch at Tonkatsu Aoba, a pork cutlet so thick Tomás took a photo of it from the side. Afternoon in Shibuya: the crossing from the station's upper floor, then the long walk back to Shinjuku through Yoyogi park, full of people under the cherry trees.

Tomás and I disagree about mornings: he wants to be out at 7:00, I want coffee first. We settled on 8:00 and a convenience store coffee on the way, which turns out to be very good in Japan.

Weather: sunny, the warmest day so far. Walked 14 km.
Spent: 9,800 yen.

## Day 3. Tokyo, Sunday 5 April

Asakusa and the Senso-ji temple early, before the crowds. Rain started at noon and did not stop. I left my blue umbrella on the Ginza line on the way to Ueno and bought a clear one at a convenience store for 600 yen. Ueno park in the rain was almost empty, and the cherry blossoms on the wet paths were the best sight of the trip. Dinner at Izakaya Kumo in Asakusa: grilled skewers, cold beer, an old man next to us who wanted to practise his English.

Weather: rain all afternoon. Walked 11 km.
Spent: 7,300 yen.

## Day 4. Tokyo, Monday 6 April

The outer market of Tsukiji for breakfast: tamagoyaki on a stick, strawberries, grilled scallops. Sushi lunch at Sushi Tsukasa, a counter for eight. Afternoon at teamLab Planets, where you walk barefoot through water and mirrors; the tickets were 3,800 yen each and worth it. Packed in the evening for Kyoto.

Learned today that you cannot eat while walking here, or not politely: people stand by the stall until they finish. Also that every station has spotless toilets, and that the metro map makes sense after the third day and not before.

Weather: sunny. Walked 9 km.
Spent: 11,200 yen.

## Day 5. Tokyo to Kyoto, Tuesday 7 April

The Nozomi from Tokyo Station at 9:00, in Kyoto at 11:15: two hours and fifteen minutes, smooth as a lift. No view of Mount Fuji, all cloud. The tickets were 14,170 yen each, the largest part of today's spending.

Checked in at the Ryokan Momiji, near the Kamo river: tatami, futons, a shared bath of hot water. I paid the ryokan with my card for both of us, for the five nights: 90,000 yen. Tomás owes me half, 45,000 yen. Lunch at Soba Yoshino, cold soba near the Nishiki market, then a slow walk through the market itself.

Weather: cloudy. Walked 6 km.
Spent: 19,600 yen.

## Day 6. Kyoto, Wednesday 8 April

Up at 6:00 for the Fushimi Inari shrine before the crowds: thousands of orange gates up the hill, and we climbed to the top. Afternoon at Tofuku-ji. Evening in Gion, hoping to see a geiko, and we did, for two seconds, between two taxis. Dinner at Gion Yamato: a set menu of many small plates.

The shrine at dawn was the first moment of the trip that felt like the pictures in my head. Tomás counted the gates for a while and gave up somewhere past four hundred. At the top there is a view over the whole of Kyoto, and a small stall selling cold tea to the people who made it.

Weather: sunny. Walked 16 km, the most of the trip.
Spent: 6,900 yen.

## Day 7. Kyoto, Thursday 9 April

Arashiyama: the bamboo grove in light rain, then the monkey park on the hill, then Tenryu-ji's garden once the rain stopped. Lunch at Yudofu Saga, simmered tofu in a garden. The train back to the ryokan was packed with school trips.

Weather: rain in the morning, clear after lunch. Walked 12 km.
Spent: 8,500 yen.

## Day 8. Kyoto, Friday 10 April

Kinkaku-ji, the golden pavilion, which looks exactly like the postcards and somehow still surprises. Ryoan-ji's stone garden after it; we sat for half an hour. Dinner at Okonomiyaki Hana, where the cook let Tomás flip his own and it went badly.

In the afternoon we wrote postcards in the ryokan's garden. The owner, Mrs. Ishida, told us her family has run the place for four generations, and showed us the guest book with names from 1962.

Weather: cloudy. Walked 10 km.
Spent: 7,700 yen.

## Day 9. Nara, Saturday 11 April

Day trip to Nara by train, 45 minutes from Kyoto. The deer bow if you bow first; one ate the corner of my map. Todai-ji and its great Buddha, bigger than any photo prepares you for. Lunch at Kamameshi Tsubaki: rice cooked in its own iron pot. Back to Kyoto for the last night at the ryokan.

Weather: sunny. Walked 13 km.
Spent: 5,200 yen.

## Day 10. Kyoto to Lake Kawaguchi, Sunday 12 April

The Hikari to Mishima, then a bus over the hills to Kawaguchiko, the town on Lake Kawaguchi. Four hours of travel in all. Checked in at the Fuji View Lodge, a guesthouse with a view of the mountain, except that the mountain was hidden in cloud. Dinner at Hoto Kogetsu: thick noodles in a miso stew, the local dish.

The bus from Mishima climbed through tea fields and then pine woods. Fuji should have been in front of us the whole way; we saw only a white wall of cloud. The owner of the lodge promised clear skies for the morning, and pointed at the alarm clock.

Weather: cloudy, no Fuji. Walked 5 km.
Spent: 12,400 yen.

## Day 11. Lake Kawaguchi, Monday 13 April

Up before dawn on Tomás's insistence, and he was right: Fuji was clear, pink and then white, from Oishi Park on the north shore of the lake. We finally saw it there, after a whole day of waiting. Rented bikes and rode around half the lake. Coffee and cake at Lakeside Café Kaze.

Weather: sunny and cold. Walked 6 km, cycled 18 km.
Spent: 6,100 yen.

## Day 12. Lake Kawaguchi to Tokyo, Tuesday 14 April

Bus back to Shinjuku, two hours, rain all the way. Same hotel as the first days. A lazy evening; dinner at Curry Ganesh, a basement curry place near the hotel.

Weather: rain. Walked 4 km.
Spent: 4,900 yen.

## Day 13. Tokyo, Wednesday 15 April

Akihabara for Tomás, who wanted old game consoles, and Kappabashi for me, the street of kitchen shops: a knife for my sister and chopsticks for everyone. Gifts took most of today's money. Dinner at Yakitori Ren in Kanda, under the railway arches.

We spent the evening sorting what to carry home. My bag is heavier than when I came, mostly with kitchen things. Tomás says the consoles are an investment.

Weather: cloudy. Walked 12 km.
Spent: 15,300 yen.

## Day 14. Tokyo, Thursday 16 April

The last morning, before the airport: Yanaka, the old neighbourhood with its temples, cats and small shops, the quietest part of Tokyo we saw. Coffee at Kissa Yanaka. Train to Haneda at 13:00 and the flight home in the evening.

Weather: sunny. Walked 5 km.
Spent: 3,600 yen.

## Afterwards

Best: Fuji at dawn, Fushimi Inari before the crowds, Ueno in the rain. Next time: fewer cities, more days in each, and a reservation for the Ghibli museum well ahead.
