# ASADA assembly, 29 October

General assembly of the ASADA de San Isidro in the community hall. 46 members present. Chaired by Hernán Alfaro, president. Carolina Mora presented the [[Water audit 2026]] and answered questions for an hour.

## Decisions on the audit's recommendations

- R1: approved. 2,800 USD from the reserve; Hernán hires the work before the end of November.
- R2: approved in two phases. The first phase, 1.5 km, in 2027; the second depends on a grant.
- R3: approved. The plumber starts with the connections of Calle El Cedro.
- R5: postponed to the January assembly; the neighbouring farmer wants to talk first.
- R6: approved. The regional office gives the course in January.
- R7: rejected for this year by 29 votes to 17.

There was no time for the rest of the agenda.
