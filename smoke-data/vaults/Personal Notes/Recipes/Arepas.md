# Arepas

## Ingredients

- 2 cups pre-cooked white corn flour
- 2 1/2 cups warm water
- 1 teaspoon salt

## Steps

1. Mix the water and salt, then add the flour slowly while stirring.
2. Rest the dough for 5 minutes, shape 6 discs about 1 cm thick.
3. Cook on a hot griddle for 6 minutes per side, then 10 minutes in the oven at 180 °C.

Pairs well with [[Café con leche]].
