# Okafor museum brochure

Exhibition brochure for the riverside museum extension designed by [[Okafor Studio]]. EN → PT-PT.
Contact: [[Tom Okafor]].

## Scope
- 11,000 words in two parts of 5,500 words each
- Part 1: the building (concept, materials, structure) — delivered 2026-10-17, invoiced INV-2026-041
- Part 2: the exhibition spaces and the landscape — deadline 2026-12-04
- Format: Word file with comments; the studio's designer lays it out in InDesign afterwards

## Style
- Warm, readable, for a general public — not an engineering report
- Keep the studio's own terms in English where they are brand names: "Light Spine", "River Room"
- Measurements in metres with a decimal comma in Portuguese (12,5 m)
- Dates written out in full ("17 de outubro de 2026")
- No Brazilian forms: "ecrã", not "tela"; "projeto" with the new spelling
