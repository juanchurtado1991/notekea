# Wine EN-PT

For [[Quinta do Vale Azul]] and any other wine work. Direction mostly PT → EN.

| Portuguese | English |
|---|---|
| casta | grape variety |
| vinha velha | old vines |
| estágio em barrica | barrel ageing |
| lagar | stone treading tank |
| aguardente vínica | grape spirit |
| Tawny 10 anos | 10 Year Old Tawny |
| notas de prova | tasting notes |

Rita wants "Reserva" kept as it is on the labels (it is a legal term).
