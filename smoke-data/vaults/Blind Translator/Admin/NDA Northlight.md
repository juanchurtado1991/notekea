# NDA Northlight

My summary of the non-disclosure agreement with [[Northlight Localization]] (Northlight
Localization B.V., Utrecht). The signed PDF is in the Nimbo Drive folder "Contracts". This summary
is mine, clause by clause, so I don't have to reread twelve pages every time.

## 1. Parties
Northlight Localization B.V., registered in Utrecht, "the Company"; and me, Inês Carvalho, freelance
translator, "the Contractor". The agreement covers every project the Company sends me, now and
later, under any purchase order.

## 2. Purpose
The Company shares unreleased games, scripts, art, audio and business information so that I can
translate, review or test them. I may use the information only for that purpose.

## 3. What counts as confidential
Everything the Company or its clients give me, in any form: source files, translations I make from
them, screenshots, builds, character names, story details, release dates, prices, the names of the
Company's clients and the fact that a given game is being localised at all. It does not matter if it
is marked "confidential" or not.

## 4. What does not count
Information that was already public, that I already knew before (and can show it), that I receive
from someone else without a duty of confidence, or that I develop independently without using the
Company's information. The burden of proof is on me.

## 5. My obligations
- Keep the information secret and protect it at least as well as my own confidential material.
- Store files only on encrypted devices and in the approved cloud storage.
- No public posts about the projects: no titles, no screenshots, no "working on a cool game".
- Do not use machine translation services that keep the data, and never paste the text into public
  online tools.
- Tell the Company at once if something leaks or a device is lost or stolen.

## 6. Subcontracting
I may not pass any work or information to another translator or reviewer without written consent
from the Company. If they consent, the other person must sign the same NDA first.

## 7. Portfolio and references
I may name the Company as a client in my CV and on my website. I may not name the games or show any
work until the game is released, and even then only with written permission for each title.

## 8. Duration
The agreement starts on signature and lasts while I work with the Company. The duty of
confidentiality continues for five years after the last project ends. For trade secrets it continues
for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 9. Breach
If I breach the agreement, the Company may end all open projects immediately and keep any unpaid
amounts for the work affected. There is also a penalty clause: liquidated damages of EUR 20,000 per
breach, without the Company having to prove the actual loss, and without prejudice to claiming more
if the loss is higher. My note: that is more than all my invoices to them so far — be careful.

## 10. No licence
Nothing in the agreement gives me any right to the games, the characters or the trademarks. My
translations become the Company's (or its client's) property on payment.

## 11. Return and deletion
When a project ends, or whenever the Company asks, I must delete or return all the files and
copies, including translation memories and termbases built from their material, within 30 days, and
confirm the deletion by email. Backups that delete themselves automatically on a schedule are
acceptable if they cannot be restored by me.

## 12. Notices
Notices go by email to the Company's legal address and to the localisation lead ([[Hannah Lowe]]),
and to my business email.

## 13. Whole agreement
This NDA replaces any earlier talk about confidentiality. Changes only in writing, signed by both.
If one clause is invalid, the rest stays in force.

## 14. Law and courts
Dutch law applies. Any dispute goes to the competent court in Rotterdam, not Utrecht — they told me
it is where their lawyers are. English is the language of the agreement; my Portuguese notes have no
legal value.
