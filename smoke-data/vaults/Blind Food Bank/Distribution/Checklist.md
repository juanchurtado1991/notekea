# Distribution day checklist

## Before doors open
- [ ] Set up intake table and sign-in sheets
- [ ] Put out the allergen signs
- [ ] Check the walk-in cooler temperature and write it in the [[Freezer log]]
- [ ] Count the bread racks

## After close
- [ ] Count households from the sign-in sheets
- [ ] Weigh what is left and enter pounds out
- [ ] Sweep and mop the distribution floor
