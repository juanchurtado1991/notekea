# Care plan for Mom and Dad

Agreed on the family call of 2026-08-30, after Mom's fall, and updated since. The goal is simple: keep both of them
at home in Bellbrook, safe and as independent as they can be, for as long as that is the right thing for them.
Everybody who helps should read this once. Linked notes hold the details: [[Home Aides]], [[Dad/Medications]],
[[Mom/Medications]], [[Emergency Contacts]], [[Equipment]], [[Meal Plan]].

## 1. Goals
1. No more falls. Mom is the high risk; Dad is the second.
2. Medications given on time, every time, by a person who checks them off.
3. Mom's memory: keep the routine steady, keep her busy, keep her calm in the late afternoon.
4. Dad's heart and sugar: steady readings, no skipped appointments.
5. Bills paid on time from Dad's checking account, and everybody can see where the money goes.
6. Laura does not burn out. (Theresa insisted on writing this one down.)

## 2. A normal weekday
- **6:00** — Dad up, coffee, paper. He is fine alone at this hour.
- **8:00** — Aide arrives (Gloria on Monday, Wednesday and Friday; Yvonne on Tuesday and Thursday). Mom up,
  washed, dressed. Breakfast for both. Morning pills from the organizers, checked off on the chart on the fridge.
- **10:00** — Mom's exercises from PT. Dad reads or naps.
- **12:30** — Lunch. Mom's vitamin D with lunch.
- **13:00–14:00** — Aide leaves (Yvonne at 13:00, Gloria at 14:00). Laura arrives most days between 13:00 and 15:00.
- **14:00** — Mom rests. Dad's walk around the block if the weather allows, never alone on ice.
- **16:00–18:00** — The hard hours for Mom. Music, folding towels, looking at photo albums.
- **18:00** — Dinner. Dad's tamsulosin after dinner.
- **20:00** — Dad's evening apixaban. Laura or Dad gives Mom her bedtime pills if Laura has left.
- **21:00** — Stove knobs off, doors locked, motion lights on, baby monitor on in the den.

## 3. Weekends
Saturday: Yvonne comes 10:00 to 15:00. Sunday: family day. One of the sisters comes when she can; otherwise
Laura. Sunday evening the pill organizers are filled for the week.

## 4. Falls and mobility
- Walker for Mom everywhere until Kara Lindqvist and Dr. Amsel say otherwise. Cane only at PT for now.
- Night path lit from the den to the bathroom.
- No rugs. The hall runner is gone; Dad complained, it stays gone.
- Ramp at the back door. Front steps have a rail on both sides.
- Dad does not go into the basement. Laundry moved upstairs.
- If either of them falls: do not lift them alone. Check for pain, call 911 if there is any doubt (Dad is on a
  blood thinner — any knock to the head means the emergency room).

## 5. Medications
- Two organizers, labeled, on the kitchen counter. Nobody else's pills go near them.
- The chart on the fridge gets a check mark per dose. Missing marks get a call to Laura.
- Refills: Kettle Creek Pharmacy delivers. Laura orders on the 20th of every month.
- Changes in a dose go into the medications note the same day, with the date.

## 6. Memory care for Mom
- Same faces, same times. The whiteboard tells her the day and who is coming.
- Do not argue with her about the past. Redirect.
- Keep her teaching things around: the alphabet cards, the old lesson plans. She likes to "grade" them.
- If she asks for her mother, say Grandma Ruth is resting and offer tea.
- Never leave her alone with the stove or the car keys (she has not driven since 2024; the car is sold).

## 7. Food
Dad diabetic, Mom low sodium. Soft foods for Mom until the crown is fixed. Weekly plan in [[Meal Plan]].

## 8. Who does what
- **Laura** — daily visits, appointments, medications, bills with Dad, this whole vault.
- **Denise and Mike Kowalski** (Columbus) — monthly money, a weekend each month, cover in emergencies.
- **Theresa and Mike Harlan** (Cincinnati) — monthly money, drive to some appointments, the insurance phone calls
  when Laura cannot face them.
- **Gloria Pratt** — private aide, hired directly.
- **Yvonne Becker** — aide through Heartland Companions.

## 9. Money
- Bills are paid from Dad's checking. Laura's card only when there is no choice, and then it is reimbursed from Dad's
  account at the end of the month.
- The sisters' contributions go into Dad's checking. See [[Sibling Contributions]].
- Every bill gets a line in the bills note of the month.

## 10. Legal and documents
The originals are in the fire box in Dad's closet; copies in the blue folder in the hall closet.
- Wills: both updated 2021, lawyer in Xenia.
- **Healthcare power of attorney:** Laura for both parents; Denise is the backup.
- **Financial power of attorney:** for Dad it is **Theresa** (she is the accountant). For Mom it is Dad, and
  Theresa after him.
- Living wills: both signed. Copies at Dr. Vasquez's office.
- Medicare cards and the Buckeye Senior Advantage cards: in Dad's wallet and Mom's purse, photos on Laura's phone.

## 11. Emergencies
- Call 911 first. Then Laura, then whichever sister is nearer.
- **Hospital:** both of them go to **St. Brendan's Regional**, not Greene Hollow Medical Center. Dr. Okafor has
  privileges at St. Brendan's and Mom's records were moved there after the rehab stay. Tell the ambulance crew.
- The grab-and-go folder (med lists, allergies, insurance copies, POA copies) hangs on the hook by the back door.
- Mom's allergy goes on every form. So does Dad's.

## 12. Looking ahead
- Stairlift: Lift & Glide visit to measure, then decide. See [[Equipment]].
- Respite: if Laura needs a week off, Heartland Companions can do overnights; ask early.
- If Mom's memory gets worse, we tour the memory-care homes the social worker listed. Not yet.

## 13. Review
This plan is reviewed with all three sisters every two months, and whenever something big happens.
