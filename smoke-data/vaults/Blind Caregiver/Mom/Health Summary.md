# Mom — health summary

Margaret "Peggy" Brennan, 81. Retired second-grade teacher. Loves her garden, her cardinals at the feeder and the
Sunday crossword she does "with" Dad (she reads the clues, he writes). Some days she is fully herself, some days
she asks for her own mother.

## Conditions
- **Early-stage Alzheimer's disease** — diagnosed 2025-06 by Dr. Lena Moravec, neurology. On donepezil. Short-term
  memory is the main problem: repeats questions, forgets whether she ate. Long-term memory is still very good.
  Dr. Moravec repeats the MoCA test every six months. The letter with the last result is in the blue folder in the
  hall closet at the house; I never copied the number into these notes.
- **Hypertension** — on lisinopril. Readings went up after the surgery, dose raised in October.
- **Osteoporosis** — on weekly alendronate plus vitamin D. That is why the fall was so bad.
- **Depression / anxiety** — mild, on sertraline since 2023. Better since the aides started.

## The fall and the hip
- 2026-08-19 — fell in the kitchen around 7 in the evening, reaching for a pot. Dad found her and called 911.
  Ambulance to Greene Hollow Medical Center.
- 2026-08-21 — left hip replacement, Dr. Victor Amsel.
- 2026-08-26 — discharged to Bellbrook Rehab for inpatient therapy.
- 2026-09-11 — home, with a walker. Outpatient PT since then, see [[Mom/Physical Therapy Log]].
- She is not allowed to bend the hip past 90 degrees or cross her legs until Dr. Amsel clears her.

## Mobility now
Walker everywhere, cane trial with Kara at PT. Needs standby help in the shower (shower chair and grab bars are in,
see [[Equipment]]). No stairs — she sleeps in the downstairs den now, in the hospital bed we rented.

## Memory and daily life
- She does better with a fixed routine. Breakfast at 8, lunch at 12:30, a rest at 2.
- The whiteboard in the kitchen says the day, the date and who is coming. Gloria updates it each morning.
- She must not be left alone in the kitchen with the stove on. The knobs come off at night.
- She still writes cards to her old students at Christmas. I help her with the addresses.
- Sundowning: late afternoon she gets restless and wants "to go home". Music helps — the big band records.

## Eating
Low sodium for the blood pressure, plenty of calcium. Small appetite since the surgery; she lost about eight pounds.
Ensure shake in the afternoon. See [[Meal Plan]].

## Sleep
Sleeps in the den. Up once at night for the bathroom; the motion light in the hall helps.

## History
- 2011 — cataract surgery, both eyes.
- 1998 — hysterectomy.
- Three daughters: Denise, Theresa, and me (Laura).

## Allergies
- **Sulfa drugs** — a bad rash in 2019 after an antibiotic for a urinary infection. Write it on every form.
- Adhesive tape irritates her skin; ask for paper tape.
