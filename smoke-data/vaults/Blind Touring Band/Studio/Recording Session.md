# Recording Session — Greywater, December

Four days at Greywater Studio, up the valley past Hebden Bridge, from 2026-12-14 to 2026-12-17. We drive
over on the 13th in the van ([[Van Rental]], booking PVH-2310) and come back on the 18th. Hollis Vane
engineers; the studio day rate includes him. Money side: the Budget note in the Studio folder.

## Goal
Five tracks, tracked live as a band where we can, for an EP in spring 2027. The label conversation can wait
until we have something finished. Mixing happens at Greywater in January; mastering is booked separately.

## Tracks
1. Paper Saints
2. Lowtide Hymn
3. Saltlight
4. Halfway Kitchen
5. Ember Street (new, still missing a bridge, see [[Song Ideas]])

## Who
- Drums: [[Ollie Brennan]], if the wrist is right by then. If the physio says no, we ask [[Ollie Sato]].
- Bass: [[Priya Okafor]]
- Vocals and keys: [[Dan Whitlow]]
- Guitars: me
- Violin: [[Mags Ferreira]], days three and four only

## Gear we bring
- My Telecaster and the Jazzmaster, both restrung on the 13th
- The Vox AC15 and the little Fender for overdubs
- Priya's P-bass and the Ampeg head (Greywater has the cab)
- Ollie's Rogers kit, his snare collection, and his own cymbals
- Dan's Nord; Greywater has the upright piano, tuned on the 14th at our request
- Pedalboards, spare cables, every spare string we own
- Hard drive for stems (2 TB), labelled

## Gear Greywater has
- Neve-style console, 24 channels in
- Drum room with high ceiling, and a dead booth for vocals
- Two good room mics, a ribbon pair, and the usual dynamic mics
- Upright piano, Rhodes, a Hammond with Leslie (extra charge, not booked)

## Day plan
### Day one — 2026-12-14
Set up, get sounds, drum tones first. Hollis wants the kit in the big room with the doors open to the
hall. Aim to track Paper Saints and Halfway Kitchen live as a four-piece. Guide vocals only.

### Day two — 2026-12-15
Lowtide Hymn and Saltlight live. Ember Street if the bridge is written by then; if not, drums and bass
to a click and we finish it in day four. Fix any guitar parts from day one.

### Day three — 2026-12-16
Overdubs. Guitars in the morning, then Mags arrives after lunch. She plays violin on Lowtide Hymn and
Saltlight, nothing else; the charts go to her a week before. Dan does the first lead vocals in the evening.

### Day four — 2026-12-17
Mags again in the morning for doubles and the harmony line on Saltlight. Lead vocals in the afternoon.
Rough mixes of all five to take home. Pack down by 21:00, sleep, drive back on the 18th.

## Things to remember
- Hollis hates click tracks on live takes; bounce them anyway, just in case.
- Greywater's kitchen is self-catered: food shop on the way over.
- No phones in the live room during takes.
- Ask Hollis about stem delivery format before day four.
