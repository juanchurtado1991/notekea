# Harbourline Agency Contract

My plain-English copy of the agreement with Harbourline Artists Ltd. The signed PDF is in the band
Dropbox. Signed 2026-02-16 by me for the band and by [[Tom Reyes]] for Harbourline.

## 1. Parties
Harbourline Artists Ltd ("the Agent") and The Lantern Coast, a band made up of Cass Moreland, Dan
Whitlow, Priya Okafor and Ollie Brennan ("the Artist"). Session players are not parties to this agreement
and the Agent has no claim on their own work outside the band.

## 2. Definitions
- "Engagement" means any live performance by the Artist booked during the term, whether a headline show,
  a support slot, a festival, a private event or a livestream with a paying audience.
- "Gross fee" means the guarantee, plus any door split or bonus actually received, before the Artist's
  own costs, and before any VAT.
- "Territory" means the countries in section 3.
- "Term" means the period in section 4.

## 3. Territory
The United Kingdom and Ireland. Shows outside the Territory can be booked by the Agent only with the
Artist's written approval, case by case. The Agent may work with partner agencies abroad, but the Artist
pays no more than it would under this agreement.

## 4. Term
Twenty-four months from 2026-03-01. The term rolls on for a further twelve months unless either side ends
it under section 11.

## 5. Appointment and exclusivity
The Artist appoints the Agent as its exclusive booking agent for live Engagements in the Territory. The
Artist will pass every live offer it receives directly to the Agent within seven days, including offers
that come from friends, other bands or venues we already know. The Artist keeps the right to play
unpaid charity shows and in-store appearances, as long as the Agent is told in advance.

## 6. What the Agent does
- Finds, negotiates and confirms Engagements, and sends a written offer sheet for each one.
- Routes tours sensibly; no more than 450 miles between two consecutive shows without our approval.
- Chases late payments from promoters when the Artist asks.
- Gives the Artist a quarterly report of offers received, accepted and declined.

## 7. What the Artist does
- Turns up, on time, and plays the agreed set length.
- Keeps the Agent informed of availability, studio time and holidays.
- Does not cancel a confirmed Engagement except for illness, injury or events outside its control.
  Where a member is injured, the Artist may use a session player, as long as the promoter is told.

## 8. Offers, approvals and radius clauses
No Engagement is confirmed without the Artist's approval, which may be by email or message. The Agent
will flag any radius clause in an offer. The Artist will not accept a radius clause wider than 60 miles
or longer than 90 days before a show without the band agreeing as a whole.

## 9. Deposits and the client account
Deposits paid by promoters are held in the Agent's client account and passed on, less commission, after
the show. Where a promoter pays the Artist directly, the Artist invoices the promoter and the Agent
invoices the Artist for its commission.

## 10. Commission
The Agent's commission is 12% of the gross fee of every Engagement, headline shows, support slots and
festivals alike. No commission is due on merchandise sales, recording income, publishing or sync
licences. Where the gross fee of an Engagement is under £300, commission is a flat £30 instead.
Commission is due within 14 days of the Agent's invoice.

## 11. Ending the agreement
Either side may end this agreement by giving 90 days' written notice, but not before 2027-03-01 (the end
of the first twelve months). Either side may end it at once if the other commits a serious breach and
does not put it right within 21 days of being told in writing. If the Agent's named contact, Tom Reyes,
leaves Harbourline, the Artist may end the agreement with 30 days' notice.

## 12. After it ends
The Agent is still owed commission on Engagements confirmed before the end date, even if they take place
after it, for up to six months. Nothing else survives the end of the agreement apart from section 13.

## 13. Confidentiality
Both sides keep the fees and terms of this agreement private, except from their accountants, lawyers and,
for the Artist, the band members.

## 14. General
English law applies. Changes must be in writing and signed by both sides. Notices go to the email
addresses at the top of the signed copy.
