# Van Hire Terms

Pennine Van Hire standard terms, as they apply to our bookings ([[Van Rental]]).

- Mileage: 250 miles a day included, 18p a mile after that
- Fuel: return full or pay their rate plus a refuelling fee
- Damage excess: £1,000 unless we buy the waiver (we never do)
- Deposit: amount set per booking
- Late return: £60 per half day
- Drivers: over 25, licence held 3 years. Cass, Priya and Dan are on the list.
