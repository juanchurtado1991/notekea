# Rebalancing log

Targets: SAU 40%, NGI 40%, BDX 20%. I check twice a year, in March and September, and only trade when a
fund is more than 5 percentage points away from its target. New money each month goes in at the target
split, which usually keeps things close enough without selling anything. When I do sell, I sell the fund
that is over and buy the one that is under, and nothing else. Every trade costs brokerage, so fewer trades
is better. Trades are recorded at the price I actually got, not the price on the day's close.

## How I work out the drift
1. Take the units from the Tallow holdings page.
2. Multiply by the last price shown in the app.
3. Divide each by the total to get the weights.
4. Compare with the targets; anything more than 5 points off triggers a rebalance.

I write the weights before and after so I can see whether the trades did what I meant them to.
