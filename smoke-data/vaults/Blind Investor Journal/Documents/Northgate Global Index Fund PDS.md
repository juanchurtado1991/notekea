# Northgate Global Index Fund PDS

My own summary of the product disclosure statement, section by section, copied out so I can search it.
Issued 2026-04-01 by the responsible entity, Northgate Fund Services Ltd.

## 1. About the fund
The fund aims to track the return of a broad index of large and mid-sized companies listed in developed
markets outside Australia, before fees and costs. It holds the shares in the index in about the same
weights, rather than picking companies. The currency exposure is not hedged, so the value in Australian
dollars moves with exchange rates as well as with share prices. The fund is offered on the exchange and
directly through the application form at the back of the PDS.

## 2. Who it is meant for
Investors who want exposure to international shares over a long period, who can accept that the value
can fall, sometimes for several years. The PDS suggests a minimum investment timeframe of seven years.
It is described as high risk on the standard risk measure, with a negative annual return expected in
about four to six years out of every twenty.

## 3. Risks
- Market risk: share markets fall, sometimes sharply and for long periods.
- Currency risk: a stronger Australian dollar lowers the value of the overseas holdings.
- Tracking risk: the fund may not match the index exactly, because of fees, costs and timing.
- Liquidity risk: in stressed markets the fund may suspend applications and withdrawals.
- Operational risk: systems, people and service providers can fail.
- Regulatory risk: tax and super rules can change and affect returns.
The PDS stresses that past performance is not a guide to future performance.

## 4. How the fund invests
The investment manager uses full replication where it can and sampling for the smallest holdings. It may
lend securities to approved borrowers for a fee, with collateral held. It does not borrow to invest. It
may use futures for a short time to stay fully invested when cash comes in, but not to gear the fund.

## 5. Investing and withdrawing directly
- Minimum initial investment: $5,000.
- Minimum additional investment: $500, or $100 a month by regular plan.
- Applications received by 2pm Sydney time on a business day get that day's price.
- Withdrawals are usually paid within five business days.
- Cooling-off: 14 days for direct retail investors; it does not apply to units bought on the exchange.
I buy through Tallow, so most of this section doesn't apply to me, but the cooling-off point matters.

## 6. Distributions
Distributions are paid half-yearly, for the periods ending 30 June and 31 December, usually within four
weeks of the period end. You can choose cash or reinvestment. Reinvested units are issued at the price at
the start of the next period, with no buy spread. The amount varies and can be nil in some periods.

## 7. Fees and costs
- Management fees and costs: 0.18% a year of the fund's net assets, taken out of the fund, not billed.
- Performance fee: none.
- Transaction costs: estimated 0.01% a year, on top of the management fee.
- Contribution fee, withdrawal fee, exit fee, establishment fee: nil.
- Switching fee: nil.
The PDS gives a worked example on a balance of $50,000 with $5,000 added during the year; total
cost of about $99 for the year on those assumptions.

## 8. Tax
The fund is an attribution managed investment trust. Investors are taxed on their share of the fund's
taxable income each year, whether it is paid as cash or reinvested. Foreign income tax offsets are
passed through where available. An annual tax statement is issued after 30 June. The PDS recommends
getting tax advice for your own situation.

## 9. Buy/sell spread
When you invest or withdraw directly, the price includes a buy/sell spread to cover the cost of buying
or selling the underlying shares. At the date of the PDS it is +0.08% on applications and -0.08% on
withdrawals. The spread is kept by the fund, not by the responsible entity, and can change without
notice in volatile markets.

## 10. Other information
- Responsible entity: Northgate Fund Services Ltd.
- Investment manager: Northgate Index Management Pty Ltd.
- Custodian: Harbourline Custody Pty Ltd, which holds the fund's assets separately from the responsible
  entity's own assets.
- Unit registry: Ledgerpoint Registry Services.
- Auditor: Penhallow & Co.
- Complaints go first to the responsible entity, then to the external dispute body if not resolved
  within 30 days.
- Labour, environmental, social and ethical considerations are not taken into account in selecting,
  keeping or selling investments, beyond what the index provider does.
