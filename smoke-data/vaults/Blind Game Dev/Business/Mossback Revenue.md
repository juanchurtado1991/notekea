# Mossback Revenue

Net revenue for Mossback in CAD, after store cuts and refunds, by month of 2026.

| Month | Steam | itch.io | eShop |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | 1,240 | 180 | 610 |
| February | 980 | 95 | 455 |
| March | 2,310 | 140 | 720 |
| April | 860 | 70 | 390 |
| May | 790 | 65 | 405 |
| June | 1,575 | 110 | 530 |

March was the spring sale (40% off on all three stores). June was the anniversary update.

## Not income
- March: Mossback was part of a charity bundle on itch.io. The bundle brought 300 CAD that went straight to the
  charity, so it is **not** counted in the table and is not income.

Payouts arrive about 30 days after the end of each month.
