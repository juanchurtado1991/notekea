# Fieldwork trip plan – Cape Arrow, March 2027

## Purpose
Survey urchin density, size structure and kelp cover at three sites around Cape Arrow, and collect adult urchins for
the next grazing trials (Chapter 3). The North Reef site sits inside one of the barrens polygons from
[[Mbeki 2025 - Urchin barrens mapping]], so it gives me a barrens site to compare with a kelp site.

## Survey design
At each site we lay two 30 m transects parallel to shore. Along each transect we place a 1 m² quadrat every 5 m
(7 quadrats per transect, 14 per site). In each quadrat we count all urchins, note whether they are exposed or in
cracks, and estimate kelp cover. We measure test diameter of the first 30 urchins per site with calipers. Same design
as Mbeki so the numbers can be compared.

Collections: up to 40 adult urchins in total, only from North Reef, kept in the station's flow-through tanks and
driven back in the cooler on the last day.

separately and is included in the boat time line of the travel budget.
