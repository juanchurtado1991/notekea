# Experiment 01 – Thermal ramp (four-arm plutei)

**Goal:** upper thermal limit of four-arm plutei from the 2026-09-08 spawning (see [[Spawning log]]).

**Setup:** larvae reared at 14 °C, ramped at 1 °C per hour (same as [[Kowalski 2024 - Larval heat tolerance]]) to each
target, then held 48 h. Survival counted on a 0.5 mL subsample.

| Temperature (°C) | Replicates | Survival 48 h (%) |
|---|---|---|
| 14 | 4 | 92 |
| 18 | 4 | 88 |
| 22 | 4 | 71 |
| 24 | 4 | 54 |
| 26 | 4 | 19 |
| 28 | 4 | 0 |

**Result:** logistic fit gives my LT50 estimate for four-arm plutei: **24.3 °C**.

Observations:
- 2026-09-23: beaker 3 at 26 °C had a film of bacteria, counted anyway.
- Plutei at 24 °C had visibly shorter postoral arms.
- Only four-arm plutei tested this time; prism stage not run.
