# Tanaka 2022 – Respirometry methods

Closed-chamber respirometry for single echinoderm larvae and small batches.

- Recommends glass microchambers of 2 mL or less for batches of 20–50 larvae.
- Optical oxygen sensor spots, calibrated at 0 % and 100 % air saturation before each run.
- Discard any chamber whose blank drift is above 5 % of the measured slope.
- Measure for at least 40 minutes; use the slope between minute 10 and minute 40.
