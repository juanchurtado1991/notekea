# Mbeki 2025 – Urchin barrens mapping

**Question:** How much of the northern coast has turned from kelp forest into urchin barrens since 2014?

**Method:** drone imagery at low tide plus diver transects (30 m transects, 1 m² quadrats every 5 m) to ground-truth the images.

**Main result:** 312 hectares of barrens mapped along 40 km of coast; barrens patches had a median urchin density of 18 per m².

**My take:** good maps, weak statistics. The North Reef site in my [[Fieldwork trip plan]] is inside one of their barrens polygons, so their density numbers give me a baseline.
