# Fischer 2021 – Grazing rates

Still reading (stopped at the discussion).

- Lab feeding trials with adult purple urchins on giant kelp blades.
- Mean grazing rate 0.8 g wet weight per urchin per day at 14 °C.
- Rate roughly doubled at 18 °C, then dropped at 21 °C.
- Starved urchins (7 days) ate about 40 % more on the first day.
