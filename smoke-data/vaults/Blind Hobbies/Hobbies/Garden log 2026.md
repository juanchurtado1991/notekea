# Garden log 2026

My allotment at Hortas do Vale Escuro, plot 17, next to [[Rita Fonseca]] on plot 18. Beds are in [[Beds plan]];
seeds in [[Seed orders]].

## 2026-01-18
Cold, clear. Turned the compost and covered bed A with cardboard to kill the grass. Broad beans from November are up
and look sturdy. Rita lent me her wheelbarrow, which has a flat tyre, so I carried everything in buckets.

## 2026-02-21
Seeds arrived (tomatoes and peppers). Sowed them indoors on the kitchen windowsill in egg boxes. Three tomato
varieties: Coração de Boi, a cherry type and a yellow pear.

## 2026-03-12
Planted seed potatoes in bed C, two rows. Tomato seedlings have their first true leaves. Bought a second water butt
from the association for 15 €.

## 2026-04-09
Planted out the tomatoes in bed B, twelve plants, with canes. Lettuces in bed D. Slugs ate four lettuces in two
nights; beer traps set.

## 2026-05-02
Strong wind broke two tomato canes; tied them again. Broad beans harvested, a full basket, and shelled them on
Rita's bench while she told me about her grandson. Courgettes planted in bed A.

## 2026-05-27
First courgette flowers. Fried some stuffed with cheese. Watering every second evening now.

## 2026-06-18
Back from the Azores to find bed D bolted and the courgettes enormous: picked 3.5 kg of courgettes and gave most of
them away. Rita watered for me while I was away; owe her a dinner.

## 2026-07-02
Dug the potatoes. A nice crop, clean skins, no scab. I didn't weigh them — two full crates, stored in the dark
under the stairs.

## 2026-07-14
First proper tomato picking. Tomatoes: 2.4 kg, mostly the cherry ones. Made a quick sauce.

## 2026-07-28
Tomatoes: 3.1 kg. The Coração de Boi are huge this year, one weighed almost 600 g on its own. Courgettes: 2.2 kg.

## 2026-08-09
Heatwave. Watering every evening, shade net over bed B. Tomatoes: 3.6 kg. Some split from the heat; made jam with
the yellow pear ones, which was a strange idea that worked.

## 2026-08-25
Tomatoes: 1.5 kg, the plants are tired. Started pulling the worst ones. Blight spots on two plants, burnt the leaves.

## 2026-09-14
Cleared bed B completely. Compost on everything. Sowed green manure (phacelia) in bed A.

## 2026-10-20
Planted garlic in bed C, 40 cloves, from the order that came on the 2nd. Broad beans sown in bed B. Harvest of garlic
expected in June 2027.

## 2026-11-07
Rain at last. Ordered fleece for the frost nights. Rita and I shared a thermos of tea and complained about the
association's new rules on bonfires.
