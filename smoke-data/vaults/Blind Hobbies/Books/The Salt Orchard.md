# The Salt Orchard

A family of salt farmers in the Algarve over three generations. Slow first hundred pages, then I could not stop.

- Best character: the grandmother who keeps the accounts in a school exercise book.
- Discussed at the [[Book club]] on 2026-10-01; Inês hosted and brought almond cake.
- Quote I copied on the train: "the sea gives, but it keeps a ledger".
