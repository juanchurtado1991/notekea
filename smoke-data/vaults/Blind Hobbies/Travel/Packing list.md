# Packing list

My standard list, reused for every trip.

## Always
- [ ] Passport and citizen card
- [ ] Phone charger and the travel adapter
- [ ] Reading glasses (two pairs)
- [ ] Medication box for the week
- [ ] A paperback for the plane

## Clothes
- [ ] Walking shoes
- [ ] Two warm layers
- [ ] Scarf

## Camera
- [ ] Camera body, 35 mm lens, two batteries
- [ ] Small tripod if there is room
