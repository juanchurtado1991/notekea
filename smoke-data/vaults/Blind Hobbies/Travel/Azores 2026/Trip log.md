# Trip log — Azores 2026

Seven nights on São Miguel, a week I had been promising myself since I retired. I stayed at the Casa da Lagoa
guesthouse outside Ponta Delgada and hired a small car for the whole week.

## Costs

| Item | Amount (€) |

| Flights Lisbon–Ponta Delgada | 212 |
| Car rental, 7 days | 287 |
| Casa da Lagoa guesthouse, 7 nights | 588 |

I didn't keep track of what I spent on meals; it was mostly cash at small places.

## 2026-06-08 — Arrival
The plane came in low over green fields cut into squares by black stone walls and hydrangea hedges. The car was
waiting at the airport desk, a little white thing with a gearbox that complained in second. The guesthouse owner,
Senhora Fátima, gave me the room at the back with a view of the hills and a jar of her own passion fruit jam. Dinner
was soup and bread at a café in the village, then bed by ten. I wrote three postcards and did not post any of them.

## 2026-06-09 — Sete Cidades
Drove up to the Vista do Rei viewpoint early. Fog first, then it lifted all at once and the two lakes appeared, one
green and one blue, exactly like the postcards. Walked part of the crater rim, about eight kilometres, and ate a
sandwich on a wall with cows watching me. Photographed the lakes with the 35 mm and wished I had brought the tripod.
In the afternoon I drove down to Mosteiros and watched the sea against the black rocks until the light went orange.

## 2026-06-10 — Furnas
Furnas valley: the fumaroles smell of eggs and the ground hisses. Watched them lift the cozido pots out of the hot
earth at Lagoa das Furnas and then ate it at a long table with a Dutch family. Terra Nostra garden in the afternoon,
an hour in the orange iron water of the big pool. My silver ring went black from the water and took a week to come
back. Bought a bag of bolos lêvedos for the guesthouse breakfast.

## 2026-06-11 — Tea plantation and the north coast
Visited the Gorreana-style tea estate on the north coast (the old family one with the machines from the 1800s),
walked the rows of tea, bought two packets of green tea for Kenji, who will laugh at me for bringing tea to a
Japanese man. Swam at a natural pool at Caloura in the late afternoon; the water was cold enough to stop my breath.

## 2026-06-12 — Lagoa do Fogo
A long hard walk down to the lake shore and back up. Clouds came and went over the crater. My knees complained all
evening; Senhora Fátima gave me a cream that smelled of eucalyptus. Read half of a novel on the terrace and slept
early. Note to self: walking poles next time.

## 2026-06-13 — Whale watching
Out of Ponta Delgada harbour at 09:00 with a small company run by a marine biologist and her brother. The boat was
the Gaivota Azul, an orange rigid inflatable with twelve of us on board in yellow coats. We saw a big group of common
dolphins first, maybe forty animals, and then — after an hour of nothing — a sperm whale blowing at the surface,
which dived with its tail up right in front of us. The biologist said it was a female they know and call Maria.
I cried a little, which I am not ashamed of. Seasick on the way back; ginger biscuits did nothing.

## 2026-06-14 — Last full day
Ponta Delgada town: the city gates, the market, a pineapple greenhouse where they still smoke the plants to make them
flower. Bought a pineapple to carry home in my lap. Last dinner with Senhora Fátima and her husband, who insisted
on opening a bottle of wine from Pico.

## 2026-06-15 — Home
Returned the car with a scratch on the bumper that was already there (I had photographed it on day one — good habit).
Flight home in the afternoon. #travel/done
