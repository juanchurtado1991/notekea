# Itinerary — Scotland 2027

Ten nights in Scotland in May, with two of them near [[Margaret Doyle]] in Pitlochry. Bookings are tracked in
[[Travel/Scotland 2027/Bookings|Bookings]].

## Day 1 — 2027-05-14
- Flight Lisbon → Edinburgh at 10:40, land 13:35.
- Check in at The Grey Close Inn, Old Town.

## Day 2 — 2027-05-15
- Old Town on foot: the Royal Mile, the closes, the National Museum.
- Evening: Arthur's Seat if the weather is kind.

## Day 3 — 2027-05-16
- Train Edinburgh → Inverness at 09:33, about three and a half hours.
- Afternoon by the River Ness.

## Day 4 — 2027-05-17
- Pick up the hire car in Inverness. Loch Ness south shore, Fort Augustus.

## Day 5 — 2027-05-18
- Drive to Pitlochry. Two nights at a B&B there.
- Tea with Margaret and a look at her garden.

## Day 6 — 2027-05-19
- With Margaret: the fish ladder, the dam, Blair Atholl.

## Day 7 — 2027-05-20
- Drive west to Glencoe. Short walks only.

## Day 8 — 2027-05-21
- Glencoe to Glasgow, drop the car.

## Day 9 — 2027-05-22
- Glasgow: the cathedral, the botanic gardens.

## Day 10 — 2027-05-23
- Train back to Edinburgh, last evening.

## Day 11 — 2027-05-24
- Flight home.
