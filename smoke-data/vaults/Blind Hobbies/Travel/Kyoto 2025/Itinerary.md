# Itinerary — Kyoto 2025

Ten nights in Kyoto in cherry blossom season. Base: Hotel Kamogawa Rest, a small hotel by the river, ten minutes'
walk from Sanjo station. Costs are in [[Travel/Kyoto 2025/Costs|Costs]].

## Day 1 — 2025-04-02
- Land at Kansai 15:40 after the change in Frankfurt.
- Airport express to Kyoto at 16:16, taxi to the hotel.
- Early night. #jetlag

## Day 2 — 2025-04-03
- Fushimi Inari at 07:00 before the crowds; walked to the top and back down in three hours.
- Afternoon: Nishiki market, bought pickles and a small knife.

## Day 3 — 2025-04-04
- Day trip to Nara. Train at 09:15 from Kyoto Station, back around 17:00.
- Deer park, Todai-ji, lunch of kakinoha-zushi.

## Day 4 — 2025-04-05
- Philosopher's Path from end to end, Ginkaku-ji.
- Evening walk along the river under the blossoms.

## Day 5 — 2025-04-06
- Arashiyama: bamboo grove early, the monkey park, a boat on the river.

## Day 6 — 2025-04-07
- Rain all day. Kyoto National Museum and the manga museum.
- Dinner at the hotel bar with Margaret, a Scottish lady staying on the same floor — we talked until midnight
  about teaching and gardens. See [[Margaret Doyle]].

## Day 7 — 2025-04-08
- Tea ceremony class booked for 10:00 — cancelled by the school because of the rain the day before flooded their
  garden room. Refund promised.
- Walked Gion and Higashiyama instead.

## Day 8 — 2025-04-09
- Day trip to Himeji castle by shinkansen, 08:40.

## Day 9 — 2025-04-10
- Kiyomizu-dera, last souvenirs, sent a parcel of books home by post.

## Day 10 — 2025-04-11
- Slow day. Botanical garden, packing.

## Day 11 — 2025-04-12
- Airport express at 07:45, flight home at 11:05.
