# Isla — progress record, 2026

This is the running record I keep for Isla. It is what I would show a reviewer if the Ministry ever
asked how she is going, so I try to write something every few weeks and keep test results in
[[Kids/Isla/Test results]].

## Maths
Isla started Kowhai Maths 6 in January. She flew through the number chapters but slowed down in
geometry; angles in polygons took her most of September. Since [[Hannah Ngata]] started tutoring her
in August she asks better questions and is far less likely to give up on a hard problem. Hannah's
comment after the October sessions: "Strong on algebra now, needs more practice setting out working."
Plan for the rest of the year: finish chapter 10 by the end of term, then a holiday revision pack.

## English
Reading is her strong suit. She finished Kites over Kaikoura in August and wrote a review that the
co-op put in its newsletter. Writing is improving but her essays still wander; the persuasive essay
unit this term is meant to fix that. Spelling is fine. Handwriting is messy but she types most things
now and I've stopped fighting it.

## Science
Forces and motion this term. She designed her own friction ramp with Finn's toy cars and kept a
proper lab book for it, which is a first. The science fair project is about bridges (see
[[Science fair]]). She would like to do more chemistry, which I can't really teach at home.

## Te reo Maori
Joins the family te reo sessions on Wednesdays. Comfortable with greetings, numbers and her pepeha.
She led the waiata at the co-op in October.

## Physical education
Swimming every Friday at the Matangi pool, bike rides, and she has started running with me on
Sunday mornings. Wants to try the junior park run in summer.

## Social
Co-op twice a month, youth group on Thursday nights, and a pen pal in Dunedin. Still a bit shy with
new adults but fine with other kids.

## Things to watch
- Gets stuck on worded maths problems; reading the question twice helps.
- Tired on Mondays after weekend sport. Keep Monday mornings lighter.
- Screen time creeping up again in the evenings.

## Notes from the term so far
- 2026-10-14: settled back in after the holidays quickly.
- 2026-10-29: first full lab write-up without help.
- 2026-11-06: 89% on the linear equations test, her best maths result this year.

## Looking ahead to 2027
We talked about next year at dinner on 2026-11-01. Isla wants a proper chemistry course with labs,
and Hannah suggested the distance option rather than me trying to fake it at the kitchen table. The
plan we agreed: Isla enrols with **Rimu Online School** for Level 1 chemistry in Term 1, two online
lessons a week plus a practical day each term at their Hamilton campus. Enrolment opens in December;
I need to send them a copy of her exemption letter and a short summary of her science so far.
Everything else stays at home.
