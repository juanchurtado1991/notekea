# Hull History Centre

Two-day trip, 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15. Train from Waverley, stayed at a B&B near the station.

## What I saw
- Humber Dock Company minute books 1830–1850 — timber yard leases, complaints about Leith competition
- Hull Trinity House shipping registers — three Leith-owned brigs listed in 1839
- Merchant's letter book (Pease family) — not much on the Baltic
