# Reading list

Core reading for my tutorial groups, one or two items a week. Copies on the course page.

- Week 2 — course handbook, essay guidance
- Week 3 — A. Morrow, *A Union of Interests* (2002), ch. 1
- Week 4 — J. Pettigrew, *The Tobacco Lords Revisited* (2010), ch. 3
- Week 5 — L. Strachan, *Linen and Improvement* (1995), ch. 2
- Week 6 — S. Okafor, *Sugar, Slaves and Scottish Fortunes* (2018), ch. 4
- Week 7 — no reading (essay clinic)
- Week 8 — D. Mair, *Leaving Caledonia* (2007), ch. 6
- Week 9 — R. Talbot, *Bread and Ballots* (1998), ch. 4
- Week 10 — F. Inglis, *Juteopolis* (2013), ch. 2
- Week 11 — revision, past papers
