# The Flax Frontier

Margaret Ellwood, *The Flax Frontier: Baltic Fibre and Scottish Mills* (2004).

## Argument
East-coast Scottish linen depended on Riga and Pernau flax from the 1820s, and the agents in Leith and
Dundee who bought it on credit carried most of the risk.

## Notes
- Ch. 2: Fife spinners and their Leith agents — very close to my Kirkcaldy letters.
- Ch. 5: prices at Dundee, 1830–1860, with a long table.
- Little on how the agents were paid; I think she misreads the commission system.
