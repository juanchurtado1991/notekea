# Ports and Paperwork

Jonas Vik, *Ports and Paperwork: Customs Records and the Historian* (2019).

The best guide to customs ledgers I have found. How the quarterly accounts were compiled, what the
clerks left out, and why the figures for bonded goods look wrong.

- Explains the low-duty entries I keep finding (bonded cargoes, p. 88).
- Good on the difference between loads and lasts.
- Method chapter for [[Chapter 1 Introduction]] should lean on this.
