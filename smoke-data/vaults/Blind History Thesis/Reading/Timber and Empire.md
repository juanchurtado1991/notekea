# Timber and Empire

Priya Natarajan, *Timber and Empire* (2016).

## Argument
The colonial preference on timber shifted British imports from the Baltic to British North America
between 1810 and 1840, and the duty reforms of the 1840s only partly reversed it. Strong on policy,
weaker on the ports themselves.

- Chapter 3: the duty tables in the appendix (pp. 301–309)
- Comparing Leith with Liverpool and Hull
