# Abstract

Submitted to Nordwind Data Days 2027 for [[Nordwind Data Days talk]]. Accepted 2026-11-04.

> **Forecasts people actually trust**
>
> Most forecasts fail not because they are wrong, but because nobody acts on them. Drawing on projects in
> logistics, healthcare and media, this talk shows three habits that make planners use a forecast instead of
> their own spreadsheet: intervals people can read, stability between runs, and overrides treated as data.
> Includes a live demo on public bike-share data.
