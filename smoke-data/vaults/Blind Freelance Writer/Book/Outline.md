# Outline

*Rented Lives*. One household or one building per chapter.

- Chapter 1 — The deposit (a couple in Phibsborough, two months' rent up front)
- Chapter 2 — The register (a landlord who never registered)
- Chapter 3 — The viewing (forty people on a Tuesday evening)
- Chapter 4 — The zones (Stoneybatter, five years on)
- Chapter 5 — The sale (an eviction because the landlord is selling)
- Chapter 6 — The student bed (a purpose-built block and its prices)
- Chapter 7 — The commute (moving to Kildare to keep a Dublin job)
- Chapter 8 — The inspector (a day with a council housing inspector)
- Chapter 9 — The kitchen table (what renters want)
