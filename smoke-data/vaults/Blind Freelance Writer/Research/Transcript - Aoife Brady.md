# Transcript — Aoife Brady, 2026-10-23

Tolka Water Lab, 55 minutes. On the record. She wants to see her quotes before print.

**OK:** How did you come to sample the stream at Portrane?
**AB:** A swimmer's group got in touch with us in the summer. They'd noticed a smell after rain and a film on the
water near the outflow. We do a certain amount of citizen-science work, so we went out three times in August and
September.

**OK:** Where did you take samples?
**AB:** Three points. Upstream of the farm, beside the farm on the east edge, and about 700 metres downstream where
the stream meets the estuary. Upstream is our control.

**OK:** What did you test for?
**AB:** The standard set: ammonia, nitrates, phosphates, a few metals, hydrocarbons, and conductivity. We also
looked at the invertebrates in the stream bed, which tells you more about the longer story than a single sample.

**OK:** And upstream, the control?
**AB:** Upstream looked like a normal lowland stream. Nothing to worry about. Ammonia under 0.2 milligrams per litre,
which is what you'd expect.

**OK:** And downstream?
**AB:** Downstream it's diluted, but still elevated. The estuary does a lot of the work for you there.

**OK:** The invertebrates?
**AB:** Beside the farm there's almost nothing living in the bed. Upstream it's healthy. That's not one bad day,
that's years.

**OK:** Can you say where it comes from?
**AB:** I can say what's in the water and where it gets worse. I can't say who put what in the ground. That's your
job. What I can say is that the change happens along the stretch that runs beside the farm, and not above it.

**OK:** Hydrocarbons?
**AB:** Present beside the farm, absent upstream. Low levels, but they shouldn't be there at all.

**OK:** Would a soil survey help?
**AB:** It would tell you the depth and the make-up of the fill. It's expensive. Several thousand euro.

**OK:** And the figure that worried you most, beside the farm?
**AB:** Ammonia. Beside the farm, on the September sample after heavy rain, we measured 38 mg/L. For a stream like
that you'd want it well under one. That's the reading I'd stand over in print.

**OK:** Will you sample again?
**AB:** Yes, a second round in November. I can talk you through it when the results are in.
