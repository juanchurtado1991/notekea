# Outline

The Portrane Fill, for Northlight. 6,000 words.

## I. The field
Open on the coast road at dawn, the gate, the height of the ground.

## II. The night runs
The former driver's account. Cash, dockets, the route.

Aoife Brady's samples. What's in the water and what it means downstream.

## V. The postponed inspections
The former inspector, the FOI, the council's answer (if any).

## VI. Who answers for it
Right of reply. What happens next.
