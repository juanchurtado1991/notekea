# Rate card

What I ask for, and what I'll accept.

- Floor: €0.40 per word for print, €0.30 for online if the piece is short (under 1,000 words).
- Long reads (3,000+): ask €0.80, settle at €0.70.
- Kill fee: 50% of the agreed fee.
- Expenses on top for travel outside Dublin.
- Re-use / syndication: 25% of the original fee.
