# Terms and Conditions — Clara Whitmore Interiors

These terms apply to every project unless the letter of appointment says otherwise. Version 3, updated June 2026.

## 1. Appointment
1.1 The studio is appointed by a signed letter of appointment that sets out the scope, the design fee and the stages
of work. Work starts when the signed letter and the first design fee invoice have both been received.
1.2 Any work outside the agreed scope is charged at the hourly rate in force at the time, or by a separate letter of
appointment for larger additions.
1.3 The client agrees to give the studio reasonable access to the property, including for contractors, deliveries
and photography, at times agreed in advance.

## 2. Design fee and stages
2.1 The design fee is split into stages: concept, developed design, and delivery. Each stage is invoiced at its start.
2.2 Revisions: two rounds of revisions are included in each stage. Further rounds are charged at the hourly rate.
2.3 Drawings, boards and specifications remain the property of the studio until all invoices are paid in full.

## 3. Procurement
3.1 The studio can order furniture, fabrics, lighting and accessories on the client's behalf. A procurement fee is
charged on the net value of these orders and shown on a separate invoice.
3.2 Orders are only placed once the client has approved the item in writing (email or text is enough) and paid any
deposit required by the supplier.
3.3 Custom and made-to-order items, including upholstery made up in a chosen fabric, need a deposit of 50% before the
order is placed. Custom items cannot be cancelled once production has started.
3.4 Stock items may be cancelled within 14 days of the order date. The supplier's own restocking fee, if any, is
passed on to the client at cost.
3.5 Lead times are given by suppliers and are estimates. The studio will chase suppliers and keep the client
informed, but is not liable for supplier delays.

## 4. Contractors
4.1 Contractors recommended by the studio are engaged and paid by the client directly unless agreed otherwise. The
studio coordinates their schedule and checks the quality of finishes against the design.
4.2 The client is responsible for any permissions, party wall agreements and building management notices required
before works start.
4.3 Day rates for contractors are quoted in advance and confirmed in writing before each booking.

## 5. Payment
5.1 Invoices are payable within 14 days of the invoice date.
5.2 Late payment: interest is charged at 2% per month on any amount unpaid 30 days after the invoice date.
5.3 The studio may pause work, including procurement, while any invoice is overdue.
5.4 Payments are made by bank transfer only. The studio never asks for payment details by text.

## 6. Deliveries and inspection
6.1 Deliveries are made to the project address or to the studio's storage. The client or the studio must be present
to receive large items.
6.2 Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photographs, so the supplier can be claimed against.
6.3 Items delivered more than 21 days before their installation date are kept at the studio's storage unit in
Bermondsey. Storage is charged to the client at £45 per week per project, starting from the 22nd day.
6.4 The studio is not liable for items supplied by the client or by suppliers the client chose directly.

## 7. Handover and snagging
7.1 At the end of the delivery stage the studio walks the project with the client and agrees a snagging list.
7.2 The client reports any further defects within 10 working days of handover. After that, defects are dealt with
directly between the client and the contractor or supplier.
7.3 Styling items lent for photography are collected within one week of the shoot.

## 8. Photography and publicity
8.1 The studio may photograph the finished project for its portfolio and website. No personal items, faces or
addresses are shown without the client's written consent.
8.2 The client may ask for the project to be kept private, in which case it is shown only in private presentations.

## 9. Cancellation by either party
9.1 Either party may end the appointment by giving 14 days' written notice.
9.2 On cancellation the client pays for all work done up to the end of the notice period and for any orders already
placed that cannot be cancelled.
9.3 Drawings and specifications are released to the client only once these amounts are paid.

## 10. Liability and insurance
10.1 The studio holds professional indemnity insurance. Its liability is limited to the value of the design fee for
the project concerned.
10.2 The studio is not liable for the work of contractors engaged by the client, though it will help resolve issues.

## 11. Disputes
11.1 If the client disputes an invoice or any part of the work, the client tells the studio in writing within 14 days
and the undisputed part of the invoice remains payable.
11.2 Any dispute that cannot be settled between the parties is first referred to mediation through an independent
mediator agreed by both parties, with the cost shared equally, before either party starts court proceedings.
11.3 These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
