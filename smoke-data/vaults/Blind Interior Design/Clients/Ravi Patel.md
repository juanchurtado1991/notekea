## Background
Ravi found the studio through the Southwark design open day in June. He works in shipping insurance and travels
a lot, so most approvals happen by text in the evening. Anjali is a violinist and the music room on the first floor
matters more to her than anything else in the house — acoustics first, looks second.

The house is a four-storey Victorian terrace that was split into bedsits in the 1980s and turned back into a family
home by the previous owners, badly. Ceilings are original on the ground floor only. The brief is a warm, layered
scheme: deep greens, aged brass, walnut, plenty of texture. They want it to feel lived in, not like a show home.

## Scope
1. Living room and hall — full scheme, curtains, sofa, lighting.
2. Kitchen-diner — lighting, dining table and chairs, no new kitchen units.
3. Main bedroom — blinds, headboard, bedside lighting.
4. Music room — acoustic panels, reupholstered armchairs (Greta).
5. Nursery — out of scope for now (maybe 2027).

## Likes / dislikes
- Likes: moss and olive greens, brass, walnut, velvet, ribbed glass.
- Dislikes: grey kitchens, chrome, anything glossy, "hotel" headboards.
- Anjali wants nothing above the piano that could rattle.
