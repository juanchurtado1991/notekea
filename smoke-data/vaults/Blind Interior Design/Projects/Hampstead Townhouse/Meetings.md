# Client meetings — Hampstead Townhouse

## Minutes

### 2026-08-01 — appointment meeting
Present: Ravi, Anjali, me. Went through the letter of appointment line by line. They asked about what happens if
an order arrives damaged; I pointed them to the terms. Agreed a total budget of £48,000 including the design fee,
split as in [[Projects/Hampstead Townhouse/Budget]]. Ravi wanted to know if the nursery could be added later; told
him yes, as a separate letter of appointment. Anjali asked for the music room to be done before the living room so
she can practise over the winter. Not possible because Greta's slot is in December — explained, she was fine with
it. First design fee invoice to go out today.

### 2026-09-14 — furniture sign-off
Showed the Hollis table in walnut and the Harrow chairs. Both approved on the spot. Long talk about the sofa: they
like the Elm frame but could not decide on the fabric. Took away the Moss, Olive and Rust velvet samples. Ravi asked
whether the chairs could have arms; Oakbarrow does not do an armed Harrow, so we kept the plain version. Lead time
quoted at five to six weeks.

### 2026-10-03 — site visit
Walked the whole house with Ravi and Anjali. Agreed the Moss velvet for the curtains and, later by text, for the
sofa. Measured the living room windows again — the bay is 40 mm narrower than the old survey. Checked the hall
plaster: Kwame will need to cut back to brick in two places. Anjali asked that nothing be fixed above the piano.
Ravi asked for brass, not chrome, on every switch plate. Discussed the alcoves either side of the chimney breast:
oak shelving, oiled. Talked through the kitchen lighting: three pendants over the table, two wall lights by the
dresser. They want dimmers everywhere. Agreed that the hall runner waits until after the plastering.

### 2026-10-24 — phone call
Short call with Ravi. He has seen a boucle he likes for the bedroom chair; we ordered six metres of Chalk to test
on 2026-10-26, then cancelled when Anjali said it looked like a sheep. Refund requested from Linden.
