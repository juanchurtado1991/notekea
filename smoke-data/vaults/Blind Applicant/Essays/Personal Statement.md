# Personal Statement

Common App prompt 5: an accomplishment, event or realization that sparked a period of growth.
Limit: 650 words.

## The idea
The bike library. In ninth grade I started fixing donated bikes in our garage so kids on our street
could borrow them. It turned into a real thing: a shelf of parts, a sign-out sheet on the fence, and
a waiting list. The essay is about learning that fixing things is not the same as fixing problems:
the bikes kept breaking because nobody had a place to keep them dry, so the real fix was a shed, a
schedule and two other kids who learned how to true a wheel.

## Outline (current)
1. Opening: the morning the waiting list hit twenty names and I had three working bikes.
2. The garage and the first bikes. Dad's old tools. The derailleur I ruined.
3. The turn: bikes coming back rusted. Realizing the problem was storage, not bolts.
4. Building the shed with Mr. Okafor from next door. The permit question.
5. Teaching Jonah and Priya to do repairs so it does not depend on me.
6. Ending: what engineering means to me now, systems over parts.
