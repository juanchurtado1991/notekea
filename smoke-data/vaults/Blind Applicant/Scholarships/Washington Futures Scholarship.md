# Washington Futures Scholarship

State-wide, for students going into STEM at a Washington school. Only counts if I end up at
Rainier State, Sound Valley, Evergreen Ridge or Olympic Coast.

- Two essays: one on a challenge, one on career goals (500 words each).
- Transcript upload, FAFSA required.
- Finalists interview in March.
