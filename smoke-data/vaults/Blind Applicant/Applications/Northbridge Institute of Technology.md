# Northbridge Institute of Technology

My reach school. Early Action, non-binding. Submitted on 2026-10-30, two days before the deadline.
Decisions come out in mid-December.

## Why I want it
- The mechanical engineering program starts hands-on in the first semester: every first-year builds a
  small autonomous vehicle in the intro design course.
- The maker space is open 24 hours and first-years get badge access after one safety class.
- Co-op program: two six-month placements are optional, and most students who do them graduate in
  five years instead of four. I need to decide if I want that; it makes the total cost higher but the
  co-op pay helps.
- Undergraduate research: the fluid dynamics lab takes first-years as paid assistants.
- The robotics club competes in the same league as my club at Lakeview, so I already know two of their
  members from the regional tournament.

## What worries me
- Cost. The net price calculator says around $21,900 a year (see [[Net Price Estimates]]), and they
  need the CSS Profile, which is a whole extra form. Details in [[FAFSA and CSS Profile]].
- Distance. A flight home is five or six hours with a connection. Mom already asked how many times a
  year I would come home.
- The weather. I have lived in Seattle my whole life; I know rain, not snow.
- Admit rate for EA last year was low. [[Hannah Lee]] keeps saying to treat it as a reach and to keep
  the regular list strong.

## Things I learned at the virtual tour (2026-07-18)
- First-year housing is guaranteed; most students live on the east campus.
- Freshmen take the same core: calculus, physics, chemistry, a writing seminar and the design course.
- The tour guide said the "big hack" weekend in March is the best part of the year.
- Financial aid office meets 100 % of demonstrated need, but they count home equity, which may hurt us.
