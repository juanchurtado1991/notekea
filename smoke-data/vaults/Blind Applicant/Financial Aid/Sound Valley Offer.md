# Sound Valley Offer

Admitted on rolling, and the aid offer came the same day.

## Cost of attendance (one year)
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Tuition and fees | $41,200 |
| Housing and food | $13,900 |
| Books | $1,100 |
| Personal and travel | $2,200 |
| **Total** | **$58,400** |

## Aid offered
| Award | Type | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Presidential Scholarship | merit, renewable with a 3.0 GPA | $18,000 |
| Sound Valley Grant | need-based grant | $9,500 |
| Federal Direct Loan | loan | $5,500 |
| Federal Work-Study | work | $2,800 |

Loans and work-study are not free money; [[Grace Lee]] says compare schools on grants and
scholarships only. Compare with [[Net Price Estimates]].
