# FAFSA and CSS Profile

## FAFSA
- Submitted on 2026-10-03, with Dad, at the kitchen table, in about two hours.
- Schools listed: Northbridge, Cascade Pacific, Sound Valley, Rainier State, Harbor Bay, Olympic Coast,
  Evergreen Ridge. Washington Futures also needs it.
- Dad used the tax return import tool, which filled most of the parent section.
- Mom is a contributor too, she had to make her own account and sign separately. That took a week
  because her account verification email went to spam.
- Confirmation email came the same night.

## CSS Profile
- Needed by Northbridge only (Harbor Bay dropped it this year).
- Northbridge deadline for the CSS Profile: 2026-11-15.
- It asks for things the FAFSA does not: home value, what we owe on the house, retirement accounts,
  Dad's small side business (the bike repair stuff he does on weekends counts, apparently).
- Fee for one school; I may qualify for a fee waiver, ask about it before paying.
- Status: started 2026-11-02, about 70 % done. Need Mom's pay stubs from October.

## Questions we had
- Does Grandma's savings bond count as my asset? Yes, it does, on both forms.
- Does my hardware store job count? Yes, my income goes in the student section.
- Do we list the car? No, cars do not count on the FAFSA.
- Should Mom and Dad file together? They file taxes jointly, so yes, the same numbers.

## Things [[Grace Lee]] told us
- Keep a PDF of every confirmation page.
- Schools can ask for verification documents later; do not panic if one does.
- Appeal letters are allowed after offers come in, if something changes or another school offers
  more. Sound Valley takes appeals by email.
- Outside scholarships may reduce a school grant at some places; ask each school how they handle it.

## Things [[Hannah Lee]] told us
- Run the calculators again after the CSS Profile is done; the numbers often move.
- Do not accept loans in the first offer reply until all the offers are in.
- Keep one spreadsheet with grants and scholarships only, in one column per school.

## Our result
The FAFSA Submission Summary came on 2026-10-09.
- Student Aid Index (SAI): 8,200.
- That means no Pell Grant for us, but we qualify for need-based aid at most private schools.
- Dad was surprised; he expected higher.
