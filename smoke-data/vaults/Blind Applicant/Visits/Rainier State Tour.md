# Rainier State Tour

Took the 6:40 bus from downtown, got in just before noon. Stayed one night at the hotel by campus.

## Campus tour (13:30)
- Huge campus, a lot of walking. The tour was almost two hours.
- The engineering quad is on the far side, next to the agriculture buildings.
- Dorms: the newer ones are suites with four people, the old ones are doubles with a shared bathroom
  down the hall. First-years mostly get the old ones.
- Dining hall was better than I expected; there is a late-night place open until 1:00.
- The rec center has a climbing wall and an indoor track.

## Engineering open house
- Direct admission into the major, no pre-major like at Cascade Pacific.
- Class sizes for intro courses are big (around 200), then drop to 30 or 40 in the major.
- Senior capstone projects are with local companies; one team built an irrigation controller for
  orchards, which is close to my soil moisture sensor.
- The robotics club is small and mostly juniors. They seemed happy to get new people.
- Co-op is not built in, but there are summer internships.

## What I liked
- The price. It is the lowest estimate on my list by a lot.
- The engineering professors talked to us like future students, not like customers.
- Being in-state; I can take the bus home for holidays.

## What I did not like
- Yakima is far from the water and from everyone I know.
- The campus felt empty on a Monday afternoon.
- The old dorms.

## Honors College info session (15:30)
Small room, about 25 students and parents. The director ran it herself.
- Honors students get priority registration and live in the honors hall in the second year.
- Honors classes are capped at 20.
- The application is a separate essay submitted with the main application, by the priority deadline.
- Prompt: describe a problem in your community and one thing you did about it.
- Limit: 350 words. She said they actually read the limit and stop at it.
- Decisions on Honors come with the admission decision.

## Afterwards
Dad called to ask how it went. I said a seven out of ten and he laughed.
Bus back the next morning at 8:15.
