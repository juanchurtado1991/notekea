# Warranty policy

This is the warranty I give on work done and bikes sold at Kettingwerk Fietsen. It sits on top of what the law
already gives a buyer, it never replaces it. Print a copy for the counter when it changes.

## 1. Who it covers

The warranty covers the person who paid for the repair or bought the bike, with the receipt or the ticket number.
If a bike changes owner, the warranty on repairs moves with the bike, but the new owner needs the original ticket
number. Without a ticket number I look the job up in the tickets by name and date; if I cannot find it, there is
no warranty claim, only goodwill.

## 2. Repairs and labour

Labour on a repair is guaranteed for 3 months: if the same fault comes back because of how I did the job, I fix
it again for free. This covers things like a wheel that goes out of true again within a few weeks, a cable that
slips, a hub that was adjusted too tight. It does not cover a new fault in the same part (a second puncture is a
new puncture).

When a warranty job needs extra work that has nothing to do with the original fault, that extra work is charged at
the normal rate, and I say so before I start.

## 3. Parts I sell or fit

New parts I fit carry 24 months against manufacturing defects, counted from the date on the ticket. That is the
general rule for everything that is not listed further down: lights, racks, stands, locks, hubs, saddles, pedals.

If the part turns out to be defective, I replace the part. The labour to swap it is free in the first 6 months
and charged at half the normal rate after that.

## 4. Wear parts

Some parts wear out with use, and wear is not a defect. These are not covered beyond the first ride check:

- tyres and inner tubes
- chains, cassettes, chainrings and sprockets
- brake pads and rim brake blocks
- cables and housing
- bar tape and grips

The first ride check means: if a new chain skips or a new pad squeals in the first week, bring it back and I look
at it for free. After that it is wear.

## 5. Frames and forks

Frames and forks follow the manufacturer's own frame warranty; I handle the claim for the customer but the
manufacturer decides. Crash damage, rust from standing outside and home modifications are never covered. I take
photos of the frame when the claim starts, and I keep them in the ticket.

## 6. Second-hand bikes

Second-hand bikes I sell (including old rental bikes from the [[Fleet]]) carry 3 months on the whole bike, wear
parts excluded. No warranty on bikes sold "as is", which must say so on the receipt.

## 7. Things that are never covered

- damage from a crash, a fall or vandalism
- damage from overloading (cargo bikes: the maximum load is on the frame sticker)
- parts the customer brought in themselves; I fit them, but the warranty is between them and their shop
- normal rust and wear on bikes kept outside
- anything changed or repaired by someone else after my job

## 8. How a claim works

1. The customer brings the bike in with the ticket number or receipt.
2. I open a new ticket with the tag warranty and link the original ticket.
3. If a supplier must decide (motors, frames, batteries), I send the claim the same week with photos and, for
   e-bikes, the diagnostic read-out.
4. While a supplier claim is open the customer can borrow a rental city bike for free, if one is free.
5. I write the outcome in the ticket and tell the customer by their preferred contact.

## 9. Electric bikes

E-bikes follow the rules above, with these additions:

- Motors: 24 months, claim through the supplier, diagnostic read-out required.
- Displays and controllers: 24 months.
- Batteries: 18 months from the date of sale, or until the battery drops below 60 % of its capacity, whichever
  comes first. Batteries lose capacity with age; that is not a defect.
- Chargers: 12 months.
- Software updates are free during the warranty.

## 10. Reporting a defect

A defect must be reported within 30 days of noticing it. A customer who keeps riding on a known fault for months
and then asks for a free repair gets a polite no, because riding on it usually makes it worse.

## 11. Changes

I can change this policy for new jobs. Jobs already done keep the policy that was on the counter on the day of
the ticket.
