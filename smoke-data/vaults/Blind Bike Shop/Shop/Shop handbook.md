# Shop handbook

Everything Ilse (or anyone covering for me) needs to run the shop on a Saturday or when I am out on a ride.

## Opening and closing

- Opening hours: Tuesday to Friday 09:30–18:00, Saturday 10:00–17:00. Closed Sunday and Monday.
- Keys: the front door key and the back door key are on the red ring. The alarm panel is just inside the back
  door; the code is not written down anywhere, ask me.
- When opening: lights, heater in the workshop, put the two display bikes outside, sign on the pavement.
- When closing: bikes inside, back door locked from the inside, cash counted, heater off, alarm on.

## The counter

- Every job gets a ticket number, even a quick pump-up. Write the customer's name and phone on the paper slip
  and make the note in `Tickets/` later.
- Quick jobs under 10 minutes (pumping, a saddle height, a loose bell) are free for regulars.
- Prices: parts from the [[Parts/Price list|parts price list]], rentals from the
  [[Rentals/Price list|rental price list]]. Labour is € 58 an hour, counted in quarter hours.
- Card terminal first; cash is fine; payment by transfer only for regulars, and then an invoice goes in
  [[Invoices]].

## Rentals

- Do the [[Rentals/Checklist|handover checklist]] with the customer standing there.
- Take the deposit before the bike leaves. ID is shown, never kept.
- A bike that comes back damaged: photos, note in the booking, deposit held until I look at it.

## The workshop

- Tools go back on the board, every time. Torque wrench back in its case.
- Bikes waiting for parts get a yellow tag with the ticket number on the handlebar.
- Bikes that are done get a green tag and stand by the front window.
- Never test ride a customer's e-bike on the dyke road, the speed cameras there do not care that it is a test.

## Phone and messages

- Answer with the shop name. If I am riding, take a message in the log for the day.
- Suppliers call back during the week; on Saturday just note it down.

## Cleaning

- Floor swept every evening; the degreaser tray emptied on Fridays.
- Rags go in the metal bin, never the paper bin.

## Waste

- General waste: grey containers on the street, Monday and Friday evenings.
- Cardboard: flattened, in the paper container on the corner.
- Old chains and metal parts: the scrap box under the bench; I take it to the scrapyard every few months.
- Used oil and degreaser: the red jerrycans, to the waste point on my list.
- Old tyres and inner tubes: bag them and put the bags by the back door on Thursday morning; Rubberkring picks
  them up then and recycles the rubber. Do not put them out on other days, the neighbours complain.

## If something goes wrong

- Customer unhappy: write it down, promise nothing, I will call them.
- Something broken in the shop: photo and a line in the log.
