# Logline and synopsis

**Logline:** A waitress on the graveyard shift at a harbor diner calls in to the overnight radio show she
has listened to for years, and the host realizes the voice on the line is the reason he never left town.

**Synopsis:** Nell works nights at the Harbor Diner. Every night the radio on the shelf plays Cal, the
overnight host. One night, washing her uniforms at the laundromat, she calls the show from the pay phone.
They talk on air until dawn. In the morning Cal walks into the diner for the first time.

Target runtime: about 14 minutes. Genre: quiet drama. See [[Salt and Static - Draft 3]].
