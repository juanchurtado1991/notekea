# Festival strategy

- Premiere in California (the Coastal Arts fund requires it). Lantern Bay first, it is in state and
  programs quiet dramas.
- Do not burn the premiere on a small showcase. If Lantern Bay passes, Night Owl is the backup premiere.
- Student showcases (Ridgeview) are fine after the premiere.
- Make a press kit after picture lock: stills from Day 1, a one-page synopsis, director bio.
- Ask [[Prof. Helen Achebe]] for a letter for Night Owl's student category.
