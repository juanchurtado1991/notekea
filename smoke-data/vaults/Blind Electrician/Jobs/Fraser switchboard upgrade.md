# Fraser switchboard upgrade

Replace the old ceramic fuse board at [[Kelly Fraser]]'s place with a new enclosure, main switch, RCBOs on every
final circuit, and a surge protector. Quote Q-2026-034 accepted, deposit paid (see [[Invoices]]).

## First visit — 2026-11-05

Turned up at 7:30. Kelly had already left for her shift so her mum Pauline let me in. The board is in the front
porch, in a timber box that has seen better days. Five rewireable fuses, two of them with the wrong fuse wire wound
on (someone has used heavier wire on the kitchen circuit, which explains why it "never blows"). No RCD protection on
anything. Earth connection at the board looked original; the earth lead runs down the outside wall to a stake near
the garden tap.

Circuits found:
1. Lights front of house
2. Lights back of house plus the laundry
3. Power front rooms
4. Power kitchen and the laundry (this is the overloaded one)
5. Hot water (off-peak, on its own meter)

Tested insulation resistance on each circuit with everything unplugged. All fine except circuit 4, which was low but
not alarming; most likely the old kettle point near the sink, which has a cracked faceplate. Will replace that point
as part of the job and test again.

## Board backing

The backing panel behind the fuses is the old fibrous type. I did not drill or cut anything. Told Pauline we cannot
touch it until it is checked and, if needed, removed by a licensed removalist. Took photos and sent them to Kelly.

Kelly rang back at lunch. She is fine with the extra cost and the delay. I gave her two names; she picked ClearAir
Removals because they can come sooner. They will remove the board backing and leave a clean opening for the new
enclosure. Until then the job is on hold at the board end, but I can still do the cable work in the roof.

## Plan for the new board
- 18-pole enclosure, surface mounted, with room for the solar later if they ever want it
- Main switch 63 A
- RCBO on each final circuit (5 now, 2 spare ways)
- Surge protection device
- New main earth conductor and a second earth stake if the reading is poor
- Label every circuit, and leave the RCD test card on the door

## Roof space
Crawled the roof from the manhole in the hallway. Old rubber-insulated cable on circuit 1, brittle where it crosses
the ceiling joists over the front bedroom. That run gets replaced, not just reconnected. The rest is newer TPS from a
1990s extension. Insulation batts everywhere, so it is slow going. Tayla can help pull cable on the second visit.

## Access and site notes
- Street parking only, carry everything up the steps.
- Pauline is home most days; Kelly is reachable after 3 pm.
- Dog next door barks at anything on the side path, ignore it.
- The meter box at the front is locked by the network, not the owner. Kelly said the key for the outer meter box
  cover is kept by the neighbour at number 9, Barry, who has looked after it for years. Knock first, he is usually
  in the garden.
