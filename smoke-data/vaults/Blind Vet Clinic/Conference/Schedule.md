# Summit schedule

Northeast Companion Animal Summit, Portland Harborview Convention Center. Thursday 2026-12-03 to Saturday
2026-12-05. Badge pickup at the north entrance from 7:00 on Thursday. Bring the printed CE form, the summit only
scans badges and the board wants the signed form.

## Thursday 2026-12-03
- 08:00 — Opening plenary: "Pain is the fifth vital sign", main hall.
- 09:30 — Senior dog arthritis: what actually works. Lecture, main hall. (Max Hollins!)
- 11:00 — Coffee and exhibit hall. Look for a new dental unit compressor supplier.
- 12:00 — Lunch on our own. Maybe the chowder place on the wharf.
