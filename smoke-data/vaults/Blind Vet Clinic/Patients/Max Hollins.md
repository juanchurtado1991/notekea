# Max (Hollins)

Yellow Labrador retriever, neutered male, ten years old. Ruth adopted him from a shelter in Rutland when he was two.
He is the gentlest dog in the practice and the staff love him. Gets anxious on the scale; use the floor mat instead.

## Summary
Senior Lab with hip arthritis, a history of ear infections in his younger years, and a soft lump on the right
chest wall that we have been watching. Bloodwork in October was mostly normal: slightly raised liver enzymes (ALP),
probably from the arthritis medication, to be rechecked in three months.

## Medications
- Joint supplement (glucosamine/chondroitin) daily, given by owner.
- NSAID for arthritis at the lowest dose that keeps him comfortable. Owner gives it with breakfast.
- Fish oil, one capsule daily.
