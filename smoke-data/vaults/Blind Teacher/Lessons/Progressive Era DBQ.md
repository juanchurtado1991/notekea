# Progressive Era DBQ

For [[Period 5 - AP US History]]. Due 2026-11-16, scored with the 7-point AP rubric
(department agreement of 2026-10-07).

**Prompt:** Evaluate the extent to which Progressive reformers changed the role of the federal
government between 1890 and 1920.

## Documents
1. Muckraking magazine excerpt on meatpacking
2. Settlement house annual report
3. Political cartoon on trusts
4. Women's suffrage parade program
5. Federal Reserve Act summary
6. Letter from a union organizer
7. Photograph of a tenement interior
