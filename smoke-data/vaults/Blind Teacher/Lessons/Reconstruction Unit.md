# Reconstruction Unit (1865–1877)

For [[Period 1 - US History]]. Common unit test with every US History section on 2026-11-24
(agreed at the [[Department Meetings]] of 2026-11-04).

## Big questions
- What did freedom mean to formerly enslaved people, and how did they act on it?
- Why did Reconstruction end, and who decided that it would end?
- How did the Reconstruction Amendments change the relationship between citizens and the federal
  government?

## Standards
- Analyze causes and effects of key events (C3 framework, D2.His.14).
- Use evidence from multiple primary sources to develop a claim (D2.His.16).
- Explain how the Constitution was changed by amendment (civics crosswalk).

## Day by day
1. **Day 1 — The war is over, now what?** Warm-up photo analysis of a ruined Southern city.
   Quick write: three things a newly free family would need first. Share out in pairs.
2. **Day 2 — Presidential Reconstruction.** Mini lecture on Lincoln's ten percent plan and
   Johnson's pardons. Students sort cards into "lenient" and "strict" and defend one choice.
3. **Day 3 — The Reconstruction Amendments.** Jigsaw on the 13th, 14th and 15th amendments. Each
   expert group writes a one-sentence summary in student language for the class poster.
4. **Day 4 — Quiz and the Freedmen's Bureau.** Short quiz on the amendments, then a document set on
   the bureau's schools, labor contracts and marriage records.
5. **Day 5 — Black political leadership.** Gallery walk of portraits and short biographies of
   elected officials of the period. Exit ticket: one surprise, one question.
6. **Day 6 — Sharecropping.** Simulation with debt ledgers; students track a family's account over
   three seasons and explain why the debt never goes away.
7. **Day 7 — Violence and resistance.** Testimony excerpts from congressional hearings, read in
   small groups with a guided note sheet. Content warning sent home the day before.
8. **Day 8 — The Compromise of 1877.** Role play of the negotiation; debrief on who was left out of
   the room.
9. **Day 9 — Review.** Station review; the extended-time students start the practice test early.
10. **Day 10 — Unit test.**

## Materials

## Differentiation

- Sentence starters for the claim paragraph.
- Audio versions of the testimony excerpts.
