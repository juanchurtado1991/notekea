# Curriculum Guide — Social Studies 2026–27

My working copy of the district guide, with the parts that matter for my four classes. Linked
from [[Home]].

## 1. Purpose
The social studies program prepares students to read sources critically, argue from evidence and
take part in civic life. Every course is built around inquiry: a compelling question opens each
unit, supporting questions organize the lessons, and a summative task asks students to answer the
compelling question with evidence.

## 2. Course sequence
- Grade 9: World History (two semesters).
- Grade 10: Geography and Economics (one semester each).
- Grade 11: US History, or AP US History for students who qualify.
- Grade 12: Civics (one semester, required for graduation) plus an elective.

Students may take AP US History in grade 12 if they took regular US History in grade 11, but they
may not receive credit for both as their required grade 11 course.

## 3. Pacing — US History (grade 11)
- Quarter 1: Founding era through the Civil War (review pace for content covered in grade 8).
- Quarter 2: Reconstruction, the Gilded Age, industrialization and the labor movement.
- Quarter 3: Progressive Era, World War I, the 1920s, the Great Depression and the New Deal.
- Quarter 4: World War II, the Cold War, the civil rights movement and recent history.

Each US History course must include at least one local history field study. The field study can
be a museum visit, a walking tour or an oral history project, and it must be finished by
2027-03-31.

## 4. Pacing — World History (grade 9)
- Quarter 1: Early civilizations, classical empires, world religions.
- Quarter 2: Age of Revolutions, the Industrial Revolution, imperialism.
- Quarter 3: The world wars and the interwar years.
- Quarter 4: Decolonization, the Cold War world and globalization.

## 5. Assessment policy
- Each course gives at least 6 graded assessments per quarter, at least one of them a writing task
  built on sources.
- Common assessments: departments agree on one shared unit test per quarter per course.
- Retakes: students who score below 70 on a unit test may retake it once; the retake score
  replaces the original up to a maximum of 80.
- Grades are posted in the online gradebook within ten school days of the due date.

## 6. Late work
Late work is accepted until the end of the unit. It loses 5% per school day late, up to a maximum
of 25%. Teachers may waive the penalty for documented absences, IEP or 504 accommodations, or
family emergencies. The penalty is applied when the work is graded, not when it is submitted.

## 7. Accommodations
Teachers follow every IEP and 504 plan. Extended time is given on all timed assessments,
including quizzes, and a separate setting is arranged with the special education team when the
plan requires it. Teachers keep a list of accommodations for substitutes in the sub folder.

## 8. Field trips
- Field trips need the department chair's approval at least four weeks ahead.
- One adult chaperone for every 10 students; at least one chaperone must be a district employee.
- Permission slips and fees are collected by the teacher; fees may never exclude a student, so
  the activity fund pays for any student who asks for a waiver.
- Substitutes for classes the teacher misses are paid by the district, not the department.

## 9. Technology
Students use their district laptops for research and writing. Teachers post every assignment in
the learning platform. Phones stay in pockets unless the lesson plan says otherwise.

## 10. Civics (grade 12)
The semester covers foundations of government, the Constitution, rights and responsibilities,
elections and voting, and policy making. Every unit ends with a civic action project. Students
also complete 12 hours of documented service learning before the end of the semester; the hours
are logged on the district form and checked by the Civics teacher.

## 11. AP courses
AP teachers follow the College Board course framework and the district pacing calendar. Practice
multiple-choice sets are given at least once a month from October. AP students who need a fee
waiver apply through the counseling office by 2026-11-13. The AP United States History exam is on
Friday 2027-05-07 in the morning session; the last two weeks before it are review only.

## 12. Professional expectations
Teachers attend department meetings, share common assessments, and complete the professional
development hours required by the district (see [[PD Hours]]).
