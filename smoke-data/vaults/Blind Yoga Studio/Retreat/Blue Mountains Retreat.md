# Blue Mountains Retreat

Our first studio retreat: a weekend at Wattle Ridge Lodge near Katoomba, Friday 2027-03-12 to Sunday 2027-03-14.
Details of the lodge are in [[Venue]], money in [[Retreat/Budget|the retreat budget]], guests in [[Bookings]].

## Prices
- Twin share: $890 per person
- Single room: $1,090
- Deposit to hold a place: $250 for twin share, $350 for a single
- Balance due 2027-02-12 (four weeks before)
- Capacity: 16 guests

## Teachers
- [[Mia Chen]] — morning flow both days
- [[Priya Nair]] — evening yin and the Saturday night sound bath
- Me — welcome circle, closing circle, the bushwalk

## Rough program
### Friday
- 15:00 minibus leaves the studio
- 18:00 arrive, settle in
- 19:00 dinner
- 20:30 welcome circle and gentle yin

### Saturday
- 07:00 morning flow
- 08:30 breakfast
- 10:00 bushwalk to the lookout (about 2 hours)
- 13:00 lunch, free afternoon
- 16:30 workshop: building a home practice
- 19:00 dinner
- 20:30 sound bath with Priya

### Sunday
- 07:00 morning flow
- 08:30 breakfast
- 10:00 closing circle
- 12:00 lunch, then minibus back to the studio (back around 16:00)
