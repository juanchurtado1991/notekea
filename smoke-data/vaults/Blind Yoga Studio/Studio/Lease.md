# Lease

My own plain-English summary of the retail lease for the studio. The signed PDF is in the filing cabinet (top drawer)
and a scan is on the laptop. I wrote this so I never have to read the 48 pages again.

## Parties
- Tenant: me, trading as Saltwater Yoga.
- Landlord: Calder Street Holdings (a family-owned property company).
- Guarantor: none, the bank guarantee covers it (see Bond below).

## Premises
First-floor unit at 14 Calder Street, Marrickville. Roughly 180 square metres: the main studio room, a smaller room
that I use as the lounge and office, two change rooms with showers, and the storage cupboard under the stairs. I
also get one car space at the back, which I mostly leave free for instructors who teach the early classes.

## Term
- Five years, starting 2025-03-01 and ending 2030-02-28.
- The lease was signed on 2025-02-10 after about two months of back and forth over the fit-out.

## Rent
- Starting rent was $6,400 per month, paid in advance on the first of each month.
- Payment is by direct transfer; the landlord does not take cards.
- If I pay more than 7 days late, interest is charged at the rate set in the lease schedule. I have never been late.
- My own log of every payment is in [[Rent]].

## Rent review
- Fixed review: rent goes up by 3.5% on each anniversary of the start date (every 1 March).
- There is no market review during the first term, which was the main thing I fought for.
- The landlord has to send a written notice of the new figure at least 14 days before each review date, but the
  increase applies even if they forget.

## Outgoings
- I pay my share of council rates and water, worked out on floor area (my share is listed as 22% in the schedule).
- Electricity and internet are in my name and paid directly.
- Building insurance is the landlord's; I need my own public liability and contents cover.

## Bond
- Security is a bank guarantee equal to three months of the starting rent, lodged with the landlord's bank.
- It is returned within 30 days after I hand back the keys, as long as the make-good is done.

## Permitted use
- Yoga, pilates, meditation and wellness classes, plus small workshops and the occasional retail sale of props and
  clothing.
- No amplified music above the level agreed in the schedule after 21:00 (the residents upstairs complained once
  in the previous tenancy).

## Fit-out
- I paid for the sprung floor, the mirrors in the second room and the lounge kitchenette.
- Any new structural work needs written consent from the landlord, which they cannot unreasonably refuse.
- Small things (hooks, shelving, painting the walls a similar colour) are fine without asking.

## Repairs and maintenance
- Landlord: roof, structure, gutters, main plumbing and the lift (when it exists — there is no lift yet).
- Me: everything inside the unit, including lights, the change-room fixtures and the air conditioning service once
  a year. That is why the aircon service and the downlights are on me (see [[Maintenance]]).
- Urgent repairs: if the landlord does not respond within 48 hours I may arrange the repair and claim it back.

## Signage
- Window decals and an A-frame sign on the footpath are allowed.
- No illuminated signs and nothing fixed to the brick facade without consent.

## Opening hours and access
- I have 24-hour access to the premises.
- The main entrance is shared with the physio downstairs; the rear door is mine only.

## Insurance
- Public liability of at least $20 million, with the landlord named as an interested party.
- A copy of the certificate of currency has to be sent every year by 1 March.

## Sublicensing and casual hire
- I cannot assign the lease or sublet the whole premises without consent.
- Casual hire of the studio to other teachers or groups is allowed without the landlord's consent for up to 6 days
  per calendar month, as long as the hirer has their own insurance. More than that needs written consent.

## Option to renew
- One further term of five years.
- I must give written notice to exercise the option no earlier than six months and no later than three months
  before the end of the current term.
- The rent for the option term is set by a market review at the start of that term.

## Make good
- At the end, the sprung floor can stay if the landlord agrees in writing; otherwise it comes out and the
  original floor is sanded and sealed.
- Mirrors and the kitchenette have to be removed and the walls patched.

## Contacts
- Day-to-day questions go to the landlord's agent, Ingrid Vasquez at Parkline Property, not to the landlord.
- Emergency repairs after hours: the agency's after-hours line (in my phone under "Parkline").

## My notes from the signing meeting
- They agreed to paint the stairwell before we opened (done).
- They wanted a personal guarantee; we settled on the bank guarantee instead.
- Next time: ask about putting solar on the roof and sharing the savings.
