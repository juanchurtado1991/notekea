# Arepas

## Ingredients

- 2 cups of pre-cooked corn flour
- 2½ cups of warm water
- 1 teaspoon of salt

## Steps

Mix the flour, the water and the salt, then let it rest five minutes. ^dough

Shape discs and cook them on a hot pan, five minutes a side.
