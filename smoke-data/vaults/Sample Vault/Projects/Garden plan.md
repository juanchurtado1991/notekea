# Garden plan

Three raised beds by the fence. Tomatoes go where the sun stays longest; basil next to them.

- Bed 1: tomatoes (San Marzano, cherry)
- Bed 2: peppers and basil
- Bed 3: herbs and lettuce

Water early in the morning. Harvest from late July.
